# Design Concept — VSYST Tower

The interior design language for VSYST Tower balances **modern minimalism** with **warm, human-centered detailing**. The goal: a workspace that feels calm at scale, energizing at the desk, and memorable at the threshold.

---

## Design Principles
- **Clarity** — Clean lines, generous negative space, intuitive wayfinding.
- **Warmth** — Natural materials (oak, stone, linen) soften the architecture.
- **Daylight first** — Layouts and finishes maximize natural light penetration.
- **Biophilia** — Plants, water features, and organic textures throughout.
- **Flexibility** — Modular furniture and reconfigurable zones.

## Mood
A quiet confidence. Think Scandinavian restraint meets Japanese craft, grounded by the warmth of a hospitality lobby.

## Material Palette
| Category | Primary | Secondary | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floor | Honed travertine | White oak plank | Wool loop carpet |
| Wall | Lime plaster (off-white) | Fluted oak | Bronzed metal |
| Ceiling | Acoustic baffle (white) | Exposed concrete | Linear wood slat |
| Soft goods | Natural linen | Bouclé wool | Cognac leather |

## Color Direction
- **Base:** warm whites, bone, sand
- **Mid-tones:** oak, terracotta, soft sage
- **Accents:** bronze, charcoal, deep forest

## Lighting Intent
Layered — architectural daylight + recessed ambient + sculptural task/accent. Warm color temperature throughout (2700K–3000K).

---

## Floor-by-Floor Character
- **Ground / Lobby** — Grand, hospitality-grade, double-height void.
- **Mid floors (Offices)** — Calm, productive, biophilic.
- **Top floor (Executive + Sky Lounge)** — Refined, panoramic, residential warmth.
