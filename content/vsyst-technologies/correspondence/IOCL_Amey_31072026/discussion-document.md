## 3. Problems on the Ground

### 3.1 Problem of dealers with transporters

1. "Customer takes fuel on credit & never returns. Driver can fuel up & then deny later. Vehicle fuel up and runs away. Driver can send someone else. Fraud sign."
2. "Customer takes advantage of 15 days of credit + 15 days of payment [...] Fund stuck. Double the credit/investment at minimum. [...] Pleading before customer to make payments."
3. "Fund stuck due to None receipt of bill. Accountant non-availability. Bill misplacement. Payment not bill wise. Incorrect payment. Fund stuck due to reconciliation."
4. "Typical ~200 KL/mo highway DODO carries ₹30–60L of month-end receivables. Bad debt 1–3%/yr routinely absorbed into commission margin."
5. "Different types of customer's systems imposed upon dealer. need one platform for all dealer."

### 3.2 Problem of dealer with IOCL

1. "Dealers are forced to make adjustments in transactions [...] limited time to fetch data in ITPS / Card limit issue to receive payment / virtual card OTP receiving issue. owner not available / Late night / holiday issue not being solved. / Field office availability issue"
2. "If someone is dependent on IOCL, in time of real help (Dealer), - do not get support from IOCL - can not upload transactions - can not receive payments of customer - not possible to maintain records of which DU slips is delivered to customer"
3. "high sale RO having most credit sale are not able to upload small amount transaction due to congestion"
4. "Problem with ITPS - Dealer customer relationship hinderance [...] Inaccurate data (unsolved) - Disturb sale, dealer, customer - Irregular payment - Small quantity sale left to upload"
5. "machine availability / software updates through apk / machine maintenance / machine costing / machine network problem"

### 3.3 Problem of transporters with dealers

1. "Customer problems from dealers - Re-checking if any human errors made in billing - Unrecognized / unknown / unreachable dealer - TCS/TDS liability confusion and calculation problem - Reconciliation of account - To keep a track of payments to be made"
2. "Quantity disputes (attendant fills 40L, writes 45L) [...] Slip collusion (driver + attendant inflate litres, split kickback)"
3. "Diesel rates of RO / Needs to request rate dealer wise - date wise - product wise and recheck"
4. "clumsy rate, ordering by chat app, many groups / rate group / order group / payment group / ordering and bill checking is length work for customer / multiple dealer payment with taxes needs reconciliation again and again"
5. "Dealer does not entertain small customers"

### 3.4 Problem of transporter with IOCL

1. "Limitation of FLEET CARD, - Card limit not known to dealer. - FLEET Card lost. SOLD vehicles. - OTP mobile number not reachable. - Daily limit crossed. Random data."
2. "monthly limit set of card and customer. What if monthly off-take of customer increases? Dealer are not able to receive payments due to limit exceeded."
3. "Only ~20% of aware customers actually hold a card → 13% real penetration [...] Friction: banking-style enrolment; auto-block after 2 months of non-use; single-OMC lock; rewards uncompelling for small vehicles"
4. "Xtrapower is only for Diesel. Does not maintain ledger and records for petrol and lubes. XtraRewards points redemption is time taking. And not available in app."
5. "IOCL does not provide facilities like RENT HIRe; transporter specific solutions [...] LRMS can not be facilitated by IOCL."

### 3.5 Problem of IOCL with IOCL

1. "System not eligible to handle real world scenario / Network connectivity issue. Website operation issue. Web app not available. High traffic due to use of customer & dealer for repetitive and continuous surfing. Server issue for ITPS."
2. "Data Not from original source / Lack of real-time data / Current system can not fulfill data requirement / Needs XtraEffort or XtraTime required"
3. "ITPS Limitations / Can not track customer wise credit litre daily sale / Maintainence problem / cash amount is adjusted / Discount adjustment feature not known by every dealer / Training Required"
4. "XtraPower is a loyalty program for customers. But data through ITPS itself isn't loyal."
5. "Work Overloaded for Dealer, Customer, IOC Officials [...] Manual Work creates human error"

### 3.6 Problem of IOCL with dealers

1. "Even being a centralised system, when issues are directed to HO, they are not resolved [...] that leads to manipulation instantly by dealer"
2. "Dealer are reluctant to use Xtrapower."
3. "Credit customers not converted to XP platform in market. - Do IOCL want to get data of all credit customer accounts maintained by Dealer ?"
4. "Manipulation possible b/w driver, manager, parties / 100% conversion not possible due to limitation."
5. "Dealers already gives discount, get those records, help some in need to attract customers"

### 3.7 Problem of IOCL with transporters

1. "Customers are sharing their ID, Password and Cards to dealers."
2. "Amount paid by customer is not necessary to match diesel off take [...] Maharatna company like IOCL is matching diesel off take with customer payments. They should match diesel qty not customer amount."
3. "Not sure of fuel dispatch to same vehicle of same quantity"
4. "by only vehicle number, error can occur, we must verify driver as well"
5. "customer check if he did not get less than ordered / OMC check if customer did not get more than ordered"
