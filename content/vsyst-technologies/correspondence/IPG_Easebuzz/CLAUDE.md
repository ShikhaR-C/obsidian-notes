# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## Project Purpose

This is a document project, not a codebase. The goal is to produce a **3–4 page proposal to a bank (UBI — Union Bank of India) requesting an Internet Payment Gateway (IPG)** for the business, likely routed via Easebuzz. The document must proactively clarify every doubt a bank could have before approving an IPG, and be persuasive enough to secure a positive response.

## Workflow (follow in order)

1. **Gather content & understand the business.** The user will supply raw points about the business in fragments across messages. Ask clarifying questions where a bank would probe: nature of business, legal entity type, years in operation, turnover, what is being sold online, customer base, expected transaction volumes/ticket sizes, existing banking relationship with UBI, compliance/registrations (GST, licenses), website/app readiness, refund & chargeback handling.
2. **Group and frame content topic-wise.** Organize the raw points into themes (e.g., business overview, products/services, financials & volumes, compliance & risk mitigation, technical readiness, banking relationship). Capture this working structure in `proposalplan.md`.
3. **Structure the document.** Agree on a section outline for the final proposal before drafting — keep it tight enough to fit 3–4 pages.
4. **Create the final document last.** Only draft the full proposal once content and structure are settled.

## Key Files

- `proposalplan.md` — working file for collected points, topic-wise grouping, and the agreed outline. Update it as content arrives; it is the staging area before the final document.
- Final proposal document — created in the last step (format to be confirmed with the user, e.g., Markdown → Word/PDF).

## Writing Guidelines for the Proposal

- Audience is a bank credit/operations officer: formal, factual, confident tone; no marketing fluff.
- Every section should preempt a bank objection (business legitimacy, transaction risk, chargebacks/refunds, compliance, settlement account usage).
- Hard limit: 3–4 pages. Prefer concise paragraphs and short tables over long lists.
- Do not invent business facts (turnover, registrations, volumes) — always source them from the user.
