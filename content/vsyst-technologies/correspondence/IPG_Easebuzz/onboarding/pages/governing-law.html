Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

Last updated: 11/02/2021

This clause forms part of our Terms and Conditions and our Cancellation and Refund Policy.

1. Governing law

These terms, and every policy published on the DZZLO platform, shall be governed by the laws of India, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Payments on the platform are processed by an RBI-authorised payment aggregator under the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007.

2. Jurisdiction

Any dispute will be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located at Raipur, Chhattisgarh, India.

3. How a dispute is resolved

  1. Write to us first. Send the complaint to our grievance officer, Shikhar Chawra, at info@vsyst.in, or through Help → Contact Us in the app, quoting the order, invoice or payment reference. We acknowledge within 48 hours and aim to resolve within 7 days.
  2. Disputes between a Dealer and a Customer — about quantity, quality, price, an invoice or a payment — are between those two parties. VSYST provides the order, delivery, invoice and ledger records from the platform to both sides, and does not decide the dispute.
  3. Payment disputes. A failed, duplicate or disputed gateway payment is taken up with the payment aggregator through its grievance process; a refund, where due, is raised by the Dealer as set out in the Cancellation and Refund Policy.
  4. Courts. If a dispute with VSYST is not resolved within 7 days of the complaint, either party may approach the courts named in clause 2.
