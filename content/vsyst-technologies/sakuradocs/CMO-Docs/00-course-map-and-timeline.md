# 00 — Course Map and the 12-Month Marketing Timeline

_Phase 0 · Orientation · Read this before Week 0. After this page you know what to do in Week 1, Week 6, Month 4 and Month 15 — which move, in which order, depending on what, produced by whom, landing in which template, and the test that says you may go on._

This is the **map**; the thirteen lessons are the territory. Every move is here as a dated row, and the lesson in its **Lesson** column answers whatever the row raises.

Unlike [[CEO-Docs/00-course-map-and-timeline|the CEO map]], which is a dependency graph with hard gates, this is a **production line with a fixed cycle time**: Weeks 2–12 are one week repeated eleven times, a channel added each fortnight, eight numbers counted every Monday. So what matters here is the **capacity arithmetic** (§4) and the **entry tests** (§6) — what decides when the line may be widened.

## 1. How to use this page

**Work in timeline order, not table-of-contents order.** [[11-metrics-budget-and-the-marketing-scorecard|Lesson 11]] is read in Week 0 — the scorecard must exist before there is anything to put on it — and [[12-scaling-hiring-and-launching-the-big-campaign|lesson 12]] waits for Week 12's CAC.

**The columns.** _Why it matters_ is the failure the row prevents; _Depends on_ is what must be true first; _Owner_ is one person — **CEO** (technical, ships code), **DD** (fuel-distribution director), **D3** (third director); _Artefact_ is the M-template or workbook tab produced ([[CMO-Docs/toolkit/index|CMO Toolkit]]); _Done when_ is the test. **The order does not change.**

**Five dependency rules govern the order, and every table below obeys them.**

1. **List before message** — you cannot write in a dealer's words before naming the dealers, and [[M03-target-list-builder|M03]] is the denominator every later percentage uses.
2. **Message before visit** — three founders improvising three pitches burn the only list you have.
3. **Visit before campaign** — a campaign is a script repeated at a bigger denominator.
4. **Measured CAC before hiring** — [[M13-marketing-scorecard-and-cac-calculator|M13]] before [[M14-marketing-hiring-scorecard-and-role-charters|M14]]; you cannot hand over a channel you cannot price.
5. **District before multi-district** — a thin network everywhere dies everywhere ([Andrew Chen](https://www.coldstart.com/)).

## 4. Weeks 2–12 — the Raipur 100 campaign

**"Raipur 100" is the named list, not a goal of 100 dealers.** Working it is how you reach the **ten founding dealers**. Brief: [[M12-campaign-brief-budget-and-post-mortem|M12]]; channel spine: [[05-channels-where-to-find-them-and-in-what-order|05]] §8; arithmetic: [[06-founder-led-sales-the-pump-visit-demo-and-close|06]] §7.

**Write the funnel down before Week 2, so Week 6 can name the broken step.** 100 named → 100 contacted → **40 visits** → **25 demos** → **15 trials** → **10 activated dealers** → 30+ customer firms (≥3 each). A warm-worked list beats that 10% rate; a cold-walked one will not. **VERIFY LIVE** after Week 6.

**Then check the plan is physical.** Two field days × four pumps × eleven weeks = **88 visit-slots**; half go to second visits and onboarding, leaving **~45 first visits** — enough for the cold funnel's 39, short for a warm list, which is a hiring trigger ([RepMove](https://repmove.app/sales-efficiency-benchmarks-statistics/), 2025). M13's visit goal is therefore **8 a week** (red line 4) — this arithmetic, not an aspiration; raise it only when a hired associate adds field days ([[12-scaling-hiring-and-launching-the-big-campaign|12]]).

| Week | What happens | Number to hit | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **2** | List final, message locked, brief signed ([[M12-campaign-brief-budget-and-post-mortem\|M12]]); visits and the dealer-#1 referral open | 25 visits · 3 referral names · 1 OMC ask | D3, DD |
| **3–4** | 20 WhatsApp openers + 12 visits; first IOCL and association meetings ([[M10-partner-and-channel-playbook\|M10]]); broadcast opens | 5 warm meetings · broadcast at 50 | DD, D3 |
| **5** | First IOCL and association meetings turn into intros; CA-channel experiment starts, 14 days, kill line 2 | 5 warm meetings · 5 CAs met | DD, D3 |
| **6–7** | 8 visits/week; demos on his own numbers ([[M06-demo-flow-and-pilot-offer-sheet\|M06]]); CA verdict written | verdict written · first 5 trials | DD, D3 |
| **8–9** | **Dealer Day #1 (Week 8)** — chai and a CA on GST/TCS past ₹50L, association co-branded; pitch A vs B on alternate weeks; the **Week 9 review** kills the weakest channel | 25 invited · ~12 attend · 4 demos booked · one channel killed | All three |
| **10–11** | Activation push on every trial ([[M08-onboarding-and-activation-checklist\|M08]]); hardware-engineer experiment; store rewrite; first testimonial | ≥3 customer firms per dealer · listing live · 1 video | CEO, DD |

**Rules for the eleven weeks.** One or two channels enter per fortnight; nothing new starts until the last has a written verdict. Change the opening line, leave-behind and channel mix freely; never the audience, the offer, the meaning of "activated" or the one number — that **ends** the campaign and starts a new one. Test only what you expect to double: peeking at a running test turns a claimed 5% significance into a real 26% ([Evan Miller](https://www.evanmiller.org/how-not-to-run-an-ab-test.html)).

## 6. Months 6–18 — Hire, repeat, go big

**Nothing here is a this-week action**, and pretending otherwise is the most expensive mistake this course can cause. Write the tests down now, while you are still honest — you will not be at dealer #9 with a candidate waiting.

| Move | Owner | Artefact · lesson | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Write the **five entry gates** with your numbers and a dated review | CEO | [[M14-marketing-hiring-scorecard-and-role-charters\|M14]] · [[12-scaling-hiring-and-launching-the-big-campaign\|12]] §1 | Three channels with ≥5 paying dealers each and a defensible CAC; **≥3 closes from the script by a non-founder**; ≥60% 14-day activation; ≥4 referrals/month, ≥25% referral-attributed; ~30–40 paying GSTINs covering the hire's year |
| Build the **bench** — five names from OMC field staff, distributor reps, hardware engineers | DD | M14 §2.1 | Five names, refreshed quarterly. The JD stays **unposted** until the gates hold |
| Hire the **first field associate** (~₹20–35k/month + travel, **VERIFY LIVE**); run him beside DD on one script, then hand over the visit | CEO decides; process is [[T10-hiring-scorecard-and-process\|T10]] | M14 · [[12-scaling-hiring-and-launching-the-big-campaign\|12]] §2 | A paid half-day ride-along before any offer; variable pay on **activated** dealers, never signups; founder → both → watched → alone |
| Open **district 2 / the NH-53 corridor** | DD + associate | M12 per district | ~60% Raipur saturation and 90-day retention ≥85% first; then CAC ≤1.5× Raipur, or the playbook is not portable |
| Run the **three-district launch** — **T-60** districts named, 3 associates hired and ramped with a founder, letters to associations and OMC TMs, a proof pack of 3 named dealers · **T-30** a Dealer Day per district, ≥120 numbers and a page variant each, a 60-day bonus window · **T-0** events on consecutive days, 25 visits/week each, a daily stand-up | CEO + DD, then the lead | M12 · [[12-scaling-hiring-and-launching-the-big-campaign\|12]] §5 | ≥40 attendees and ≥10 demos per district. **T+30 go ≥5 activated, watch 3–4, stop <3**; T+60 go ≥10, CAC ≤1.5× Raipur; T+90 scale two, exit the third. The one number: **15 per district, 45 in 90 days** |

**Budget it honestly:** ~₹5.5–8 lakh over 90 days, ~65–70% of it people. **That does not fit the ₹30–50k/month envelope and is not meant to** — it comes from revenue at ₹30L+ ARR or a seed round; with neither, run **one** district at a third of the cost.

## 7. The standing weekly rhythm

From Week 2 until somebody is hired, this is the week — and it does not change when things go badly.

- **Monday 09:30** — each owner enters their own row in [[M13-marketing-scorecard-and-cac-calculator|M13]]: visits, demos, trials, dealers activated, customers per dealer, referrals asked/received, WhatsApp reply rate, CAC by channel.
- **09:40** — 15-minute huddle, on-track / off-track only; **red twice running escalates.** **10:00** — the ops meeting reads [[T05-kpi-scorecard|T05]], which already holds those rows ([[T03-weekly-ops-meeting-agenda|T03]]). **10:10** — ten minutes on [[M07-sales-pipeline-tracker|M07]]: what moved, what has a blank next step, what dies.
- **Tuesday and Thursday afternoons** — field. Four pumps a day, clustered; rows filled the same evening, in the car.
- **Friday 17:00, 40 minutes** — one sentence you actually heard, into [[M04-positioning-and-message-house|M04]]; then one experiment and its result into `Channel Experiments`. **First Monday monthly** — channel review, kill / keep / scale. **Quarterly** — positioning refresh and goals with [[T19-quarterly-plan-and-review|T19]].

Under six hours a week, and the argument is always about **controllable inputs** — visits, demos, activations ([Commoncog](https://commoncog.com/the-amazon-weekly-business-review/)).

## 8. What jumps the queue

Three things do not wait for your reading pace — which is why the plan is written down: when one interrupts, you know which row you were on.

1. **A reply from the IOCL contact.** A territory manager's word carries authority in a pump office that nothing else buys, and the Amey conversation is open. The reply goes back the same day with **exactly one ask** in it — five dealer names, or fifteen minutes at the next dealer meet ([[M10-partner-and-channel-playbook|M10]]).
2. **An association meeting date.** The chapter's calendar is not ours. If the district body meets on the 14th, everything moves to be ready for the 14th: ten minutes on a borrowed stage beats a fortnight of cold visits.
3. **A dealer who says yes today** — or a live problem at a tenant inside his first 30 days, because at this size support *is* marketing ([[T25-customer-support-sop|T25]]). Activation is a 14-day clock that cannot be paused: run [[M08-onboarding-and-activation-checklist|M08]] from day 0 — three credit customers named on day 1, one installed by day 4, the review at the pump on day 14 — and ask for two referral names there, because the activation moment **is** the referral moment.
