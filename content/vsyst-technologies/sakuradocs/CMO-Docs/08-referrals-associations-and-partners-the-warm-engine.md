# 08 — Referrals, Associations, OMCs and Partners: The Warm Engine

_Phase 3 · Go to Market · Weeks 2–12. After this lesson you can run the "Dealer Dost" referral ask on every activated dealer, capture a proof asset from each, open a dealer-association and an OMC conversation without promising what you cannot deliver, keep it all legal, and track it on five scorecard rows you fill every Friday._

## Explain-it-like-I'm-5

A wedding band in Raipur has never advertised. Every booking arrives from someone who stood in a baraat and asked the dhol player for his number. The band knows this, so at the end of the night — while the family is still happy — the leader walks up to the groom's father and says one sentence: _"Sahab, aapke bhaanje ki shaadi kab hai?"_ Not "please recommend us to anyone." A specific person, named, at the moment the man is pleased.

The band also plays free for twenty minutes at the local vyapari sangh's annual function. What it gets is four hundred shopkeepers watching, and the sangh president saying its name from the stage.

That is this whole lesson. The **ask at the moment of proof** is the referral program; the **borrowed stage** is the association and the oil company. Both are warm — someone the buyer already trusts does the introducing — and at a petrol pump, warm is close to the only thing that works.

## 1. Why warm beats cold, roughly ten to one

A **warm** introduction is one where a person the buyer already trusts vouches for you before you speak. **Cold** is everything else. The gap is not a matter of taste. Nielsen's Trust in Advertising study (40,000+ respondents, five regions) found **88% of people trust recommendations from people they know** — above every other marketing message ([Nielsen, 2021](https://www.nielsen.com/insights/2021/beyond-martech-building-trust-with-consumers-and-engaging-where-sentiment-is-high/)). In B2B data, referrals convert at about **26%** against roughly **9%** for connected cold outreach ([Landbase](https://www.landbase.com/blog/b2b-sales-statistics)). Against the Indian B2B cold baseline of **~2.23%** used in [[12_OWNER_ACQUISITION|Owner Acquisition]] §5 — where referral is ranked at 25–40% — the multiple lands near **ten to one**. That is why HC4 — and [[05-channels-where-to-find-them-and-in-what-order|lesson 05]] — puts referral first and paid ads last.

They are also better customers, not just cheaper ones: in the canonical study of ~10,000 bank customers over three years, referred customers were worth **at least 16% more** and churned **18% slower** ([Schmitt, Skiera & Van den Bulte, _Journal of Marketing_ 75(1), 2011](https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2012/04/Schmitt-Skiera-vandenBulte-2011-Referral-Programs-Customer-Value.pdf)).

**The warm engine is a system, not a mood.** One rule:

> Every dealer you activate produces three things before you move on — **a referral ask, a proof asset, and one association or OMC touch.**

Miss any of the three and the dealer is a customer but not a channel. Lesson [[07-onboarding-and-activation-getting-both-sides-live|07]] gets both sides live; this one turns that dealer into the next three.

## 2. "Dealer Dost" — the referral program

The design, economics and fraud model already exist in [[06_REFERRAL_PROGRAM|the Dealer Dost research doc]] — **do not re-derive them.** What follows is only what you do this week.

### The reward structure — summary only

| Trigger | Referrer gets | New dealer gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Friend signs up, one outlet live | ₹2,000 UPI | First 2 months free |
| Friend starts paying (month 3) | +₹3,000 UPI | — |
| 3 paid referrals | +₹5,000, "Dealer Dost Gold" | — |
| 5 paid referrals | Tablet (~₹15,000) + site feature | — |
| 10 paid referrals | ₹50,000, "State Ambassador" | — |

**VERIFY LIVE — every rupee in that table.** It was costed in April 2026 against an assumed ₹2,500/month ARPA; pricing has since moved to per-GSTIN tiers still under owner sign-off ([[08-dzzlo-subscription-strategy|subscription strategy]]). Re-check the payouts with the CEO against the signed tier sheet before printing a one-pager — and check "two months free" against the 14-day trial in that sheet, because they are different promises.

Three design choices carry the programme: **double-sided**, **fixed rupees rather than percentages**, and **paid on payment, not signup** ([ReferralRock — Dropbox](https://referralrock.com/blog/dropbox-referral-program/)).

### When to ask — the activation moment

Never at the sale. Ask when the dealer has just felt the product work. From lesson [[07-onboarding-and-activation-getting-both-sides-live|07]], two moments qualify: **the first clean month-end** (ledger tallied, invoices matched, nothing chased at 11 PM) and **the first dispute avoided** (a customer challenged a rate or a quantity; the confirmed rate and the driver OTP settled it in ten seconds).

Referral practice says the same — ask "right after the customer recognises the value your business has provided" ([ReferralRock](https://referralrock.com/blog/how-to-ask-for-referrals/)). If a dealer says something good unprompted, that _is_ the moment.

### The ask script

Hindi — say it, do not send it. A voice note beats text, and 77% of Indian SMEs run on WhatsApp Business ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/how-whatsapp-business-is-bringing-indias-smes-to-the-digital-fold/)):

> _"Sir, is mahine aapka month-end saaf nikla — koi ledger ka jhagda nahi. Ek chhoti si baat: aapke aas-paas do pump aise honge jinse aap roz baat karte hain. Unke naam bata dijiye, main khud jaake dikha dunga. Wo chalu ho gaye to ₹2,000 aapko, aur do mahine free unko."_

English, for the second-generation son:

> "Clean month-end this time, no ledger argument. One small thing — there are two pumps nearby you speak to every week. Give me their names and I'll go show them myself. If they go live, ₹2,000 comes to you and two months free to them."

### The "dealer next door" mechanic

**Never ask "do you know anyone?"** A generic ask "requires too much thinking on the potential referrer's part"; a qualified prompt lets someone retrieve a name ([ReferralRock](https://referralrock.com/blog/how-to-ask-for-referrals/)). So the ask always has one shape: **"the two pumps you already talk to."** Dealers in a district talk daily — OMC supply, a Legal Metrology visit, a customer who defaulted on both of them. You are asking him to name people already in his head.

Then take the burden off him. He does not sell; he does one of three things, in rising order of value: forwards our message, sends the name and number, or makes a three-way WhatsApp group and types one line. Ask for the third, accept the first.

Log it the same day in the `Referral Tracker` tab. Un-logged asks do not exist. Columns and the dealer-facing one-pager are in [[M09-referral-program-one-pager|M09]].

### Fraud, abuse and tax — the boring part that saves you

| Risk | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Self-referral (same owner, second firm) | Block on matching PAN or GSTIN across both sides |
| Sign-up-and-churn for the bounty | **Pay the main reward only after month 3, active and paying** — this one rule kills most of it |
| Farming names he has never spoken to | A referral counts only once the referred owner takes a meeting |
| Volume spike, or churn after payout | Above 5 referrals in a month → manual review; clawback window stated in plain Hindi on the one-pager |
| **Tax** | Rewards to a business can attract **TDS at 10% under Section 194R** once benefits to one recipient cross **₹20,000 in a financial year** ([ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/analysis-of-section-194r-of-the-income-tax-act-1961)). A dealer at the 3-referral tier crosses it — **ask our CA before the first payout. VERIFY LIVE** |

## 3. Proof capture — free today, gone in a fortnight

The visit that produces a referral must also produce proof, because a happy dealer stays happy for about two weeks and then it is just Tuesday.

| Asset | Time | How to ask | Used in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **60-second phone video** | 5 min | _"Ek minute ka video — apne pump ke saamne, apni bhasha mein."_ Vertical, at his pump. Three questions: the problem before, what changed, what he'd tell another dealer | [[M11-content-and-whatsapp-calendar|M11]] — WhatsApp status, YouTube, landing page |
| **One-line quote with a rupee number** | 1 min | _"Ek line mein — kitna paisa ya kitna time bacha?"_ Verbatim, with his name, pump, location | [[M06-demo-flow-and-pilot-offer-sheet|M06]] leave-behind; the opening line of the next pump visit ([[06-founder-led-sales-the-pump-visit-demo-and-close|06]]) |
| **Before/after month-end screenshot** | 2 min | Ask for the screen, not the data: _"screenshot le lun? Numbers blur kar denge."_ | Leave-behind, landing page, association deck |

**The rupee number is mandatory** — "it's very good software" persuades nobody; "₹40,000 ka jhagda nahi hua is mahine" persuades the next owner, because fear of loss is this persona's lever ([[12_OWNER_ACQUISITION|Owner Acquisition]] §4). And **get consent in writing** — one WhatsApp line does it: _"Main is video ko DZZLO ke marketing mein use karne ki anumati deta hoon."_ Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, personal data may be processed only for a lawful purpose after consent, and consent may be withdrawn at any time ([PRS India](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/digital-personal-data-protection-bill-2023)). A dealer's face and pump name is personal data; blur every customer name in every screenshot.

## 4. Dealer associations — the borrowed stage

A dealer association is a registered body of pump owners that negotiates with the OMCs and the state, and — the part that matters to you — **has a meeting calendar, a WhatsApp group, and an office-bearer everybody knows.** Their coordinating power is real: 550 pumps in one district have shut together on one call ([The Bridge Chronicle](https://www.thebridgechronicle.com/pune/550-petrol-pumps-pune-district-join-strike-6871)).

| Body | Use to VSYST |
| --- | --- |
| **Chhattisgarh Pradesh evam Jila Petroleum Dealers Association** — state and district body; president reported as Akhil Dhagat ([Haribhoomi](https://www.haribhoomi.com/state-local/chhattishgarh/news/petroleum-crisis-cg-petrol-diesel-shortage-35-more-petrol-pumps-raipur-run-dry--95510)) | **The one that matters. VERIFY LIVE** — registered name, current office-bearers, meeting calendar. Confirm by phone, not web search |
| **AIPDA** — All India Petroleum Dealers Association; its conclave draws the Union Minister, with a technology partner on stage ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2145847); [Motor India](https://www.motorindiaonline.in/nawgati-partners-with-all-india-petroleum-dealers-association-for-aipda-conclave/); [@_AIPDA](https://x.com/_AIPDA)) | National, later. Precedent that a software vendor can sponsor |
| **FAIPT**, **CIPD**, **UPF** — the last two claim ~54,000 dealers between them ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/article/economy-policy/petrol-pumps-will-sell-diesel-to-all-customers-dealers-113013100215_1.html)) | Know the names |
| **FIPI** ([fipi.org.in](https://www.fipi.org.in/)) | **Not a dealer body** — its members are the oil companies themselves |

**The office-bearer meeting.** One person, 30 minutes, at his pump. Bring the domain director, not a deck. Demo on a phone ([[M06-demo-flow-and-pilot-offer-sheet|M06]]), show a dealer's video, ask three things: a **slot at the next chapter meeting** (10 minutes, not 30); an **endorsement letter or newsletter line**, in writing, on letterhead; the **district member list**, or failing that permission to be introduced in the group.

**Sponsoring a meeting, or a "DZZLO Dealer Day."** Two hours, a hotel room, chai, **a practising CA on stage** for forty minutes on GST/TCS and Legal Metrology, then twenty minutes of DZZLO and a demo table. The CA is the point — you give before you ask, and the audience came for him ([[12_OWNER_ACQUISITION|Owner Acquisition]] §4.5). Research bands: **₹25k–₹2L sponsorship, ~₹40k for a district workshop — VERIFY LIVE.** State the tension honestly: against HC7's ₹30–50k/month budget, **a ₹40k Dealer Day is a whole month's spend and a ₹2L state MoU is unaffordable this year.** Do the ₹0 version first — ten minutes at a meeting already happening.

**Never promise**: exclusivity; revenue to any office-bearer personally; "association-approved" wording without a signed letter; free-forever pricing; a feature not shipped. One broken promise reaches every member in a day — the speed that makes the channel valuable makes it unforgiving.

## 5. The OMC channel — what IOCL can and cannot do for you

VSYST has a live conversation with **IOCL** through a contact named Amey (the [discussion document](../../correspondence/IOCL_Amey_31072026/discussion-document.md)). Read it before any OMC meeting. It is not a sales deck — it is a list of pains sorted by who feels them: dealers carrying ₹30–60 lakh in month-end receivables and absorbing 1–3% a year as bad debt; transporters re-checking every bill and losing litres to the slip game at the nozzle; IOCL itself seeing only about **20% of roughly 60% credit sale** flow through XTRAPOWER, with fleet-card penetration under 13% of its own loyal base. XTRAPOWER is accepted at 25,000+ outlets ([IndianOil](https://iocl.com/pages/XTRAPOWERFleetCard)). The card solves payment; it keeps no ledger. That gap is the pitch.

How it becomes warm introductions:

| Touchpoint | What it is | What to ask for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sales / territory officer visit** | The field officer who calls on every retail outlet in a territory | "Which three dealers in Raipur would benefit? Will you tell them we're coming?" |
| **Dealer meet** | Divisional-office gatherings of dealers | A 10-minute slot, or a demo table at the back |
| **New-dealer induction** | A **5-day residential induction for new RO dealers** — 3 days classroom, 2 days field ([IOCL tender](https://skillspedia.in/tender-for-engagement-of-training-agencys-for-training-of-new-retail-outlet-dealers-across-the-country/)) | Nothing yet — a two-year ask, and the best slot in the country |
| **Fleet-card angle** | DZZLO puts verified customers, drivers and vehicles on fleet-card rails and gives IOCL the credit-litre visibility it lacks | A pilot at our own RO, with numbers |

**Be honest about the ceiling.** An OMC officer **can** introduce you, invite you to a meet, let you present, carry a pilot's results upward. An OMC **cannot** quickly mandate software at outlets, endorse in writing outside a procurement process, hand over its dealer database, or move at startup speed. Treat every OMC commitment as a 6–18 month object; never let one block the district plan.

**The line you do not cross.** The April research proposes paying OMC territory managers ₹3,000–5,000 per qualified introduction. **This course's house call is: do not.** IOCL, BPCL and HPCL are public-sector undertakings and their officers are public servants. The Prevention of Corruption (Amendment) Act, 2018 made **giving** a bribe a standalone offence under Section 8, defined "undue advantage" as any gratification other than legal remuneration, and created commercial-organisation liability under Sections 9–10 ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/white-collar-crime-anti-corruption-fraud/726890/the-prevention-of-corruption-amendment-act-2018-key-highlights)). Pay OMC people **nothing**. Offer data and a working pilot instead — which, per the discussion document, is what they actually want.

**BPCL and HPCL** are the same play, later. Run it once with IOCL and repeat — not three OMC conversations in one quarter.

## 6. The CA and the hardware engineer

**Chartered accountants.** One CA in Raipur may serve **10–30 pumps** and is already the person the owner asks "should I buy this?" ([[12_OWNER_ACQUISITION|Owner Acquisition]] §5). General SaaS practice for a referral partner who hands over the lead and steps back — and the band the research suggests — is **10–15% of first-year ARR, one-time** ([Channels as a Strategy](https://channels-as-a-strategy.com/saas-reseller-commission-structure/); [SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/what-are-typical-commission-ranges-for-referral-partners-for-enterprise-b2b-saas/)). **VERIFY LIVE — and check the structure with our own CA first.** The ICAI Code of Ethics restricts a member from accepting a share of fees from a non-member, so the clean design is a written introducer agreement with the CA's **firm**, disclosed to the dealer — or no fee at all and a value exchange instead (a read-only reconciliation view that speeds his GST work).

**Hardware service engineers.** Dispenser OEM engineers — Gilbarco Veeder-Root and others — are on the forecourt quarterly for calibration and spares ([Gilbarco India](https://www.gilbarco.in/)). A word at the end of a service call carries weight. Band: **₹1,000–₹2,500 per booked demo — VERIFY LIVE.** They are private-sector employees, so the right form is a written agreement with the employer — never cash to an individual behind his employer's back.

Outreach templates for all four partner types are in [[M10-partner-and-channel-playbook|M10]]; partnership strategy in depth is CEO territory ([[14-partnerships-ecosystem-and-founder-relationships|CEO 14]]).

## 7. The warm-engine scorecard and the weekly habit

Five rows, added to the marketing scorecard in [[M13-marketing-scorecard-and-cac-calculator|M13]] and [[11-metrics-budget-and-the-marketing-scorecard|lesson 11]], read weekly (HC8; the company scorecard is the COO's [[T05-kpi-scorecard|T05]], which these feed).

| # | Row | Owner | Green | Red |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Referral asks made this week | Domain director | = dealers activated | 0 |
| 2 | Referral names received | Domain director | ≥ 2 per ask | 0 |
| 3 | Referrals converted to paying | CEO | ≥ 1 per month | 0 for 2 months |
| 4 | Proof assets captured (video / quote / screenshot) | Third director | 1 per activated dealer | 0 |
| 5 | Association / OMC / CA intros made | CEO | ≥ 1 per week | 0 for 2 weeks |

Row 1 is the only one you fully control; it must never be zero.

**The habit: Friday, 30 minutes, all three founders.** Open the `Referral Tracker` tab. For every dealer activated this week — ask made, logged, proof asset in the folder? For every open referral — what stage, who owns the next step, by when? Then one calendar action: book the next association or OMC meeting. A referral programme not reviewed weekly is a poster, not an engine.

## 8. At VSYST — applying this now

Three people, no marketing hire (HC1), ₹30–50k/month (HC7), Raipur only (HC3). This week:

- **Domain director** — makes the referral ask, in person or by voice note, to **every dealer already activated**, using the §2 script; logs each in `Referral Tracker` the same day; fills [[M09-referral-program-one-pager|M09]] and gets the ₹ amounts signed off before anything is printed.
- **Third director** — captures **one 60-second video testimonial** at a dealer's pump with a written consent line, plus the rupee-number quote and a blurred month-end screenshot, filed where [[M11-content-and-whatsapp-calendar|M11]] expects them.
- **CEO** — books **one meeting**: the association office-bearer or the IOCL contact; records ask and outcome in [[M10-partner-and-channel-playbook|M10]]. Settles two items with our CA: the 194R position on payouts, and whether a CA introducer fee is structurable at all.
- **All three, Friday** — the 30-minute warm-engine review; five rows into the scorecard.

What we do **not** do this quarter: pay any OMC officer anything; sponsor a ₹2L state MoU; promise exclusivity; or market to transporters in a district where no dealer is live (HC2).

## 9. Exercises

**9.1 — Ask every activated dealer, and log it (45 min).** List every dealer activated to date and, for each, the activation moment you will reference. Then make the ask — call or visit, never a forwarded template — using the two-pumps-you-already-talk-to framing. **Artefact:** every activated dealer has a row in the `Referral Tracker` tab with referrer, date asked, names received, and a dated next step.

**9.2 — Capture one video testimonial (30 min).** Pick the happiest dealer, go to his pump. Vertical phone video under 60 seconds, in Hindi or Chhattisgarhi, three questions: the problem before, what changed, what he would tell another dealer. Take the rupee-number line separately, and the consent line on WhatsApp. **Artefact:** one video file, one quote with a name and a number, one consent message — saved and linked from [[M11-content-and-whatsapp-calendar|M11]].

**9.3 — Book one warm-channel meeting (30 min).** Choose the Chhattisgarh association office-bearer or the IOCL contact. Call, do not email. Beforehand, write the three things you will ask for (§4 or §5) and the one thing you will offer. Afterwards, fill the partner row in [[M10-partner-and-channel-playbook|M10]] with date, ask, answer, next action. **Artefact:** a dated meeting in the calendar and a completed partner row.
