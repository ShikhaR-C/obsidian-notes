# 09 — Content, WhatsApp and the Minimum Digital Presence

_Phase 4 · Scale · Months 3–12. After this lesson you can build the seven digital assets DZZLO needs and skip the twenty it doesn't, choose between the free WhatsApp Business app and the paid Platform, run a one-piece-a-week content calendar three people can sustain, write a broadcast that gets replies instead of blocks, pick the two lead magnets to build first, and name the four numbers that say it is working._

## Explain-it-like-I'm-5

A wedding band in Raipur gets almost all his work by word of mouth — one shaadi leads to the next. But just before every booking the family asks, _"koi video hai?"_, and he plays ninety seconds of last November's baraat off his phone. Loud, shaky, real, with the client's own house in the background.

That video did not get him the enquiry; the uncle who recommended him did. The video **closed** it. The signboard, the number someone picks up, the album at the shop — none of them find customers. They stop the customer who is already half-sold from worrying.

That is this whole lesson. **DZZLO's digital presence exists to make the warm, in-person motion credible and repeatable — not to replace it.** The pump visit ([[06-founder-led-sales-the-pump-visit-demo-and-close|lesson 06]]) and the referral ([[08-referrals-associations-and-partners-the-warm-engine|lesson 08]]) are still the business.

## 1. The minimum digital presence

HC4 and [[12_OWNER_ACQUISITION|Owner Acquisition]] §5 rank content and SEO **seventh**, ahead of only paid ads. What changes from Month 3 is that the warm motion starts producing questions you cannot answer in person — _"aapki company ka website hai?"_, _"Google pe kuch nahi aata"_, _"aur koi dealer use karta hai kya?"_ So the test for every asset below is: **does a dealer who already met us, or was referred to us, close faster because this exists?** If it only helps strangers find us, it is a "not yet" (section 8).

## 2. The one-page landing site

One page, not a website — the CEO's evening in Framer or Webflow. Tickable version in [[M11-content-and-whatsapp-calendar|M11]].

**Must contain, in order:**

1. A **Hindi/English toggle**, Hindi first — the buyer is 45–60, the son reads both (HC5).
2. A **rupee-number headline, not a category headline.** Not "Order Management System for Fuel Distribution" but closer to _"रेट का झगड़ा, बिना बिल का उधार, टैंक की कमी — हर महीने ₹—— जा रहे हैं"_, the number from [[M04-positioning-and-message-house|M04]].
3. The **three proofs from [[08-referrals-associations-and-partners-the-warm-engine|lesson 08]]** — named dealer, pump, district, photo, one number each. Named beats anonymised.
4. A **WhatsApp click-to-chat button as the primary CTA**, sticky on mobile: a `wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX` link with a pre-filled first line — not a contact form.
5. **Two Hindi app screenshots** plus one DIP screen.
6. **"Billing on the web, per GSTIN"** and the trial — companies pay per GSTIN, users/staff/drivers unlimited and free, bought on the website and never in the app: ₹599 / ₹1,799 / ₹4,999 + Enterprise, ex-GST, 14-day no-card trial (**VERIFY LIVE — owner sign-off pending**, [[08-dzzlo-subscription-strategy|subscription strategy]]).
7. A **real footer** — company name, Raipur address, CIN, GSTIN, phone, email, privacy, terms. Needed anyway for the Play data-safety form and Easebuzz onboarding.

**Must NOT contain:** a form asking more than name, phone and pump name; a blog with zero posts; a chatbot; stock photos of American gas stations; features without numbers; any claim you cannot show in the ten-minute demo; English only; **a store badge next to a price.**

## 3. WhatsApp: the app, the Platform, and the bill

WhatsApp is not "a channel" in India, it is the medium — 84% of Indian SMBs say it helps them communicate and 80% say it drives growth ([Indian Retailer](https://www.indianretailer.com/article/technology/digital-trends/indian-smbs-tap-into-whatsapp-s-powerful-features-to-win-customers-and-fast-track-sales.a8013)). The only decision is which WhatsApp.

| | **WhatsApp Business app** (free, ₹0) | **WhatsApp Business Platform / API** (paid) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you get | Profile, catalog, quick replies, labels, greeting/away messages, broadcasts, up to 10 linked devices and agents ([WhatsApp Business app features](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/business-app-features/)) | Pre-approved templates, webhooks, automation, quality-rated tiers, CRM integration |
| Reaching someone who never messaged you | Broadcast list ≤256, and **only people who saved your number** receive it | A template message, with opt-in, inside a limit that starts at **250** recipients/24h and rises 2,000 → 10,000 → 100,000 → unlimited as quality holds ([Meta — messaging limits](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messaging-limits)) |
| **Fit at VSYST today** | **Yes** — the whole answer for the first 100 dealers | **Not yet.** Trigger: the same transactional message going to 200+ dealers, *or* a WhatsApp send becoming a product feature — product roadmap, not marketing budget |

**Meta's pricing model, because you will be quoted nonsense about it.** Since **1 July 2025** Meta charges **per delivered template message**, not per 24-hour conversation; rates vary by category and country code; **service messages are free** (since November 2024) and **utility templates delivered inside an open customer-service window are free** ([Meta — Platform pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing/)). India rates quoted by BSPs in mid-2026 sit near **₹0.86 per marketing template and ₹0.115 for utility/authentication, plus 18% GST and the BSP fee** — **VERIFY LIVE** against the INR rate-card file on that page and your BSP's sheet.

**Broadcast list vs group.** A **broadcast list** takes ≤256 contacts, lands as separate 1:1 chats, and **only reaches contacts who saved your number** — which is why _"sir, mera number save kar lijiye"_ is a line in the pump-visit script ([[M05-pitch-scripts-and-objection-handling|M05]]). A **group** (~1,024 members) must be announcement-only or it is a forward dump in three weeks.

**Opt-in and the spam rules.** Meta requires opt-in before any business-initiated message; it must say the person agrees to receive messages **and name the business**, and need not be collected inside WhatsApp — a line on the pump-visit form, a tick on the landing page or "reply START" all qualify ([Meta — get opt-in](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/getting-opt-in)). On the Platform, blocks and reports drive a quality rating that throttles your tier. On the free app there is no dashboard and no warning — **the penalty is a straight ban of the number your whole pipeline sits in.**

## 4. Store listings, Google Business Profile and YouTube — the hygiene pass

**Play / App Store.** In one sitting: a **≤80-character short description** localised to Hindi as well as English, and **four or more screenshots at 1080px+ in 9:16** showing the Hindi UI, the first two carrying the promise rather than the login screen ([Play Console — graphic assets and localisation](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151)). Play bans unattributed testimonials, "#1"/"best" claims, promotional pricing language, competitor comparisons and keyword stuffing ([Play Console — store listing and promotion](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898842)). **Reviews:** ask activated dealers at the day-30 check-in ([[M08-onboarding-and-activation-checklist|M08]]); never pay, gift or discount for one, since Play prohibits incentivised ratings while allowing a clear, non-deceptive ask ([Play Console — ratings, reviews and installs](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898684)).

**The web-billing wording.** Play allows an app to be consumption-only even when part of a paid service ([Play Console — Payments policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10281818)); Apple's 3.1.3(f) permits a free stand-alone companion to a paid web service **provided there is no purchasing inside the app and no calls to action to purchase outside it** ([App Store Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)). The marketer's rule is one line: **no price, no "subscribe", no "upgrade", no link to the pricing page — anywhere in the app or either listing.** All of that lives on the website ([[08-dzzlo-subscription-strategy|subscription strategy]]).

**Google Business Profile and YouTube.** VSYST's Raipur office qualifies for a Profile — eligibility turns on a physical location customers can visit, or a business that travels to them ([Google — eligibility](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177)). Verification (postcard, phone, email or video call) can take 14 days, so **start it this week** ([Google — verify](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7107242)); then use the free weekly Update post for that week's video ([Google — add posts](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169)). On YouTube, publish three Hindi cuts of the [[M06-demo-flow-and-pilot-offer-sheet|M06]] demo — the 10 PM–6 AM rate window; order → driver OTP → GST/TCS invoice; DIP, tank dip to shortage number — each ≤2 minutes, so each is also a Short.

## 5. Content means proof and education, in the dealer's language

Forget "content marketing". For DZZLO, content is two things: **proof that someone like him already did this**, and **education useful even if he never buys.** Ranked by return per founder-hour:

| # | Piece | What it proves | Shot by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Dealer testimonial**, 60–90s, his pump, his words | "Dealer next door" (HC5) — the highest-value asset you own | Domain director |
| 2 | **"Month-end in 10 minutes"** screen recording | The product does in minutes what costs him three days | Third director |
| 3 | **Rate-dispute story**, 60s | Removes the fear that sells (HC5) | Domain director |
| 4 | **DIP shortage story** — the tank that didn't add up | Reaches the operator and accountant, not just the owner | Domain director |
| 5 | **GST/TCS explainer with a CA**, ≤2 min | Borrowed authority; doubles as the CA-partner warm-up ([[M10-partner-and-channel-playbook|M10]]) | Third director |

Format rules, non-negotiable: **≤2 minutes, phone-shot, vertical 9:16, Hindi audio, Hindi + English burned-in subtitles** (WhatsApp and Shorts autoplay muted).

**The two lead magnets to build first.** [[04_LEAD_MAGNETS|Lead Magnets]] lists ten. Build **#2, the Daily Shortage / Evaporation Audit Template**, and **#3, the GST Invoice Template for Petrol/Diesel Dealers** ([ClearTax — GST on petrol](https://cleartax.in/s/gst-on-petrol) covers the VAT/GST split it handles). Why these two: one founder-day each in Google Sheets with **zero engineering time**; both sit on pain the product already solves (the DIP module; the three-tier invoice with TCS past ₹50L), so using the sheet creates the ache the software cures. Skip the DIP-chart and ROI calculators — they cost the CEO's product hours and assume SEO traffic we do not have — and the compliance quiz, which only works with paid social.

**A lead magnet is not a form.** Do not gate it — hand it over inside the chat: _"Sir, ye shortage audit sheet bhej raha hoon, free hai; apne pump ka data daal ke dekhiye."_ Three days later, _"Sheet chal gayi? Kitna shortage aaya?"_ — the discovery question from [[06-founder-led-sales-the-pump-visit-demo-and-close|lesson 06]].

## 6. The WhatsApp playbook

- **Cadence: one broadcast per week, per list, maximum.** Never Sunday. Send 10:30–12:30 or 16:00–18:00 — owners are at the pump for the morning and evening rush.
- **The message pattern: proof → one line → one tap.** A 60–90s video or a photo of a real dealer; one sentence naming the loss in rupees; one action. Five lines and one link, maximum; templates in [[M11-content-and-whatsapp-calendar|M11]]. Never "Dear Sir/Madam", a PDF as the opening move, or a "forwarded many times" label.
- **The "DZZLO Dealer Community" group:** announcement-only, four rules pinned — no rate or politics forwards, no selling to each other, questions answered within four working hours, leave any time. **Add only activated dealers.** It is a retention and referral asset ([[06_REFERRAL_PROGRAM|Dealer Dost]]), not a lead list.
- **Reply SLA: two working hours between 09:00 and 20:00, one working day always.** Away message outside — the marketing mirror of the support SLA in [[10-customer-operations-support-and-success|COO lesson 10]].
- **What gets you blocked:** messaging people who never met you; more than one send a week; a forward you didn't make; no obvious way to stop.

For the **customer side** — transporters, factories, fleets — the corridor variant exists already: the 21-day WhatsApp sequence, Hindi long-tail and shorts cadence in [[08_Marketing_Strategy|transporters 08]] §2–3. Use it **only under HC2**: never broadcast to transporters in a district where no dealer is live. Until several dealers in one district are running, that message is delivered *by the dealer, with our material* ([[11_EDUCATION_GAP|Education Gap]]).

## 7. The content calendar

One piece per week. Forty-odd pieces a year.

- **Shoots:** domain director, on visits he is already making — phone, clip-on mic, thirty seconds of setup. **Edits:** third director, CapCut plus Canva, both free, **forty minutes per piece**.
- **Posting order:** WhatsApp Status (everyone who saved your number, ₹0, zero policy risk) → the weekly broadcast → YouTube Shorts → a Google Business Profile update → LinkedIn **only** for a second-generation owner or an OMC/partner contact (HC4).
- **Reuse:** one shoot = four assets — the 90-second video, a still frame with a pull-quote for Status, a three-line text version for the broadcast, a paragraph for the landing page's proof block. Re-run a strong piece in a **different district** after three weeks; that is distribution, not repetition.
- **Kill rule:** a format that draws zero replies twice is dead. Delete the row from [[M11-content-and-whatsapp-calendar|M11]]; don't debate it.
