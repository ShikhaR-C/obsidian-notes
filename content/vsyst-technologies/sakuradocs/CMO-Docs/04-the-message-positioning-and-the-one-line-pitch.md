# 04 — The Message: Positioning, Segment-Specific Pitches and Objections

_Phase 2 · Aim · Week 1. After this lesson you can state DZZLO's positioning in one paragraph, compute a rupee number for a specific pump with its owner watching, fill a segment × message × channel matrix answering "which business from where, and what do we say", deliver a 30-second and a 2-minute pitch in Hindi and English to both dealer and customer, and handle the eight objections you will hear this week._

## Explain-it-like-I'm-5

A mobile-recharge shop stands at the corner of every Raipur market. It sells recharges, SIMs and covers — and the thing that pays the rent, Aadhaar-linked cash withdrawal. What does the owner paint on his board?

Paint **"Mobile Shop"** and he is on the same shelf as the glass-front showroom two streets away; everyone compares, and he loses. Paint **"यहाँ आधार से पैसा निकालें — 24 घंटे"** and he is on a shelf where the showroom is not standing at all, and the auntie who needs ₹2,000 today walks straight in. Same shop, different shelf.

**That board is positioning.** What he shouts at the man walking past is the message. When the man says "मेरा तो खाता SBI में है", the answer he has ready is objection handling. This lesson is DZZLO's board, shout and answer.

## 1. Positioning: which shelf are we on?

**Positioning** is choosing what your product gets compared to, so the buyer instantly sees why you are better. April Dunford's model has five inputs, filled in order: **competitive alternatives** (what he'd do if you didn't exist), **unique attributes**, **value and proof**, **target-market characteristics**, and last, **market category** ([Heinz Marketing on *Obviously Awesome*](https://www.heinzmarketing.com/blog/five-components-of-effective-positioning-an-obviously-awesome-book-summary-part-2/); [April Dunford](https://www.aprildunford.com/books)). Her finishing test: **a competitor must not be able to say your line** ([Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/a-guide-to-advanced-b2b-positioning)).

The canvas is [[C08-positioning-and-messaging-canvas|C08]]; the depth work is [[07_Positioning_and_Messaging|transporters 07]]. **Do not redo either** — this lesson turns their output into sentences you say at a pump.

**The category call:** DZZLO is **"the order-and-ledger app between a fuel dealer and his credit customers"** — not "petrol pump billing software." That difference is the whole business. (Fleet cards — IOCL XTRAPOWER, BPCL SmartFleet — are payment-only and single-OMC: not our fight.)

| If we say…                     | Compared to                                | What happens                                     |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| "Petrol pump billing software" | Local DSR vendors, Petrosoft, Excel        | A feature-and-price fight we lose               |
| "Accounting software"          | Tally ([Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/tally-solutions/__ummn8YHX1Tr6e7nCjArAhhn9ZrAH94OOacbtx3TsQDk)) | Unwinnable — and it insults the munim          |
| **"Order-and-ledger app"**     | **The parchi book and WhatsApp**           | **Nobody owns this shelf; paper is beatable**   |

**The positioning statement** (blank template in [[M04-positioning-and-message-house|M04]]):

> **For** petrol-pump dealers, bulk-diesel operators and lubricant distributors around Raipur selling much of their volume on credit to 20–200 B2B customers, **who are dissatisfied with** a paper DSR + Tally + WhatsApp + Excel stack where rate, order, delivery and ledger sit in four places and nothing reconciles until month-end, **DZZLO is** the order-and-ledger app between the dealer and his credit customers, **that provides** one rate agreed *before* the sale (the 10 PM–6 AM window, auto-locking at 6 AM), one order-to-invoice trail with driver OTP, and one ledger both sides read, **unlike** pump billing software (dealer-only, so the dispute survives), Tally (books after the fact, not the transaction) or the parchi book — **because** DZZLO is the only one **the dealer's customer also opens.**

That last clause is what a competitor cannot copy — and what [[02-the-two-sided-problem-sell-to-the-dealer-seed-the-customer|lesson 02]] protects. Product facts: [[00_OVERVIEW|product overview]].

**One tension, flagged.** [[07_Positioning_and_Messaging|Transporters 07]] claims the category "digital credit book for India's fuel economy", transporter-first; this course puts the **dealer** first, because the dealer pays (HC2). Same product, different buyer named first. Keep **"डीज़ल लो, हिसाब भूलो"** as the customer-facing compression. **VERIFY LIVE:** the domain director signs off which line goes on the landing page — you only get one.

## 2. The rupee number (HC5)

Fuel dealers are not upside buyers, they are **loss buyers**. Prospect theory's finding — a loss hurts about twice as much as an equal gain feels good — is the most reliable thing behavioural economics offers a salesman ([Simply Psychology](https://www.simplypsychology.org/prospect-theory.html); [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/prospect-theory/)). Add Cialdini's **social proof** and **liking**: the dealer next door, by name ([Cialdini](https://www.cognitigence.com/blog/cialdini-7-principles-of-persuasion)). The persona work is done in [[12_OWNER_ACQUISITION|Owner Acquisition]] §4 — read it once, don't re-derive it.

Ask him five questions in this order — "रेट पर कितनी बार बहस?", "पर्ची खोई, बिल बना ही नहीं?", "डिप और मीटर में कितना फ़र्क?", "कितने credit note, कोई notice?", "पैसा कितने दिन में आता है?" — and fill this in front of him:

| Leak                | Formula                                       | Illustrative input               | ₹/month     |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ----------- |
| Rate disputes       | disputed litres × paise conceded              | 12,000 L × ₹0.35                 | **₹4,200**  |
| Unbilled credit     | credit litres × leak % × rate                 | 1,00,000 L × 0.2% × ₹101         | **₹20,200** |
| Shortage / variance | throughput × variance % × rate × recoverable  | 2,50,000 L × 0.15% × ₹101 × 50%  | **₹18,900** |
| GST / TDS errors    | credit notes + amortised penalty              | ₹5,000 + ₹25,000/yr ÷ 12         | **₹7,000**  |
| Receivables ageing  | (credit sales ÷ 30) × days saved × carry cost | (₹1 Cr ÷ 30) × 10 days at 15% pa | **₹41,000** |
| **Total**           |                                               |                                  | **≈ ₹91,000/mo ≈ ₹11 L/yr** |

**Every figure is illustrative — VERIFY LIVE on the pump's own numbers, with the owner watching you write.** The inputs: diesel dealer commission is about **₹2.5–2.9/litre** (petrol ₹3.7–4.0), OMC-fixed, last revised October 2024 ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/pump-commission-rules-explained-how-much-petrol-pump-owners-earn-per-litre-in-india-what-costs-do-they-have-to-manage-and-more-520996-2026-03-18); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/petrol-pump-dealer-commission-raised-retail-prices-remain-unchanged-124103000842_1.html)); Raipur diesel is near **₹101/litre** ([Shriram Finance](https://www.shriramfinance.in/diesel-price-in-raipur)); permissible dispenser variation is **±25 ml per 5 litres** ([Delhi Weights & Measures](https://weightnmeasures.delhi.gov.in/weightnmeasures/instructions-checking-and-testing-dispensers-petrol-pumps)); Indian SMEs wait a median **73 days** to be paid, on terms of 30 days or less for 82% of invoices ([Recordent, 2026](https://mediabrief.com/recordent-report-highlights-working-capital-stress-for-indian-smes-as-average-overdue-receivables-cross-%E2%82%B93-83-crore/)).

The line that lands is the ratio, not the total: **35 paise conceded on a disputed rate is one-eighth of the entire diesel margin on those litres.**

**Two honesty rules.** DZZLO recovers perhaps 30–50% of that in 90 days, not all — variance is his discipline, not our software, and over-claiming is how the second visit gets cancelled. And on tax: **TCS under 206C(1H) was omitted from 1 April 2025**, buyer-side TDS under 194Q now carrying the ₹50L threshold ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/tcs-sale-goods-removed-april-1-2025-faqs.html); [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/section-206c-of-income-tax-act)), while DZZLO's ₹50L auto-trigger still exists in the product. **VERIFY LIVE with the CA before any pitch says "TCS"** — and petrol and diesel sit outside GST, lubricants do not ([ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/gst-on-petrol)).

## 4. The message house

One promise on the roof, three pillars, proof under each — the construction that stops a small team telling four stories in a week ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/resources/frameworks/marketing-frameworks/message-house-framework/); [Bare Strategy](https://barestrategy.com/blog/messaging-house-framework)).

**Roof:** **रेट पक्का, पर्ची पक्की, हिसाब पक्का.** _(Rate pakka, parchi pakki, hisaab pakka)_ — "Rate settled, slip settled, ledger settled."

| Pillar             | What it means                                                              | Proof (never claim without it)                                     |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **1. रेट पक्का**    | Rate set at night, confirmed before 6 AM, unconfirmed auto-locks | Confirmed-rate screen, customer name and timestamp |
| **2. पर्ची पक्की**  | Order → vehicle + driver → OTP at delivery → invoice at source | The driver-OTP screen; an invoice made in the demo |
| **3. हिसाब पक्का**  | One shared ledger, ageing, vouchers, a GST-ready trail for the CA | A statement PDF the customer opens himself |

Every asset carries the roof plus **exactly one** pillar. Mixing them dilutes all three.

**30 seconds — dealer (Devanagari):**
> "साहब, उधार वाले ग्राहक कितने हैं — तीस? चालीस? और महीने में एक-दो बार रेट पर बहस होती ही है, ठीक? DZZLO में आप रात को कल का रेट डाल देते हैं, ग्राहक छह बजे से पहले फ़ोन पर कन्फर्म कर देता है — फिर बहस का सवाल ही नहीं। गाड़ी निकले तो ड्राइवर का OTP, बिल वहीं। ग्राहक को भी वही खाता दिखता है जो आपको। [दीपक भाई] की पंप पर चल रहा है — पूछ लीजिए। दस मिनट दीजिए, आपके ही आँकड़ों पर दिखा दूँ।"

**English:** "Sir — how many credit customers do you carry? Thirty? Forty? And once a month there's an argument about the rate, correct? On DZZLO you set tomorrow's rate at night; the customer confirms it before 6 AM, and the argument stops existing. The driver carries an OTP; the invoice is made at delivery. Your customer sees the ledger you see. [Deepak bhai]'s pump runs on it — ask him. Ten minutes, on your own numbers."

**30 seconds — customer (delivered by the dealer, or with him present):**
> "भाईसाहब, हमारा पंप अब DZZLO पर है। कल का रेट रात को आपके फ़ोन पर आएगा — कन्फर्म कर दीजिए, सुबह वही लगेगा। हर डिलीवरी OTP से, पूरा खाता आपके फ़ोन पर, महीने के अंत में मिलान नहीं। आपके लिए मुफ़्त है।"
> _English:_ "Your supplier is on DZZLO now. Tomorrow's rate reaches your phone tonight — confirm it, and that's the rate you pay. Every delivery is OTP-verified, your ledger is on your phone, and it's free."

**2 minutes — dealer, six beats, always this order** (full text in all three renderings: [[M05-pitch-scripts-and-objection-handling|M05]]):

1. **His leak, his numbers.** "चलिए आपके ही आँकड़े लगाते हैं." _(Chaliye aapke hi aankde lagaate hain — "let's put your own numbers in".)_
2. **Three pillars, one proof each.** "रेट पक्का, पर्ची पक्की, हिसाब पक्का." _(Rate pakka, parchi pakki, hisaab pakka — "rate settled, slip settled, ledger settled".)_
3. **The dealer next door.** "अभी फ़ोन लगा दूँ?" _(Abhi phone laga doon? — "shall I call him right now?")_
4. **Cost in time.** "मुंशीजी दो घंटे देंगे, बाक़ी हम करेंगे." _(Munshiji do ghante denge, baaki hum karenge — "your munim gives two hours; we do the rest".)_
5. **Price and trial.** "चौदह दिन मुफ़्त, कार्ड नहीं चाहिए." _(Chaudah din muft, card nahi chahiye — "14 days free, no card".)_ **₹599 / ₹1,799 / ₹4,999 per GSTIN ex-GST, users free, web-billed** ([[08-dzzlo-subscription-strategy|subscription strategy]] — **VERIFY LIVE**).
6. **One dated ask.** "शुक्रवार को आपके तीन ग्राहक जोड़ दूँ?" _(Shukravaar ko aapke teen grahak jod doon? — "can I set up your first three this Friday?")_

## 6. Objections — the eight you will hear this week

Use **LAER** — Listen, Acknowledge without defending, Explore the real driver, Respond ([Ringy](https://www.ringy.com/articles/objection-handling); [HubSpot](https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/handling-common-sales-objections)). The trap column matters more than the reply.

| Objection                       | Reply                                                                                          | Proof                                     | Trap to avoid                                                        |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "We have Tally" | "Tally रहेगा — वो आपकी किताब है; हमारा काम बिक्री के वक़्त का है." | Export → Tally; invoice at dispatch | Never position against Tally — you lose and insult his CA |
| "My munim does this" | "मुंशीजी का काम कम होगा, हटेगा नहीं. उन्हीं को पहले सिखाएँगे." | Show *him* the statement he didn't build | Never imply the munim is the problem — he kills deals silently |
| "My customers won't use an app" | "शुरू में सिर्फ़ दो ग्राहक. बाक़ी के ऑर्डर आप डालिए." | Dealer-side value alone; ledger PDF on WhatsApp | Don't promise a zero-install WhatsApp flow — **not built** ([[11_EDUCATION_GAP|education gap]] §7–8) |
| "Internet here is bad" | "ऑर्डर मोबाइल डेटा पर चलता है; रेट रात को कन्फर्म होता है." | Demo it on his forecourt, your phone | Don't claim offline mode unless engineering confirms — **VERIFY LIVE** |
| "What about my data?" | "आपका डेटा आपका है. Export कभी भी; बंद करें तो साथ ले जाइए." | Tenant isolation; a one-page policy | No "cloud", no "encrypted at rest" — no English security words |
| "Too expensive" | "महीने का ₹1,799 — और अभी आपके आँकड़ों पर ₹91,000 का रिसाव निकला." | His leak table, in his handwriting | Never discount at the first meeting — re-open the leak table |
| "OMC already gives us software" | "वो OMC का सिस्टम है — उसमें आपके ग्राहक नहीं आते." | The customer-side app on a customer's phone | Never criticise IOCL/BPCL/HPCL — live talks ([[discussion-document|IOCL doc]]) |
| "We'll think about it" | "ठीक है — शुक्रवार को आपके दो ग्राहक सेट कर दूँ?" | A calendar date, not a brochure | Never leave without a dated next step — "thinking" with no date is a no |

## 7. What NOT to say

1. **No feature lists.** "Three-tier invoicing with TCS automation and DIP reconciliation" is a spec line, not a forecourt line.
2. **Banned: "AI", "cloud", "platform", "seamless", "empower", "disrupt", "enterprise-grade"** — full list in [[07_Positioning_and_Messaging|transporters 07]] §8.
3. **No English jargon.** मिलान not "reconciliation", खाता not "ledger", पर्ची not "slip". आप/साहब, never तू.
4. **Never insult the munim, the CA or Tally.** Making his people and tools look foolish makes *him* look foolish for choosing them.
5. **No US-casual tone, no ALL CAPS, no emoji spray** — read as disrespect ([[12_OWNER_ACQUISITION|Owner Acquisition]] §8).
6. **No unverified proof.** "50+ pumps use DZZLO" when six do cannot be undone in a district where every dealer knows the others.
