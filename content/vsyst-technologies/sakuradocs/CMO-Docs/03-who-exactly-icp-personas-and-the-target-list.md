# 03 — Who Exactly: ICP, Personas and the Named Target List

_Phase 2 · Aim · Week 1. After this lesson you can say in one sentence who DZZLO is for, score every fuel-trade segment in Raipur district on fit, describe the four people inside a dealer family and the transporter owner on the customer side well enough to talk to each of them, and hand your co-founders a spreadsheet of named pumps, ranked, with a route to an introduction for each._

## Explain-it-like-I'm-5

A woman starts a tiffin service in Raipur. She could say "I cook for anyone who eats lunch" — true, and useless. Instead she walks two kilometres around her kitchen, writes down the forty offices she can reach hot by 1 PM, and ticks the eleven where she knows somebody: her cousin's CA firm, two showrooms her husband supplies, the clinic her daughter goes to. She starts with those eleven. Nine say yes.

Three things on that walk. She defined **who** ("offices within two kilometres, twenty-plus staff, no canteen"). She worked out **who inside each office decides** (the office manager, not the boss). And she made a **named list with a way in for each name**. That is this entire lesson. Her advantage is not the food — it is that she never wastes a morning on an office thirty kilometres away with its own canteen.

## 1. The ICP, in one line

An **ICP** — Ideal Customer Profile — describes the customer who, if you had a hundred of them, would give you a company you actually want. Not everyone who *could* use DZZLO. The theory, scoring rubric and switch-interview script are [[C09-icp-and-customer-discovery-guide|C09]]; market map and sizing are [[C06-market-map-sizing-and-segments|C06]]. Do not rebuild either. This is the field layer: the card you carry to the pump ([[M02-icp-and-persona-field-cards|M02]]) and the list you work ([[M03-target-list-builder|M03]]).

> **A DODO petrol-pump dealer in Raipur district, within ~60 km of the city, who sells 5–40 B2B customers on credit off a paper slip book, with a second-generation family member already involved in running the pump.**

DODO — Dealer-Owned, Dealer-Operated — is the vast majority of Indian retail outlets and the only model where the person who feels the pain signs the cheque ([[12_OWNER_ACQUISITION|Owner Acquisition §2.1]]). Every clause does work; §5 says what happens when one is missing.

Two notes. The customer side is **not a second ICP with a second sales motion**: per HC2 a transporter is scored on how good a *seeded customer of a live dealer* they are, not on whether we could sell cold ([[02-the-two-sided-problem-sell-to-the-dealer-seed-the-customer|lesson 02]]). And every count below carries a **VERIFY LIVE**; exercise 8.3 is how they stop being estimates.

Read the fit column as an instruction. **Two segments earn Week-1 attention: the second-gen-run pump and the owner-run pump with a real credit book — highway ones first.** Everything else waits.

## 3. The two persona cards, in prose

**The dealer family.** A 58-year-old owner of an IOCL pump on the Raipur–Durg road. Asset-rich, cash-tight: land and forecourt worth crores, fuel netting well under 1% ([[12_OWNER_ACQUISITION|Owner Acquisition §2.1]]). His day runs 6 AM to the day-end summary near 11 PM, and three anxieties recur — **stock reconciliation** (density and meter drift can eat a day's margin), **compliance** (GST on lubes and commission, Legal Metrology sealing a dispenser, an OMC show-cause), and **the credit book**, where he can name every customer who ever defaulted (§2.3). He does not buy software. He buys protection, and only because someone he trusts told him to.

Three others stand around him. The **son**, 28–42, engineering or MBA, lives on WhatsApp and is the de-facto CTO of the pump — he installs the app and opens the dashboard. The **munim**, the part-time accountant who keeps the ledger and feeds the CA, is the quiet veto: make his month-end harder and he kills it in one sentence. The **pump manager** runs shifts and fears being measured. The choreography §2.2 names: **convince the son with numbers and a live demo, reassure the father with proof and a face, show the munim a shorter month-end, give the manager one less phone call.** The son influences; the father signs.

**The transporter owner.** Eighteen trucks out of Raipur Transport Nagar on the Nagpur and Kolkata legs. Diesel is 40–60% of a truck's operating cost — ahead of EMI, driver wages and tyres. He buys on credit at three or four home pumps; drivers sign paper slips; his munshi spends the first ten days of each month matching slips to the trip register. His job-to-be-done, as [[04_Target_Customer_Transporters|the transporter research]] frames it: *when I settle month-end with three to five pumps, I want to know exactly how many litres each truck took, at what rate, from which pump, with GST-compliant proof, so I can pay without a fight, catch driver fraud early, and see which routes bleed.* Functionally he wants accuracy; emotionally he wants out from under somebody else's slip book; socially he wants to be seen as a professional. Six to a hundred trucks is the strong band; one to four self-manage on paper.

Both cards, in fill-in form, are [[M02-icp-and-persona-field-cards|M02]].

## 4. Building the named "Raipur 100" this week

### 4.1 Where the names come from

| Source | What you get | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **IndianOil locator** | Outlet name, address, phone, timings, state → city | [locator.iocl.com/location/chhattisgarh/raipur](https://locator.iocl.com/location/chhattisgarh/raipur) |
| **BPCL locator** | Station search by state/district | [bharatpetroleum.in/fuel-station-locator](https://www.bharatpetroleum.in/fuel-station-locator) |
| **HPCL outlet list** | Dealer details by state/UT | [rooutletlist](https://www.hindustanpetroleum.com/rooutletlist), [petrolpump.hpretail.in](https://petrolpump.hpretail.in/) |
| **PPAC statewise data** | The denominator, so coverage % is knowable | [PPAC retail outlets](https://ppac.gov.in/infrastructure/retail-outlets) |
| **Google Maps** | Gaps the locators miss; reviews show night traffic | "petrol pump" + tehsil |
| **The domain director's phone** | The only source with a relationship attached | His contacts, WhatsApp groups |
| **Dealer association roll** | Names, office-bearers, meeting calendar; state bodies publish committees ([VPDA](https://www.vpda.co.in/)) | Ask the Chhattisgarh chapter secretary; national body [AIPDA](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2145847) |
| **CAs / tax consultants** | One CA handles 10–30 pumps; the intro carries authority | Raipur CA branch; our CA |
| **Hardware service engineers** | OEM engineers visit every pump quarterly and know who is automated ([Gilbarco](https://www.gilbarco.in/)) | Local service franchisee |
| **OMC territory manager** | Territory dealer lists; strongest authority signal | The live IOCL thread — [[discussion-document|IOCL discussion document]] |

**The locators give you rows; the last five sources give you a way in.** A row with no intro path is a cold call, and cold converts at a fraction of warm ([[12_OWNER_ACQUISITION|Owner Acquisition §5]]).

### 4.2 How to score and rank

Four factors, each 1–5, weighted, out of 50. Full rubric and sheet: [[M03-target-list-builder|M03]].

| Factor | Weight | 5 looks like | 1 looks like |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B2B credit volume | ×3 | 20+ credit customers, ₹50L+/month udhaari | Cash and UPI only |
| Second-generation presence | ×2 | Son/daughter running operations | Owner 65+, nobody younger |
| Distance from Raipur | ×2 | In the city or ≤20 km | 60 km+ |
| Intro path strength | ×3 | The domain director knows him | No path found yet |

**A ≥ 38 · B 26–37 · C ≤ 25.** Work the A list in geographic clusters so one morning covers four pumps, and re-score after every visit — pump #3 changes pump #11.

### 4.3 Finding the customer side through the dealer

Never build a transporter list before a dealer is live in that district (HC2). At the end of a good pump visit ask one question: **"Aapke top paanch udhaari waale customers kaun hain?"** Write those five names into `Target List` against that pump. It beats any directory — his introduction is the invite, his rate is the reason to open the app, his ledger is what the customer wants to see. Both sides live: [[07-onboarding-and-activation-getting-both-sides-live|lesson 07]].

## 5. Disqualifiers — say no in the first ten minutes

Knowing who *not* to sell to buys back more founder hours than any technique here. The general list is [[C09-icp-and-customer-discovery-guide|C09]] Template (b); this is the fuel-trade version.

| Disqualifier | Why | Instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cash/UPI only, or fewer than ~5 credit customers** | Rate window, shared ledger and vouchers have nobody on the other side, and there is too little pain to change habit | Mark C; revisit when DIP/GST proof is heavy |
| **Owner absent — manager-run or NRI** | No signer, no champion, nobody carrying risk | Drop. No second visit |
| **Chain- or corporate-managed site** | The decision is made outside the district | Log the operator's name; drop the site |
| **>60 km from Raipur in Week 1** | Support and second visits are the constraint | Park in the corridor list ([[05_Beachhead_and_Expansion\|Beachhead and Expansion]]) |
| **Active OMC dispute or suspension** | Attention is elsewhere; the risk is ours | Park with a date |
| **Already on a paid OMS, live contract** | Switching cost beats us today | Log competitor and renewal month — intelligence, not a lost lead |

## 7. At VSYST — applying this now

Three people, one week, no budget beyond phone calls and diesel for the car.

- **Domain director — owns the list.** Two hours with his phone and the three locators: the first 30 rows of `Target List`, with a named intro path for at least 15. He is the only one here who can turn a row into a warm call.
- **CEO — owns the ICP.** Write the one-line ICP and the must-have / nice-to-have / disqualify lists into [[M02-icp-and-persona-field-cards|M02]], then argue the §2 fit scores with the domain director for 30 minutes. Disagreement now is cheap; in Month 4 it is not.
- **Third director — owns the sources.** Retire the three **VERIFY LIVE** counts: how many outlets are actually in Raipur district (count the locators, do not estimate), how many sit within 60 km, how many the domain director knows by name.
- **Together, Friday, 45 minutes.** Pick the two beachhead segments, agree the A/B/C bands, confirm next week's top 10 visits ([[06-founder-led-sales-the-pump-visit-demo-and-close|lesson 06]]).

The honest state of it: **nobody publishes how many Raipur pumps have a real credit book.** It is discovered one visit at a time and written into the list. Treat the first 20 visits as research — counting matters more than closing until the numbers stop surprising you ([[11-metrics-budget-and-the-marketing-scorecard|lesson 11]]).

## 8. Exercises

**8.1 — Score the segments, pick two beachheads (30 min).** Each director scores every §2 row independently, 1–5 on fit today, then compare. Where scores differ by 2 or more, argue to agreement or write the disagreement down. Pick exactly **two** beachhead segments, one-line reason each.
*Artefact:* the "Beachhead segments" line and the agreed fit column in [[M02-icp-and-persona-field-cards|M02]].

**8.2 — Fill both persona cards from real people (45 min).** Not from this lesson — from memory. The domain director names one dealer family he knows (father, son, munim, manager) and one transporter owner, and fills both cards: what each cares about, fears, must hear. Boxes you cannot fill get "don't know" — those are your interview questions ([[C09-icp-and-customer-discovery-guide|C09]] has the script).
*Artefact:* both cards in [[M02-icp-and-persona-field-cards|M02]], with "don't know" boxes marked.

**8.3 — Build the first 30 rows of the Raipur 100 (45 min).** Three OMC locators and the domain director's contacts, side by side. Fill 30 rows of the `Target List` tab of `vsyst-cmo-workbook.xlsx` per [[M03-target-list-builder|M03]]'s columns. Rank by score; highlight the top 10.
*Artefact:* 30 scored rows, top 10 highlighted, ready for [[06-founder-led-sales-the-pump-visit-demo-and-close|lesson 06]].
