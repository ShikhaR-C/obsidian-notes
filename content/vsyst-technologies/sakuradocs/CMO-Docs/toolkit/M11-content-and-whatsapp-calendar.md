# M11 — Content and WhatsApp Calendar + Landing-Page Checklist

_Toolkit · fills the exercises in [[09-content-whatsapp-and-the-minimum-digital-presence|09 — Content, WhatsApp and the Minimum Digital Presence]] · Owner: CEO (site, tracking), domain director (WhatsApp, shoots), third director (edits, publishing) · Cadence: weekly — row filled Friday, broadcast Tuesday · Workbook tab: `Content Calendar` in [vsyst-cmo-workbook.xlsx](vsyst-cmo-workbook.xlsx)._

## Purpose

Four checklists and one table that turn [[09-content-whatsapp-and-the-minimum-digital-presence|lesson 09]] into a runnable week: the one-page site, the Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Business and its broadcast rules, the one-piece-a-week calendar. All of it exists to close the pump visit ([[06-founder-led-sales-the-pump-visit-demo-and-close|06]]) and the referral ([[08-referrals-associations-and-partners-the-warm-engine|08]]) faster. It never outranks HC4.

## When to use

- **A–C once**, the week you reach lesson 09 — and start Google verification that same day: postcards take up to 14 days, review up to 5 business days ([Google — verify your business](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7107242)).
- **D every Friday**, forever. One row, one piece. **F monthly**, into [[M13-marketing-scorecard-and-cac-calculator|M13]] and the post-mortem ([[M12-campaign-brief-budget-and-post-mortem|M12]]).

## How to fill (rules)

1. **Ship the site at 80%** — every row in A ticked or deferred with a date. **Hindi is the default, English is the toggle**: the signer is 45–60 (HC5), and every rupee figure comes from [[M04-positioning-and-message-house|M04]] with its calculation.
2. **UTMs on every outbound link** — always `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` ([Google Analytics — custom campaign URLs](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10917952)). House form: `?utm_source=whatsapp&utm_medium=broadcast&utm_campaign=raipur100-w07`.
3. **The CTA is a chat, not a form:** `https://wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX?text=<url-encoded first line>` — international format, no zeroes, brackets or dashes ([WhatsApp — click to chat](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934)).
4. **Opt-in before any broadcast.** The line must say the person agrees to receive messages and must name the business; a tick on the pump-visit form or the site both count ([Meta — get opt-in](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/getting-opt-in)).
5. **One piece a week, forty minutes of editing.** Four hours means the format is wrong; zero replies twice means the row is deleted, not debated. Count replies by hand — the free app has no dashboard.

## Template

### A. Landing-page checklist — `dzzlo.in`, in this order

- [ ] **1 · Language toggle** — Hindi first, English second, top-right
- [ ] **2 · Headline — the rupee number, not the category:** _"रेट का झगड़ा, बिना बिल का उधार, टैंक की कमी — हर महीने ₹—— जा रहे हैं"_
- [ ] **3 · Sub-line** — who it is for: pump dealers, bulk-diesel and lubricant distributors, Raipur ([[M02-icp-and-persona-field-cards|M02]])
- [ ] **4 · Three proof slots** — one dealer each: name, pump, district, photo, **one number**; named beats anonymised. Slot 3 may be the IOCL or association relationship, stated exactly as it is ([[M10-partner-and-channel-playbook|M10]])
- [ ] **5 · Primary CTA — click-to-chat, sticky on mobile**, pre-filled: _"DZZLO के बारे में जानना है — मेरा पंप ——"_
- [ ] **6 · Screenshots** — two Hindi app screens + one DIP screen ([[M06-demo-flow-and-pilot-offer-sheet|M06]])
- [ ] **7 · Pricing — "billing on the web, per GSTIN"**; users, staff, drivers unlimited and free; ₹599 / ₹1,799 / ₹4,999 + Enterprise, ex-GST, **14-day no-card trial** (**VERIFY LIVE — sign-off pending**, [[08-dzzlo-subscription-strategy|subscription strategy]])
- [ ] **8 · Footer** — company name, Raipur address, CIN, GSTIN, phone, `hello@`, privacy, terms

**Must NOT be on it:** a form asking more than name, phone and pump · an empty blog · a chatbot · stock photos of American gas stations · features without numbers · any claim the demo cannot show · English only · **a store badge next to a price**.

**Tracking, before you announce the URL:** GA4 in; a **click event on the `wa.me` button**; UTMs everywhere; a Friday hand-count of **chats opened** — GA4 sees the click, only the inbox sees the conversation.

### B. Google Business Profile — VSYST Technologies, Raipur

- [ ] Claimed; **verification submitted today** (Google picks the method, you don't)
- [ ] Category; Raipur address; ~60 km service area; real hours; phone = **the WhatsApp Business number**; website `dzzlo.in` with UTMs
- [ ] **10 photos** (office, team, three app screens, a pump visit — with consent); Hindi + English description, **no price words**; three real questions seeded in Q&A
- [ ] Weekly Update post reserved for that week's video; reviews asked at the day-30 check-in ([[M08-onboarding-and-activation-checklist|M08]]) — **never paid for or gifted**

### C. WhatsApp Business setup, then the broadcast rules

Free app: profile, catalog, quick replies, labels, broadcasts, up to 10 linked devices ([WhatsApp Business app features](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/business-app-features/)).

- [ ] Profile: DZZLO — VSYST Technologies, category, Raipur address, `dzzlo.in`, hours, `hello@`
- [ ] **Catalog, 5 items, Hindi, no prices:** rate window · order → driver OTP · GST/TCS invoice · credit ledger · DIP reconciliation
- [ ] **Quick replies** `/demo` `/sheet` `/price` `/trial` `/help`; greeting and away messages, away outside 09:00–20:00
- [ ] **Labels mirroring [[M07-sales-pipeline-tracker|M07]] stages** exactly — one vocabulary, not two
- [ ] **One broadcast list, `Pipeline — Raipur`:** ≤256 dealers who opted in **and saved your number**, or they receive nothing
- [ ] **Opt-in line** in the pump-visit form and the script ([[M05-pitch-scripts-and-objection-handling|M05]]): _"मैं DZZLO (VSYST Technologies) से WhatsApp पर मैसेज पाने के लिए सहमत हूँ।"_

| Rule | |
| --- | --- |
| Cadence | **One per list per week, maximum.** Never Sunday; 10:30–12:30 or 16:00–18:00, outside the pump's rush |
| Shape | **Proof → one line → one tap.** ≤5 lines, one link, one action |
| Reply SLA | **2 working hours, 09:00–20:00; one working day always.** A broadcast you don't staff is worse than none |
| Gets you blocked | People who never met you · more than one send a week · a forward you didn't make · no way to stop. **One ban costs the number your pipeline lives in** |
| API / Platform | Only when the list outgrows the app — the same message to 200+ dealers, or a second 256-cap list. Meta charges **per delivered template** since 1 July 2025; service messages and in-window utility templates are free ([Meta — Platform pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing/)). India ≈ **₹0.86 marketing / ₹0.115 utility, +18% GST + BSP fee — VERIFY LIVE** |

### D. `Content Calendar` tab — columns

`Week` · `Piece` · `Type` (testimonial video / screen-recording / story / explainer with CA) · `Persona` · `Shoot owner` · `Edit owner` · `Channels` (Status → broadcast → Shorts → GBP → LinkedIn) · `Posted ✓` · `Replies / Views @48h` · `Reuse` (still, pull-quote, three-line text, landing-page paragraph) · `Verdict` (keep / kill)

### E. Message patterns

**1 — Testimonial broadcast**, video first, then:
> _"शर्मा जी का पंप, धमतरी रोड। month-end 3 दिन से 10 मिनट। ₹—— का बिना बिल का उधार पकड़ में आया। 10 मिनट का डेमो? हाँ लिखिए।"_
> _"Sharma ji ka pump, Dhamtari road. Month-end 3 din se 10 minute. ₹—— ka bina bill ka udhaar pakda gaya. 10 minute ka demo? Haan likhiye."_
> _"Sharma ji's pump, Dhamtari road. Month-end: 3 days to 10 minutes. ₹—— of unbilled credit found. A 10-minute demo? Reply YES."_

**2 — Lead magnet, handed over, never gated** — with the follow-up three days later:
> _"सर, ये शॉर्टेज ऑडिट शीट फ्री है — अपने पंप का डेटा डालकर देखिए।"_ → _"शीट चल गई? कितना शॉर्टेज आया?"_
> _"Sir, ye shortage audit sheet free hai — apne pump ka data daal ke dekhiye."_ → _"Sheet chal gayi? Kitna shortage aaya?"_
> _"This shortage sheet is free — put your pump's data in."_ → _"Did it work? How much shortage came out?"_

**3 — Save-my-number ask**, said at the pump, never sent:
> _"सर, मेरा नंबर सेव कर लीजिए — हफ़्ते में एक बार कुछ काम का भेजूँगा, बस।"_ / _"Sir, mera number save kar lijiye — hafte mein ek baar kuch kaam ka bhejunga, bas."_ / _"Save my number, sir — one useful thing a week, that's all."_

## Worked example — VSYST (illustrative)

Month 3; `Pipeline — Raipur` holds 34 opted-in dealers. Every number below is illustrative — VSYST has no baseline yet.

| Wk | Piece | Type | Persona | Shoot | Edit | Channels | ✓ | Replies / Views @48h |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| W1 | "3 din se 10 minute" — Sharma ji | Testimonial, 84s | Owner, 52 | Domain dir | Third dir | Status, broadcast, Shorts, GBP | ✓ | **7 / 34 (21%)**, 260 views |
| W2 | "Month-end in 10 minutes" | Screen recording, 110s | Accountant | Third dir | Third dir | Status, broadcast, Shorts | ✓ | 3 / 34 (9%), 190 views |
| W3 | "जो रेट तय हुआ था" | Story, 62s | Owner + son | Domain dir | Third dir | Status, broadcast, Shorts, GBP | ✓ | **9 / 36 (25%)**, 410 views |
| W4 | "TCS ₹50L ke baad", with CA Bhatia | Explainer with CA, 118s | Son / accountant | Third dir | Third dir | Status, broadcast, Shorts, LinkedIn | ✓ | 4 / 36 (11%), 150 views |

Friday of W4: the **story-shaped** pieces out-pull the explanatory ones two to one, so W5–W8 are all dealer stories from other tehsils and the CA explainer survives as an [[M10-partner-and-channel-playbook|M10]] warm-up, not a broadcast. Reply rate **16.4%** clears the ≥15% goal and goes to [[M13-marketing-scorecard-and-cac-calculator|M13]]; click-to-chat 4.6% falls just short. Founder time: five hours.

## Common mistakes

- **Building the site before three real proofs exist.** Then it is a brochure, and converts like one.
- **A contact form where the WhatsApp button belongs.** Nobody in this market fills a form.
- **A price, "subscribe" or a pricing link inside the app or either store listing.** That lives on the website only.
- **Broadcasting to people who never saved your number** — they receive nothing, and you conclude the channel is dead.
- **Counting views instead of replies**, or buying the API to fix what is really a 34-name list and no proof.
