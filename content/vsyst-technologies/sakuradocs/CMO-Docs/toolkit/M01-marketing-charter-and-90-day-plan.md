# M01 — Marketing Charter, Hire-or-DIY Decision and 90-Day Plan

_Toolkit · fills the exercises in [[01-what-marketing-and-sales-actually-are|01 — What Marketing and Sales Actually Are]] · Owner: CEO; one named founder per plan row · Cadence: written in Week 0, read every Monday, rewritten at Week 12 · Workbook tab: `Marketing Scorecard` in [vsyst-cmo-workbook.xlsx](vsyst-cmo-workbook.xlsx)._

## Purpose

One page that stops three arguments recurring every month: **who owns marketing, whether we hire for it, and what we do each week for twelve weeks.** The charter fixes the owner and the number; the decision record fixes the hire question with dated triggers, so it re-opens on evidence, not fatigue; the plan turns [[12_OWNER_ACQUISITION|Owner Acquisition]] §6 into named weeks.

## When to use

- **Write it in Week 0**, before lesson [[03-who-exactly-icp-personas-and-the-target-list|03]] — one hour, the three exercises in lesson 01.
- **Read §3 every Monday**, inside the twenty minutes you fill [[M13-marketing-scorecard-and-cac-calculator|M13]].
- **Rewrite it at Week 12**, or when all three triggers go green.

## How to fill (rules)

1. **One owner per row, by name, never "the founders".** Shared ownership is no ownership.
2. **The goal is one sentence with one number**, countable in five minutes on a Monday. "Brand awareness in Chhattisgarh" is not.
3. **Hours are booked before they are promised** — recurring calendar blocks in the same sitting, or they are a wish.
4. **All three hiring triggers must hold**, not a majority. One firing alone is noise (HC1, [[01-what-marketing-and-sales-actually-are|lesson 01]] §4).
5. **Every cost or salary figure carries `**VERIFY LIVE**`** and who verified it.
6. **Weekly targets are inputs you control** — visits, demos, activations; outcomes get a cumulative line only. The plan may be wrong, never vague: change a row in the Monday read and note why.

### 1. The charter

| Field                               | Fill                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |

### 2. The hire-or-DIY decision record

| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| **Dated · agreed by** | Date; all three directors                                          |

| **Trigger 1**         | ≥ __ paying dealers · today __                                     |

### 3. The 90-day plan

| Week     | Lessons | Templates            | Field work                                    | Weekly target                                 |
| -------- | ------- | -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **0**    | 01, 02  | **M01**              | Write the charter, block the hours          | 3 directors agreed; 12 weeks booked           |
| **1**    | 03, 04  | M02, M03, M04, M05   | Build the named list; write message + scripts | 100 named pumps; pitch rehearsed aloud      |
| **2**    | 05, 06  | M06, M07             | First pump visits; demo on your own phone   | 8 visits · 2 demos · 1 trial                  |
| **3**    | 06      | M07                  | Refine the script after every no            | 10 visits · 3 demos · 1 trial · 1 paying      |
| **4**    | 07      | M08                  | Onboard trials; link each dealer's top three | 10 visits · 3 demos · 2 dealer activations   |
| **5**    | 07      | M08                  | Customer side live in the 10 PM–6 AM window | 10 visits · 1 customer live per dealer        |
| **6**    | 08      | M09                  | Referral asks — activated dealers only      | 10 visits · 3 referrals asked                 |
| **7**    | 08      | M10                  | OMC territory manager, association          | 10 visits · 2 partner conversations           |
| **8**    | —       | M13; review M01 §3   | Mid-point: CAC by channel, kill the worst   | 10 visits · one channel cut, one doubled      |
| **9–11** | 09      | M11                  | Repeat the best channel; Hindi demo live    | 10 visits · 3 demos · 2 activations weekly    |
| **12**   | 10, 11  | M12, M13; rewrite M01| Post-mortem; re-run the hire decision       | Cumulative: 100 worked, ≥10 paying, ≥8 both sides |

**"Raipur 100" means 100 named pumps *worked*, not 100 paying dealers in 90 days.** [[12_OWNER_ACQUISITION|Owner Acquisition]] §6 sets 100 paying on day 90, but assumes an association MoU, a printed lead magnet and a field crew. Three part-time founders at ten visits a week reach roughly ten paying dealers — exactly the number that unlocks the first hire. Ten is the win.

## Worked example — VSYST (illustrative)

**Charter.** The **CEO** owns marketing: he holds the number and fills the scorecard every Monday at 09:40. The **domain-expert director** owns the conversation — every pump visit, the IOCL and association relationships, every referral ask. The **third director** owns the list and follow-up. Goal: *"By 30 November 2026, 10 dealers in Raipur district are paying and live on both sides."* Number: **activated dealers with ≥1 activated customer**. Hours 5 / 12 / 6, blocks booked 27 August. Budget ₹35,000/month: fuel, chai, printing, one Hindi demo video. Out of scope: logo, paid ads, LinkedIn, cold email, second district.

**Decision.** *DIY — the three founders. 27 Aug 2026, all three directors.* Why: searching for a business model is a founder's job ([Steve Blank](https://steveblank.com/2012/03/05/search-versus-execute/)); "the most common unscalable thing founders have to do at the start is to recruit users manually" ([Paul Graham](https://paulgraham.com/ds.html)); a rep hired before the script exists churns in four months ([SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/what-are-the-best-ways-to-transition-from-the-founder-led-sales-stage/)). Triggers: ≥10 paying dealers (today 0) · one channel producing ≥3 repeatably (none) · selling >15 h/week for 3 straight weeks (~8 h). If all three fire: open **field sales associate, Raipur, ₹20–35k/month + travel** — **VERIFY LIVE**; local postings run ₹8k–₹75k, a Paytm field-executive listing sits at ₹18–25k ([apna, Aug 2026](https://apna.co/jobs/field_sales-jobs-in-raipur)). Review date **26 November 2026**, calendared regardless.

## Common mistakes

- **A goal with no number, or one nobody can pull.** If Monday's fill runs over five minutes, pick a metric you already store ([Sean Ellis — North Star metric](https://medium.com/growthhackers/what-is-a-north-star-metric-b31a8512923f)).
- **Hours promised but not booked.** Calendar, or it did not happen.
- **Re-opening the hire question mid-quarter because one week went badly.** That is what the review date is for.
- **Treating the plan as a forecast.** Visits and demos are yours to hit; paying dealers are not.
- **Copying the old ₹999/₹1,999 numbers into the goal.** Pricing is per-GSTIN and web-only, tiers under sign-off — [[08-dzzlo-subscription-strategy|subscription strategy]], **VERIFY LIVE**.
