# M14 — Marketing Hiring: Role Charters, Comp Bands and Scorecards

_Toolkit · fills the exercises in [[12-scaling-hiring-and-launching-the-big-campaign|12 — Scaling: Hiring, Agencies, Brand and the Big Campaign]] · Owner: CEO (the decision) with the COO's process [[T10-hiring-scorecard-and-process|T10]] · Cadence: only when the HC1 triggers hold · Workbook tab: —_

## Purpose

One page: **whether to hire at all**, **three role charters**, **the scorecard that decides who**, **the ramp**, **the incentives**. Process is the COO's ([[T10-hiring-scorecard-and-process|T10]]); exec loops are [[C19-executive-hiring-scorecard-and-loop|C19]]. A scorecard is "not a job description, but a set of outcomes and competencies" ([Who](https://geoffsmart.com/books/who-the-a-method-for-hiring/who-the-book-excerpt/)) — this is the marketing layer over it.

## When to use

- **Write it now, post nothing** (lesson 12 §8). Honest triggers get written at dealer #0, not at dealer #9 with a candidate in the room.
- **Open it when all three HC1 triggers hold**, each dated. The record is [[M01-marketing-charter-and-90-day-plan|M01]].

## How to fill (rules)

1. **All three triggers, each with the date it was met.** A blank date means not met.
2. **Charter before sourcing** — no scorecard, no posting. Head it **"DO NOT POST UNTIL: 10 paying dealers · one channel ≥3 · >15 h/week."**
3. **Every rupee is VERIFY LIVE** against two live postings the week you offer, plus travel and loaded cost.
4. **The ride-along is not waivable.** A structured interview plus a real work sample is the highest-validity combination there is ([IO at Work](https://www.ioatwork.com/best-selection-methods-according-to-research/)); a CV says nothing about a pump office.
5. **Incentives pay on activation only** (E); outcomes come from [[M13-marketing-scorecard-and-cac-calculator|M13]] and [[M07-sales-pipeline-tracker|M07]], not memory.

### C. Scorecard — field associate (weights total 100; 1–4 on T10's rubric)

| Criterion | Wt | Evidence question | 1–4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rejection tolerance | 25 | "You missed target last month — what did you do on day 20?" | _ |
| The owner's language | 20 | "Sell me something in Hindi to a busy 58-year-old owner." | _ |
| Script discipline | 20 | "When did you follow a script you disagreed with?" | _ |
| Writes down what he heard | 15 | "Show me your notes from three recent visits." | _ |
| Domain quickness | 10 | Five minutes on the rate window: "what happens at 10 PM?" | _ |
| Says "I don't know" | 10 | "A promise to a customer you could not keep?" | _ |

**Decisive stage — the paid half-day ride-along.** Four pumps; he runs the M05 opener at two while a founder watches silently. Score what a CV cannot: **the owner's Hindi**, **staying in the room after the first "abhi nahi"**, **writing down what he heard.** Hire at **≥3.0 weighted, nothing at 1, ride-along ≥3**.

### D. The 30-day ramp — every week with a founder

| Week | Shape | Gate at week's end |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Founder runs it, he watches** (8 pumps); the product on his phone; M02, M04, M05 | Recites pitch and objections |
| 2 | **Together**; he opens at 4 pumps; owns [[M03-target-list-builder\|M03]] and M07 | 4 openers run, every row dated |
| 3 | **He runs it, founder silent** (10 pumps); shadows onboarding ([[M08-onboarding-and-activation-checklist\|M08]]) | One demo booked solo |
| 4 | **Alone** (12–15 pumps); founder reads the notes nightly | First activated dealer, **or** 2 demos |

He is a **trainee until gate 2 of lesson 12 §1**: three closes from the written script, by him.

### E. Incentives — pay for activation, never for signups

| Line | Pays when | **VERIFY LIVE** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Per activated dealer** | **Both sides live** — ≥1 customer linked **and** ≥1 invoice inside 14 days ([[M08-onboarding-and-activation-checklist\|M08]]) | ₹1,500–2,500 |
| **Per referral converted** | A Dealer Dost name ([[M09-referral-program-one-pager\|M09]]) reaches *paying* | ₹500–1,000 |
| **Per district milestone** | 15 activated dealers in his district inside 90 days | ₹10,000–15,000 |

A signup bounty buys dead tenants; the loop needs the dealer's customer to confirm a rate. **Never per signup, demo or lead.** Three lines he can compute in his head — complexity "creates confusion and constant clarifications", and you "pay for what you want more of" ([Everstage](https://www.everstage.com/sales-incentive/sales-incentive-plan-template)). Clawed back if the dealer churns inside 90 days.

## Worked example — VSYST (illustrative)

**27 August 2026.** (A) reads: paying dealers **0**, repeatable channels **none**, selling time **~8 h/week** — three blank date cells, review **26 November 2026**, second gate **0/5**. The CEO writes the charter anyway, heads it **"DO NOT POST UNTIL…"**, files it. The domain director starts the bench: two ex-IOCL sales officers from the association, an HPCL TM assistant, a Vyapar field executive who covered Raipur.

**If the triggers fire, say April 2027:** candidate B, three years a Sales Officer on the Dhamtari road, scores 3/4/3/3/2/3 = **3.05**. The ride-along decides it: at pump two the owner says *"abhi nahi, GST return chal raha hai"* and B does not pack up — he asks what that return costs him in hours, writes it down, asks to come back Thursday. Offer **₹26,000 + ₹8,000 travel**, incentives per (E), band checked against three live postings, then [[T11-offer-letter-and-contract-checklists|T11]].

## Common mistakes

- **Two triggers and a tired founder.** Fatigue is not evidence; that is what the review date is for.
- **Skipping a rung** — a lead before the script is proven, a CMO to *find* the engine.
- **Paying per signup**, then owning forty tenants with no customer linked.
- **Waiving the ride-along** for a good CV or a likeable candidate.
- **Letting the associate rewrite the pitch, or the lead go to pumps.**
- **Quoting a band nobody verified this month.**
