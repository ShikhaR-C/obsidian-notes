# M08 — Two-Sided Onboarding and Activation Checklist

_Toolkit · fills exercises 10.1–10.3 in [[07-onboarding-and-activation-getting-both-sides-live|07 — Onboarding and Activation]] · Owner: domain director (the dealer), third director (messages + group), CEO (the activation query) · Cadence: one copy per dealer, ticked daily for 14 days, closed at day 30 · Workbook: activation columns on `Pipeline`, numbers on `Marketing Scorecard` in [vsyst-cmo-workbook.xlsx](vsyst-cmo-workbook.xlsx)._

## Purpose

**One page per new dealer, proving both sides went live** — activated means **≥1 customer firm linked and ≥1 invoice inside 14 days** (HC8). A milestone counts only if those who hit it retain far better than those who don't ([Lenny's](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/what-is-a-good-activation-rate)); setup → aha → habit is why "installed" is never a tick ([GrowthPigeon](https://growthpigeon.com/articles/setup-aha-habit-saas-activation-moments)).

## When to use

Opened the day a dealer says yes, before day 0 is booked. Ticked daily for 14 days, read aloud at the day-14 review. Closed at day 30, when the group closes and support moves to [[T25-customer-support-sop|T25]].

## How to fill (rules)

1. **One file per dealer**, named `M08 — [Pump] — [date]`. Never a shared master sheet.
2. **A tick needs evidence** — invoice number, customer name, screenshot.
3. **Day 0 ends with a rate in the system**, or day 0 is not over.
4. **We type on day 0; the dealer types on day 3** — note who held the phone.
5. **Refusals get a verbatim reason**; that column is the spec for the WhatsApp-first flow.
6. **Amber is real:** invoices, zero linked customers = **dealer-side activated only**.

**E. Activation sign-off** (day 14, signed by DD at the pump)

> `[Pump]` installed `[date]`. Customers linked `___`. Invoices `___`. First invoice day `___`. Confirmation `___%`. **Activated: YES / DEALER-SIDE ONLY / NO.** Referrals `___`, `___`. Testimonial Y/N. Signed `______`.

**F. Early warning** — checked day 7, 14, 21, 30; act inside 48 hours, which measurably improves survival ([CampaignHQ](https://blog.campaignhq.co/b2b-saas-customer-retention-playbook-india-2026)).

- No rate published 2 nights running → call the **owner**, not the munim
- Zero customers linked by day 7 → offer to make the call ourselves
- Confirmation rate <50% for a week → visit the customer's office; check push permissions
- Invoices flat while orders continue → an hour with the munim
- WhatsApp group silent 5 days → show up at the pump unannounced

## Worked example — VSYST (illustrative)

`M08 — Shree Balaji Fuels, Dhamtari Road — 2026-09-02`. Day 0 ran 105 minutes; rates in at 8:40 PM, munim watching. Top three named day 1: Verma (₹6.2L), Gupta (₹3.8L), Sai (₹1.1L). Verma's order-placer and accountant installed day 2, **first confirmation 11:14 PM**, first invoice day 3. Gupta refused — logged verbatim, *"munim ji purane register par hi kaam karenge"* — dealer orders on his behalf, ledger PDF by WhatsApp. Sign-off: **1 linked, 22 invoices, 78% confirmed → ACTIVATED**. Gupta re-asked in week 6 after a rate dispute, and installed.

## Common mistakes

- **Ticking "installed" as activation** — the line is a linked customer *and* an invoice.
- **Installing only on the owner's phone**, when the order-placer and accountant are the users.
- **Letting the dealer skip the invite** — half-activated tenants churn to any cheaper bill book.
- **Doing the typing all fortnight.** White-glove is a start, not a service contract ([Dock](https://www.dock.us/library/white-glove-onboarding)).
- **Group left open past day 30**, or the refusal column blank.
