# M10 — Partner Playbook: OMC TM, Association, CA, Hardware Vendor

_Toolkit · fills the exercises in [[05-channels-where-to-find-them-and-in-what-order|05 — Channels]] and [[08-referrals-associations-and-partners-the-warm-engine|08 — The Warm Engine]] · Owner: DD (OMC, hardware), D3 (association, CA) · Cadence: tracker reviewed every Friday, 15 minutes · Workbook tab: `Channel Experiments` in [vsyst-cmo-workbook.xlsx](vsyst-cmo-workbook.xlsx); converted intros land in `Referral Tracker`._

## Purpose

A partner is somebody whose trust with a pump owner you borrow. This sheet stops that borrowing being improvised: per partner type, **what they want, what we offer, the opener in Hindi and English, the single ask, the follow-up cadence** — plus one tracker, so a warm introduction never dies in a chat thread.

## When to use

- **Week 2 of the Raipur 100 campaign**, before the first partner conversation.
- **Before every partner meeting**, so all three directors make the same offer.
- **Every Friday** — update `intros received`, `dealers won`, `next action`. A row with no next action is dead.

## How to fill (rules)

1. **One named human per row.** "IOCL" is not a partner; a sales officer with a phone number is ([[05-channels-where-to-find-them-and-in-what-order|lesson 05]] §2).
2. **One ask per conversation.** Five names, or a 15-minute slot — never both.
3. **Never open with money.** Lead with what the partner's own people gain; commercials come later, if at all.
4. **Money moves only through a written agreement.** A PSU officer cannot take cash-in-hand; offering it ends the relationship permanently. Whether an individual fee is payable at all is a **VERIFY LIVE** question for the CA and company secretary.
5. **Bands, not improvisation.** Introduce-only: **10–15% of first-year revenue**; co-sell and own the relationship: **20–30% plus 5–8% renewal**; total partner-assisted cost under **40% of first-year value** ([Channels as a Strategy, 2026](https://channels-as-a-strategy.com/saas-reseller-commission-structure/)). Against the ₹599 / ₹1,799 / ₹4,999 per-GSTIN tiers in [[08-dzzlo-subscription-strategy|the subscription direction]] that is roughly ₹700–9,000 a dealer-year — **VERIFY LIVE, sign-off pending**.
6. **Non-cash first** — training, a co-branded Dealer Day, a Hindi checklist with the partner's logo, a monthly data summary. Near-zero cost, always accepted.
7. **Log every intro the day it arrives**, with the dealer's name. Untracked intros are this channel's biggest leak.

## Common mistakes

- **Asking for an MoU first.** Nine months and a legal review, versus one message for five names.
- **Two asks in one message.** The partner picks neither.
- **Offering cash to a PSU officer.** Permanent, no second chance.
- **Quoting price tiers before sign-off.** They are **VERIFY LIVE** — quote the value, not the number.
- **An intro not in the tracker by Friday** did not happen.
- **Working transporter associations before dealers are live** — a dead install and a burnt name.
