# M09 — "Dealer Dost" Referral Program One-Pager and Ask Script

_Toolkit · fills the exercises in [[08-referrals-associations-and-partners-the-warm-engine|08 — Referrals, Associations, OMCs and Partners]] · Owner: domain director (the ask and the tracker); CEO signs off every rupee · Cadence: ask at each dealer's activation moment; tracker updated same day; reviewed Friday, 30 minutes · Workbook tab: `Referral Tracker` in [vsyst-cmo-workbook.xlsx](vsyst-cmo-workbook.xlsx)._

## Purpose

Three artefacts in one file: **the page the dealer sees**, **the script you say**, and **the tracker that makes it a countable channel**. It exists because 88% of people trust recommendations from people they know, above every other marketing message ([Nielsen, 2021](https://www.nielsen.com/insights/2021/beyond-martech-building-trust-with-consumers-and-engaging-where-sentiment-is-high/)). Economics, tiers and fraud model are settled in [[06_REFERRAL_PROGRAM|the Dealer Dost research doc]] — **do not re-derive them here.**

## When to use

- **Print v1 before your first activated dealer reaches his first clean month-end.**
- **Fire the ask at the activation moment**, never at the sale ([[08-referrals-associations-and-partners-the-warm-engine|lesson 08]] §2).
- **Re-issue whenever the tier sheet changes** — old rupee figures in circulation are a promise you did not make.

## How to fill (rules)

1. **One page, Hindi on top, English below.** The father reads the top half; the son reads the bottom half.
2. **Every number checked against the signed tier sheet — VERIFY LIVE.** The figures below were costed in April 2026 on a ₹2,500/month ARPA assumption; pricing has since moved to per-GSTIN tiers under owner sign-off ([[08-dzzlo-subscription-strategy|subscription strategy]]).
3. **Say the ask, do not send it.** The page is the leave-behind, not the opener.
4. **Never ask "do you know anyone?"** A generic ask "requires too much thinking on the potential referrer's part" ([ReferralRock](https://referralrock.com/blog/how-to-ask-for-referrals/)). Ask only for **the two pumps he already talks to.**
5. **Reward on payment, not signup** — the strongest anti-fraud lever there is.
6. **Log every ask the same day**, including the failures, and take consent before using any proof asset (D).
7. **TDS at 10% under Section 194R** can apply once benefits to one recipient cross ₹20,000 in a financial year ([ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/analysis-of-section-194r-of-the-income-tax-act-1961)) — our CA signs off before the first payout. **VERIFY LIVE.**

## Template

### A. The one-pager, as the dealer sees it

> **DZZLO डीलर दोस्त — अपने दोस्त को बताइए, ₹2,000 कमाइए**
>
> **आपको क्या मिलेगा:** आपके बताए पंप का एक आउटलेट चालू होते ही **₹2,000 UPI**। वो पंप तीसरे महीने पेमेंट शुरू करे तो **और ₹3,000**।
> **आपके दोस्त को क्या मिलेगा:** शुरू के **2 महीने बिल्कुल मुफ़्त** — पूरा DZZLO, कोई कार्ड नहीं।
> **कैसे बताएं:** बस उनका नाम और नंबर हमें WhatsApp कर दीजिए — या हमारा मैसेज उन्हें फ़ॉरवर्ड कर दीजिए। बाकी हम करेंगे।
> **पैसा कब मिलेगा:** आउटलेट चालू होने पर पहला हिस्सा; तीसरे महीने की पेमेंट के बाद दूसरा — सीधे आपके UPI पर।
> **शर्तें, सीधी भाषा में:** एक ही PAN/GSTIN दोनों तरफ़ नहीं चलेगा। नाम तभी गिना जाएगा जब वो मालिक मिलने को तैयार हों। पंप 6 महीने में छोड़ दे तो इनाम वापस। ₹20,000/साल से ऊपर TDS कट सकता है।
>
> **DZZLO Dealer Dost — tell a friend, earn ₹2,000**
>
> **You get:** ₹2,000 by UPI the moment the pump you named goes live on one outlet. Another ₹3,000 when they start paying in month 3.
> **Your friend gets:** the first 2 months free — full DZZLO, no card.
> **How to refer:** WhatsApp us their name and number, or forward our message to them. We do the rest.
> **When it pays:** first part on go-live, second after the month-3 payment, straight to your UPI.
> **Terms, in plain words:** the same PAN or GSTIN cannot sit on both sides; a name counts once the owner agrees to meet us; the reward is clawed back if the pump leaves within 6 months; TDS may apply above ₹20,000 a year.
>
> _All amounts **VERIFY LIVE** against the signed tier sheet before printing._

### B. Ask script and timing rules

| Moment | What you open with | What you ask for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **First clean month-end** | _"Sir, is mahine month-end saaf nikla — koi ledger ka jhagda nahi."_ | The two pumps he already talks to, by name |
| **First dispute avoided** | _"Wo rate wali baat OTP se ek minute mein khatam ho gayi na?"_ | The same two names |
| **Unprompted praise** | Stop the conversation there | The same two names |
| **Never** | At the sale, or in the first 14 days | — |
Then remove the work: he can (1) forward our message, (2) send the name and number, or (3) make a three-way WhatsApp group and type one line. **Ask for (3), accept (1).**

### C. `Referral Tracker` columns

| Column | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Referrer (dealer + pump) | The activated dealer making the introduction |
| Referred pump (name, owner, place) | One row per pump, never "2 names given" |
| Date asked | Same day, always — even if he said no |
| Date received | Blank until a name actually arrives |
| Stage | Asked → Name received → Meeting agreed → Demo → Live → Paying |
| Reward due (₹) | From stage; ₹0 until Live |
| Paid (date, UTR) | Blank until UPI is sent |
| Owner | The founder chasing the next step |

### D. Proof-capture checklist

- [ ] **60-second video**, vertical, at his own pump, in Hindi/Chhattisgarhi — problem before, what changed, what he'd tell another dealer
- [ ] **One-line quote with a rupee number**, verbatim, with name, pump, location
- [ ] **Before/after month-end screenshot**, customer names blurred
- [ ] **Consent on WhatsApp:** _"Main is video ko DZZLO ke marketing mein use karne ki anumati deta hoon."_ Personal data may be processed only after consent, withdrawable at any time ([PRS India — DPDP Act 2023](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/digital-personal-data-protection-bill-2023))

## Worked example — VSYST (illustrative)

Friday, 09:40. The domain director closes Sharma ji's first month-end on the Dhamtari road — ledger tallied, nothing chased at 11 PM. He does not open the one-pager. He says: _"Sir, aapke aas-paas do pump aise honge jinse aap roz baat karte hain."_ Sharma ji names two — the HP outlet at Tatibandh and his cousin's pump at Abhanpur — and makes a three-way WhatsApp group with the first. The director leaves the printed page on the desk, fills two rows in `Referral Tracker` before starting the car (both **Asked → Name received**, next step dated Monday), shoots a 55-second video and gets the consent line typed into the chat. Eleven minutes. Scorecard that week: asks 1, names 2, testimonials 1.

## Common mistakes

- **Handing over the page instead of making the ask.** The page is the leave-behind; the sentence is the product.
- **"Do you know anyone?"** — the ask that guarantees a polite no.
- **Printing rupee figures the CEO has not signed.** A withdrawn reward costs more trust than it bought.
- **Counting a name as a referral** — it counts when the owner agrees to meet.
- **Using a testimonial without the consent line**, or forgetting to blur customer names.
