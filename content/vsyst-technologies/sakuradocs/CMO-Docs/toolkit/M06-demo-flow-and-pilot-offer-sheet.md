# M06 — The 10-Minute Demo Flow and the Pilot Offer Sheet

_Toolkit · fills exercises 10.2 and 10.3 in [[06-founder-led-sales-the-pump-visit-demo-and-close|06 — Founder-Led Sales: The Pump Visit, the Demo and the Close]] · Owner: domain director (delivery), CEO (the seeded demo tenant) · Cadence: rehearsed against a timer before every field day · Workbook tab: none — a printed one-pager; outcomes land in `Pipeline`._

## Purpose

Two objects, one folder. **The demo flow** is ten minutes on the owner's own customers and his own rupee figures: it turns `Visited` into `Demoed`. **The pilot offer sheet** hits the counter at minute nine and turns that into `Trial started`, with two dates on it. Alone, the demo earns a compliment; the sheet, a refusal. SMB demo-to-close runs ~**32%** ([Optifai](https://optif.ai/learn/questions/demo-to-close-conversion-rate/)) — a number that belongs to people who close in the room.

## When to use

- **At the pump, first visit,** owner present. Never on a call, never as a PDF.
- **Again on visit two** with the son or the accountant — read the room per [[06-founder-led-sales-the-pump-visit-demo-and-close|lesson 06]] §2.4.
- **Never with only a manager:** it does not exit stage 3.

## How to fill (rules)

1. **Seed the tenant the night before** — real pump names, today's rates, a real vehicle number, non-round balances.
2. **Minute 0 is paper.** The phone stays pocketed until he names a rupee figure and you write it down in front of him.
3. **Timer visible to you, not him; one sentence per screen, then silence.** [[C10-founder-led-sales-pipeline-and-script|C10]] times the eight screen-minutes; this adds the paper minute and the ask. The lines below are a ceiling, not a script.
4. **Never show** settings, superadmin, DIP, the roadmap or a price list; **never promise** the WhatsApp-first zero-install flow — the direction, **not built** ([[11_EDUCATION_GAP|dealer–customer gap]] §7–8). Today: the same app, by phone-OTP invite. Say so.
5. **The sheet comes out pre-filled** — name, pump, both dates, before you leave the car.

## Template

**A. The ten minutes**

| Min | Screen                             | What you say                                                                  | He feels                       |
| --- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| 0   | **Paper only**                     | _"Sabse bada udhaar customer kaun? Aaj kitna baaki hai?"_ Write the ₹          | Asked about his money — the hesitation **is** the pitch |
| 1   | **Rate master**, that customer     | _"Kal ka rate — [customer] ke liye."_                                         | "This is my pump"              |
| 2   | **Confirm window, 10 PM–6 AM**     | _"Raat ko unke phone pe. Confirm kiya — timestamp ke saath."_                 | The minute-0 dispute is dead   |
| 3   | **Auto-lock, 6 AM**                | _"Confirm nahi kiya to 6 baje lock."_                                         | Relief, then a question        |
| 4   | **Order — customer side**          | _"Unhone yahin se order daala — 5,000 litre."_                                | Fewer 9 PM calls               |
| 5   | **Dispatch + driver OTP**          | _"Gaadi nikli, driver ko OTP. Bina OTP delivery confirm nahi."_               | Shortage protection            |
| 6   | **Invoice** — GST, TCS past ₹50L   | _"Invoice apne aap. GST sahi. TCS ka flag bhi."_                              | The accountant's work shrinks  |
| 7   | **Ledger** — that customer         | _"Ye unka khaata. Yehi unko bhi dikhta hai."_                                 | One truth, not two diaries     |
| 8   | **Statement PDF → his WhatsApp**   | _"Abhi aapke WhatsApp pe bhej raha hoon."_ Send it in front of him            | He is holding something        |
| 9   | **Phone down. The sheet.**         | _"Do hafte, bina card ke. Top teen udhaar customers hum khud chalu karenge."_ | A dated, reversible yes        |

**B. Pre-demo checklist** (the night before, ~20 minutes)

- [ ] Tenant seeded with **the pump's own customer names**, else real district names
- [ ] **Today's actual rates**, correct OMC (IOCL / BPCL / HPCL); one real vehicle number (CG-04-…)
- [ ] **Non-round opening balances** on three customers — ₹4,87,320, not ₹5,00,000
- [ ] Phone ≥80%, power bank, ten screens saved offline for no-signal villages
- [ ] Notifications silenced **except** the DZZLO push — it must fire on screen at minute 2
- [ ] Statement PDF pre-generated, for when the network dies at minute 8
- [ ] Timer at 10:00; 20 pre-filled sheets, 20 rupee-number sheets, the `wa.me` QR card, pens

**C. The pilot offer sheet — one page, both languages**

```
DZZLO -- 14 DIN KA TRIAL / 14-DAY TRIAL
[Dealer]______ [Pump]______ Shuru/Start: ______
Faisla / Decision meeting: ______ (start + 14 din)
Card nahi, payment nahi. (No card, no payment.)

HUM KYA KARENGE / WHAT WE DO
 [ ] TOP 3 udhaar customers hum khud is hafte chalu karenge --
     opening balance, invite, pehla order.
 [ ] Din 0 aur Din 1 pump pe; Din 3, 7, 13 WhatsApp/call.
 [ ] Ek naam, ek number, poore 14 din.

AAP KYA KARENGE / WHAT YOU DO
 [ ] Din 0 pe 45 minute.  [ ] Roz raat ko rate DZZLO mein.
 [ ] Kaam na kare to usi hafte bata dijiye.
 Counter pe roz kaun baithega: ________ (naam + phone)

SAFALTA / SUCCESS -- Din 14 ko saath mein tick
 [ ] Teen customers ka balance sahi, bina diary khole
 [ ] 14 mein se 12 raat rate set   [ ] Kam se kam 10 invoice
 [ ] Aap kahen: "agar ye kal band ho jaye to dikkat hogi"

DIN 14 / DAY 14 -- usi din, teen mein se ek:
 (a) chaalu, plan chun kar  (b) 14 din aur, naya likha target
 (c) band -- data export, aur aapse ek ghanta

TRIAL KE BAAD -- per company (per GSTIN); staff & driver free
 Rs 599 / Rs 1,799 / Rs 4,999 per month + Enterprise, GST alag.
 **VERIFY LIVE -- owner sign-off pending.** Payment web par, app mein nahi.

WhatsApp: __________ (VSYST -- [name])
Hastakshar: ______ (Dealer)   ______ (VSYST)
```

## Worked example — VSYST (illustrative)

Tuesday 12:10, a DODO pump near Dhamtari. Minute 0: his biggest credit customer is a 14-truck fleet at _"karib chaar-saade chaar lakh"_ — he cannot say it to the rupee; that hesitation is the sale. Minute 6, the munim speaks — _"TCS ka flag apne aap?"_ — and the demo is over: the veto has become a question. Minute 9, the sheet goes down, 26 Aug and 9 Sep on it; he signs and names his nephew as operator. Elapsed 11:40; the row moves **Visited → Demoed → Trial started** that evening in [[M07-sales-pipeline-tracker|M07]].

## Common mistakes

| Mistake                                 | The fix                                                |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Opening with the phone, not the paper   | Minute 0 is a question and a rupee figure                 |
| Demoing on "Test Customer 1"            | Seed the night before; non-round balances                 |
| Running 25 minutes because it went well | Timer. The best demos end early, sheet signed             |
| Leaving without the day-14 date in chat | A trial without a decision date drifts and dies           |
