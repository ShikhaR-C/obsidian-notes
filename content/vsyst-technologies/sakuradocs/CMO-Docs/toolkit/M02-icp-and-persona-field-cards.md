# M02 — ICP and Persona Field Cards (dealer side + customer side)

_Toolkit · fills exercises 8.1–8.2 in [[03-who-exactly-icp-personas-and-the-target-list|03 — Who Exactly]] · Owner: CEO owns the ICP, the domain director owns the personas · Cadence: written Week 1, re-read before every pump visit, re-cut every 10 visits · Workbook tab: none — a one-page card you print and carry; its scores land in `Target List` in [vsyst-cmo-workbook.xlsx](vsyst-cmo-workbook.xlsx)._

## Purpose

One page you hold on a forecourt. It answers three questions before you open your mouth: **is this dealer worth my morning, who in this family decides, and what does each of them need to hear.** ICP theory, the numeric rubric and the discovery script are [[C09-icp-and-customer-discovery-guide|C09]]; this is the field card, short enough to re-read in the car.

It exists because here **the buying unit is a family, not a person.** The father signs; the son is the de-facto technology decision-maker; the munim is the quiet veto; the manager lives with the result ([[12_OWNER_ACQUISITION|Owner Acquisition §2.2]]). Sell to one, ignore three, and a warm pump goes cold.

## When to use

- **Week 1**, once ([[03-who-exactly-icp-personas-and-the-target-list|lesson 03]], exercises 8.1–8.2).
- **Before each pump visit** — thirty seconds, so you know who you are addressing ([[06-founder-led-sales-the-pump-visit-demo-and-close|lesson 06]]).
- **After every 10 visits** — re-cut the must-haves against what closed. The ICP is a hypothesis until the win rate confirms it.
- Feeds [[M04-positioning-and-message-house|M04]] and [[M05-pitch-scripts-and-objection-handling|M05]].

## How to fill (rules)

1. **Must-have means walk away.** Visit anyway and it was a nice-to-have. Be honest or the card is decoration.
2. **Persona lines in the person's own words** — Hindi where that is how it would be said. An English pain will not survive a forecourt.
3. **"Must hear" is one sentence you could say out loud**, not a benefit statement.
4. **Fill from a real person you know**, never from this document. An unfillable box gets `don't know` — a discovery question.
5. **Disqualifiers are absolute and dated.** A disqualified pump gets a re-look date in `Target List` ([[M03-target-list-builder|M03]]), not deletion.
6. **One page.** If it does not print on one side of A4, cut.

## Template

**(a) The ICP — one line, three lists**

| | Fill |
| --- | --- |
| **ICP, one line** | _<segment> in <geography> who <does this painful thing> and has <the enabling condition>_ |
| **Must-have** (all, or don't visit) | 1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … |
| **Nice-to-have** (raises the score) | 1. … 2. … 3. … |
| **Disqualify** (mark, date, move on) | 1. … 2. … 3. … 4. … |
| **Beachhead segments (max 2)** | … and … — because … |

**(b) The seven people**, one row each, four columns — **Person · Cares about · Fears · Must hear (one sentence)**. Dealer side: father (signatory), son (2nd gen, 28–42), munim, pump manager. Customer side, reached *through* a live dealer, never cold (HC2): transporter owner (6–100 trucks), factory purchase manager, contractor. The worked example below is the same tables, filled.

## Worked example — VSYST (illustrative)

**(a) ICP.** *A DODO petrol-pump dealer in Raipur district within ~60 km of the city, selling 5–40 B2B customers on credit off a paper slip book, with a second-generation family member in the business.*

- **Must-have:** owner present and reachable · ≥5 active B2B credit customers · ≤60 km from Raipur · dealer-owned, dealer-operated.
- **Nice-to-have:** son or daughter 28–42 in operations · highway location (NH-53 / NH-30 / Ring Road) · automated dispensers · sells lubricants · known to the domain director or an existing dealer.
- **Disqualify:** cash/UPI-only, no credit book · owner absent, manager- or NRI-run · corporate-managed site · live contract with another OMS (log the renewal month) · active OMC dispute.
- **Beachhead:** **second-generation-run pumps** and **owner-run pumps with a real credit book**, highway first — both carry the credit pain and both have a signer on site.

**(b) The seven people.**

| Person | Cares about | Fears | Must hear |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Father, 58** — signs, owns the OMC relationship | Land, licence, the OMC's opinion of him | A GST notice, a Legal Metrology seal, an unrecoverable udhaari | "Sharma bhai ke pump pe chal raha hai. Aapki purani register chalti rahegi — pehle mahine dono saath." |
| **Son, 34** — engineer, runs the day-to-day | Today's numbers on his phone; being taken seriously | Staying "papa ka beta" with no system | "Kal subah phone pe dekh lijiye — kis customer ka kitna baaki hai, aaj ka rate kya confirm hua." |
| **Munim** — part-time accountant, feeds the CA | A shorter month-end | Learning software in public; being blamed for a mismatch | "GSTR data seedha export hoga. Naya kuch seekhna nahi — bas ek screen." |
| **Pump manager** | Fewer night calls; not being blamed for shortage | Being measured, then punished by a dashboard | "Driver OTP se raat ko phone nahi aayega — parchi ka jhagda khatam." |

*Customer side — reached through the dealer:*

| Person | Cares about | Fears | Must hear |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Transporter owner, 18 trucks** | Litres per truck; rate agreed vs charged; month-end in two days, not ten | Driver-attendant collusion; paying for diesel he never got | "Aapke pump wale ne aapko jod diya hai — kal ka rate raat ko confirm karenge, har truck ka hisaab app mein." |
| **Factory purchase manager** | GST-clean invoices, audit proof, one statement a month | An auditor query he cannot answer | "Every litre logged with the vehicle, time and rate — one statement your auditor accepts." |
| **Contractor / site owner** | Diesel reaching the machine, not the market | Silent leakage across scattered sites | "Site-wise diesel, per machine, driver OTP against every delivery." |

**Why these cards read the way they do.** Most Indian SMEs run on WhatsApp Business ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/how-whatsapp-business-is-bringing-indias-smes-to-the-digital-fold/)) — **VERIFY LIVE** before quoting a percentage. OMC eligibility puts owners at 21–60, 10th-pass minimum, which is why the son installs the app ([Franchise India](https://www.franchiseindia.com/insights/en/article/petrol-pump-business-plan-in-india-the-ultimate-guide.56771)). Dealer margin is a regulated commission, not a markup ([PPAC](https://ppac.gov.in/prices/dealers-distributors-commission-on-petrol-diesel-pds-kerosene-domestic-lpg)) — so "save money" lands weakly and "stop losing money" lands hard. The factory persona sits in five Raipur estates: 140 medium and large units, 5,441 registered industrial units ([MSME-DI](https://dcmsme.gov.in/dips/2016-17/Raipur.pdf)) — **VERIFY LIVE**, 2016-17 data.

## Common mistakes

1. **One persona for "the dealer."** There are four people; a pitch aimed at their average persuades none.
2. **An ICP describing everyone who could use the product.** That is a market. Exclude nothing, decide nothing.
3. **No disqualify list.** An hour on a manager-run pump is an hour off an A-list one.
4. **English pains.** "Reconciliation inefficiency" is not what anybody says. "Har mahine hisaab pe jhagda" is.
5. **Filling the customer card before a dealer is live** — HC2; nothing to show, no way to reach them ([[02-the-two-sided-problem-sell-to-the-dealer-seed-the-customer|lesson 02]]).
6. **Never re-cutting it.** After 10 visits, one must-have will be wrong.
