# M12 — One-Page Campaign Brief, Budget and Post-Mortem

_Toolkit · fills the exercises in [[10-campaigns-planning-running-and-measuring-one|10 — Campaigns: Planning, Running and Measuring One]] · Owner: third director (brief, budget, log); one named owner per budget line and per district · Cadence: one brief per campaign; weekly log filled Monday 09:30; post-mortem within five days of close · Workbook tab: `Campaign Budget` in [vsyst-cmo-workbook.xlsx](vsyst-cmo-workbook.xlsx)._

## Purpose

Four artefacts on one page: **the brief** that turns activity into a campaign, **the budget** that puts a name against every rupee, **the log** that catches the broken step in Week 6 instead of Week 12, and **the post-mortem** that makes campaign eleven cheaper than campaign one. The brief carries **one number** — Lean Analytics' One Metric That Matters, which "forces you to draw a line in the sand" ([Holistics](https://www.holistics.io/blog/lean-analytics-part-1-an-introduction-to-analytical-thinking/)). The log stays on **controllable inputs** — visits, demos, activations ([Commoncog — Amazon's WBR](https://commoncog.com/the-amazon-weekly-business-review/)). The post-mortem is **blameless** — "everyone involved in an incident had good intentions" — and every finding becomes an action or is deleted ([Google SRE](https://sre.google/sre-book/postmortem-culture/)).

## When to use

- **Once per campaign, before a rupee is spent** — signed by all three founders, at every rung ([[10-campaigns-planning-running-and-measuring-one|lesson 10]] §3).
- **§E only after the rung-3 entry test passes** ([[12-scaling-hiring-and-launching-the-big-campaign|lesson 12]] §5); before that, mark it **DRAFT — not funded**.
- **Never mid-campaign to change the audience, offer, activation definition or the number.** That ends the campaign: write a fresh brief, record the abandonment.

## How to fill (rules)

1. **One number, X to Y, by a date**, and queryable — the CEO writes the query into the brief. Two numbers is zero numbers.
2. **The entry test is a checkbox.** No measured pass of the rung below, no signature.
3. **1–2 channels, in the [[12_OWNER_ACQUISITION|Owner Acquisition]] §5 order.** No paid ads on the dealer side before ~50 dealers.
4. **Every budget line carries a rupee figure and one name.** Founder time (~15 h/week × 3) sits **outside** the cash budget, and is stated so nobody calls the campaign free.
5. **Cash inside ₹30–50k/month** while pre-revenue (HC7). Every rate here is illustrative until Week 6 — **VERIFY LIVE**, then replace it with your own.
6. **Log filled Monday 09:30, zeros included; post-mortem within five days of the close** — 45 minutes, six boxes, all three founders. Blameless and actionable, or nobody writes an honest one twice.

## Template

### A. The one-page brief

| Field | Fill |
| --- | --- |
| **Campaign name · ladder rung** | Name · rung 1–6 |
| **Goal + THE one number** | X → Y by when, defined in full (activated = ≥3 customer firms linked **and** ≥1 invoice in 14 days), and the query that returns it |
| **Audience · list source** | How many **named** businesses, from [[M03-target-list-builder\|M03]]; which customer firms |
| **Message** | The one line, in their words — [[M04-positioning-and-message-house\|M04]] |
| **Offer** | What they get for saying yes today — [[M06-demo-flow-and-pilot-offer-sheet\|M06]] |
| **Channels (1–2, in order)** | Referral → OMC TM → association → visits |
| **Budget** | ₹/month × months (table B); founder time excluded and named |
| **Calendar by week · owners** | A row per week; a founder's name on every row and line |
| **Risks · review cadence** | Three risks, each with its early warning · Mon 09:30 numbers, Fri 18:00 learnings, mid-campaign kill/double, post-mortem |
| **Entry test passed?** | ☐ Yes — evidence and date. ☐ No — do not sign |

## Worked example — VSYST (illustrative)

**Brief.** _Raipur 100 · rung 2._ One number: **10 activated dealers by end of Week 12** (≥3 customer firms linked and ≥1 invoice in 14 days). Audience: the 100 named pumps within 60 km ([[M03-target-list-builder|M03]]) and their credit customers. Message: _"पिछले महीने रेट को लेकर कितनी बहस हुई?"_ Offer: 14-day no-card trial, on-site setup, ledger migration, founding-dealer listing (price **VERIFY LIVE**). Channels: referral → IOCL TM (Amey) → association → visits. Budget ₹40,000/month × ~2.5 ≈ **₹1.0 L**. Entry test: ☑ dealer #1 both sides live, ≥1 invoice/week for four weeks.

**Log, Week 6.** 9 visits, 5 demos, 2 trials, 0 activations, ₹9,400 — _"both cold walk-ins refused the demo; both introduced pumps sat through all ten minutes."_ Change Monday: two cold-visit slots become referral asks.

**Post-mortem, Week 12.** Hypothesis: a named list worked warm converts at 10%. Result: **8 against 10** — the gap opened at demo → trial, not at the door. Spend ₹1.02 L, blended CAC **₹12,750** against a planned ₹10,000.

| Channel | Spend ₹ | Activated | CAC ₹ | Verdict |
| --- | --: | --: | --: | --- |
| Referral | 22,000 | 4 | 5,500 | **Keep** — double the ask rate |
| IOCL territory manager | 8,000 | 2 | 4,000 | **Keep** — one meeting a month |
| Association / Dealer Day | 30,000 | 2 | 15,000 | **Change** — one CA-led session, not two |
| Cold field visits | 42,000 | 0 | — | **Kill** — ₹42,000 freed |

Next campaign: rung 3, dated entry test — ≥10 paying dealers, one channel producing ≥3 repeatably, CAC known ±30%, activation ≥60%.

## Common mistakes

- **Two numbers.** Reach *and* signups means nobody can say on the last day whether it worked.
- **Founder time left out silently.** A ₹1 L campaign that ate 450 founder-hours reads as cheap.
- **Peeking at the field A/B test and calling a winner.** Stopping when it looks good turns a claimed 5% significance into a real **26.1%** ([Evan Miller](https://www.evanmiller.org/how-not-to-run-an-ab-test.html)). Fix the sample first; only test what you expect to double.
- **Back-filling the log on Friday**, or skipping the zero weeks — they carry the diagnosis.
- **A post-mortem with no consequence** is a diary; **§E run before CAC is known** is four times the burn with no diagnosis.
