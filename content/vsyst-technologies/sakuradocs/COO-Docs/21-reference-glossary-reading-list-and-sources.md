# 21 — Reference: Glossary, Reading List and Sources

_Reference · use at any time. Every term the course uses, every book worth your time, and every source behind the claims — in one place._

## 1. How to use this page

- **Look a term up** — §2 is the glossary, alphabetical. The third column names the lesson or template where the term does its real work, in the course's own shorthand (`08` = lesson 08, `T22` = template T22); §5 turns those numbers back into links.
- **Find a book** — §3 is the reading list, one operator lesson per book, ending with the five to read first if you only have a few months.
- **Check a source** — §4 is every external link the course cites, grouped by topic. If a claim in a lesson mattered to you, its source is in that list.

Two rules about §2. **Terms are defined as this course uses them** — for an Indian Private Limited, bootstrapped, pre-revenue SaaS startup of three to eight people. The same words mean slightly different things at a 500-person company or in a US context; where the difference matters, the lesson says so. And **every India-specific statutory item carries VERIFY LIVE**, because rates, thresholds, forms and due dates change every Budget and often mid-year: the glossary tells you what the thing _is_ and who owns it, never what the current number is. Confirm the number with the CA, the CS, the lawyer or the vendor's own page before you act on it — the same honesty rule the [[COO-Docs/index|landing page]] sets for the whole course.

**Start here, in this order.** Five books, one a month, if you are taking the seat for the first time and have limited reading time. Each one changes exactly one artefact — schedule that change in [[T30-coo-charter-and-90-day-plan|T30]] when you schedule the book.

1. **_High Output Management_** — because it settles what the job _is_ before you start doing it. Changes your 1:1 format ([[T04-one-on-one-template|T04]]) and how you pick what to do on Monday.
2. **_Traction_** — because it is the fastest installable operating system for a company of your size. Changes the weekly meeting agenda ([[T03-weekly-ops-meeting-agenda|T03]]) and gives you Rocks and a Scorecard.
3. **_The Goal_** — because a small company that improves the wrong step gets busier without getting better. Changes what your quarter is about: one constraint statement, written down.
4. **_The Checklist Manifesto_** — short, and it is the argument for everything in [[toolkit/index|the toolkit]]. Changes your release and onboarding checklists from "we should" into "we do".
5. **_The Great CEO Within_** — because writing before meeting is the single habit that makes a three-person leadership team fast. Changes how issues reach the weekly meeting.

After those five, read by need: _Measure What Matters_ and _Good Strategy Bad Strategy_ before your first quarterly planning day ([[19-planning-okrs-and-the-quarterly-rhythm|lesson 19]]), _Who_ before your first hire ([[09-people-operations|lesson 09]]), _Working Backwards_ when the scorecard turns into a written weekly review ([[16-metrics-dashboards-and-scorecards|lesson 16]]), and _Team Topologies_ only once there is more than one team to shape.
