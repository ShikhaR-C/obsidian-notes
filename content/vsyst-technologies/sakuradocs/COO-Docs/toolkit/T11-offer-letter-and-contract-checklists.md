# T11 — Offer Letter and Contract Checklists

_Toolkit · fills the exercises in [[09-people-operations|09 — People Operations]] and the contract-library work in [[05-legal-and-governance-foundation|05 — Legal and Governance Foundation]] · Owner: COO (the lawyer drafts, the CA confirms tax) · Cadence: run per engagement; the library reviewed yearly · Workbook tab: none — signed copies go to Drive and the binder ([[T02-company-binder-checklist|T02]])._

## Purpose

**Checklists, not legal drafts.** These lists say what each people-and-partner document must contain so you can brief the lawyer once, check every draft that comes back, and reuse the signed masters forever — the [[startup-operations-plan|Startup Operations Plan]]'s rule: a one-time lawyer fee, reused forever. Three India-specific facts make the checklists non-optional: **appointment letters are now a statutory requirement in a prescribed format** under the Labour Codes ([KPMG — Labour Codes central rules, 2026](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/2026/flash-alert-2026-127.html)); the uniform "wages" definition caps excluded allowances at 50%, so **basic must be at least 50% of wages** ([ClearTax — new labour codes](https://cleartax.in/s/new-labour-codes)); and under the Copyright Act s.17 an employer owns an employee's work by default, but **a contractor owns what they create unless it is assigned in writing** — the gap that puts DZZLO's chain of title at risk ([[05-legal-and-governance-foundation|lesson 05]] §5). **Everything here is VERIFY LIVE with the lawyer (labour, IP, POSH, stamp duty) and the CA (TDS, GST)** — the checklist tells you what to ask, never the answer.

## When to use

- Before the **first offer** goes out — the offer → appointment letter → agreement chain exists before the person does ([PolicyOwn — startup compliance checklist 2026](https://policyown.com/blog/startup-compliance-checklist-2026); [Stripe — hiring your first employees](https://stripe.com/en-br/resources/more/how-to-hire-the-first-employees-for-your-startup-a-guide-for-founders)).
- Before **any freelancer starts** — the contractor agreement and IP assignment are signed before the first commit and the first payment, retroactively for everyone who has already touched code, design or copy (lesson 05 §5).
- Before any partner, pilot or vendor conversation that shares data or plans — the **NDA** moment (IOCL and the bank/gateway conversations included).
- **Yearly**, and whenever the lawyer sends a draft: read the draft against the checklist; every unticked box is a question back to the lawyer.

## How to fill (rules)

1. **Never self-draft.** The lawyer writes each master once; you reuse instances.
2. **Employees sign in order**: offer letter → appointment letter (statutory format — **VERIFY LIVE** the prescribed format and the Chhattisgarh rules with the labour consultant) → employment agreement. Nobody starts work before the agreement is signed.
3. **Contractors sign before they start**: agreement with IP assignment, PAN on file before the first payment, TDS section noted on the engagement so the CA deducts right ([[06-money-rails-and-finance-operations|lesson 06]] §7).
4. **E-sign everything** through the tool chosen in [[07-tools-and-it-foundation|lesson 07]]; wet ink only where the excluded-documents list forces it. Signed PDFs plus the audit certificate go to Drive and the binder row in [[T02-company-binder-checklist|T02]].
5. **One master per document type, versioned in the vault** next to this note; when the lawyer updates a master, new signings use the new version.

## Template

**A. Offer letter essentials**

- [ ] Role title, reporting line, location (Raipur / field / remote terms)
- [ ] CTC with a structure annexure — **basic ≥ 50% of "wages"** (VERIFY LIVE with the CA/labour consultant)
- [ ] Start date; offer expiry date (7–10 days keeps pipelines honest)
- [ ] Probation length, notice during and after probation
- [ ] Conditions: document verification, background-check consent, signing the agreement on day 1
- [ ] Who signs for the company (per [[T22-delegation-of-authority-matrix|T22]])

**B. Appointment letter / employment agreement clauses (India)**

- [ ] Designation, duties, hours and leave (CG S&E rules where applicable — [[09-people-operations|lesson 09]] §5)
- [ ] Wage structure restated with the 50% rule; deductions listed
- [ ] Probation, confirmation, notice period both ways
- [ ] Confidentiality (survives exit)
- [ ] **IP assignment** — present-assignment wording covering past and future work
- [ ] Non-solicit to the extent enforceable (the lawyer's call)
- [ ] References to the POSH policy and code of conduct ([Ahlawat Associates — POSH compliance](https://www.ahlawatassociates.com/blog/posh-and-maternity-compliance-for-companies)) and the handbook ([[T27-policy-handbook-toc|T27]])
- [ ] Background-check consent; data-handling notice
- [ ] Governing law and **Raipur jurisdiction**

**C. Contractor agreement clauses**

- [ ] Scope and deliverables, dated; fees and payment schedule
- [ ] **IP assignment in writing** (Copyright Act s.17 — the contractor owns it otherwise)
- [ ] GST status (registered contractors invoice with 18% GST); **TDS section noted**: 194J 10% professional / 2% technical above ₹50,000 p.a., or 194C 1% individual / 2% others above ₹30,000 single / ₹1,00,000 aggregate; 20% without PAN — sections renumbered under the ITA 2025, **VERIFY LIVE** with the CA ([TDSMAN — TDS chart FY 2026-27](https://blog.tdsman.com/2026/03/tds-tcs-rate-chart-fy-2026-27/))
- [ ] Confidentiality; no use of company data after exit
- [ ] Termination (notice, kill-fee treatment of completed milestones)
- [ ] **No employment relationship** stated; contractor's own taxes and insurance

**D. NDA essentials**

- [ ] Mutual or one-way, stated; definition of confidential information with carve-outs (already public, independently developed, legally compelled)
- [ ] Term (2–3 years typical); return/destroy on request
- [ ] No licence granted; no obligation to transact
- [ ] Jurisdiction (Raipur)

**E. What goes to the lawyer vs what you reuse**

| Situation                                                 | Lawyer          | Reuse the master      |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | --------------------- |
| A new _kind_ of paper (first MSA, first DPA, ESOP scheme) | Yes — drafts it | —                     |
| A standard instance (one more contractor, one more NDA)   | —               | Yes — fill and e-sign |
| A counterparty's paper, or edits to your master           | Yes — reviews   | —                     |
| Anything touching equity, land, or a personal guarantee   | Always          | Never                 |

**F. E-sign flow**

- [ ] Verify the document is not on the excluded list — wills, powers of attorney, trusts, negotiable instruments other than cheques, immovable-property conveyances ([Leegality — the law around Aadhaar eSign](https://www.leegality.com/blog/law-around-aadhaar-esign))
- [ ] Send via the chosen tool (Aadhaar eSign is a notified method under the IT Act; per-signature costs roughly ₹10–40 — **VERIFY LIVE**, [itforsme — Zoho Sign India](https://www.itforsme.in/pricing/zoho-sign-india/))
- [ ] Stamp duty handled separately via e-stamp where the lawyer says it applies (**VERIFY LIVE** the CG treatment)
- [ ] Signed PDF + audit certificate filed in Drive; binder row updated

## VSYST example (illustrative)

A freelance designer is engaged for the dealer-app onboarding screens. Before the first file is shared: checklist C is run — scope ("six screens, source files included"), fee, IP assignment covering _past and future_ work (she reworked two screens informally last month), confidentiality, termination; PAN collected; the engagement notes "TDS u/s 194J — professional, 10% — CA to confirm section and code"; the agreement goes out on the e-sign tool the same afternoon and the signed PDF lands in Drive › 02-Legal with a T02 row. Elapsed time: under an hour, because the master existed. The counter-example is lesson 05's warning: any past freelancer without a signed assignment is an open IP gap — a [[T15-risk-register|T15]] row until the deed is signed.
