# T16 — Compliance Calendar

_Toolkit · fills exercise 10.4 in [[05-legal-and-governance-foundation|05 — Legal and Governance Foundation]] and the full build in [[13-compliance-calendar-risk-and-insurance|13 — Compliance Calendar, Risk and Insurance]] · Owner: COO (the calendar); CA/CS file most rows · Cadence: five-minute "due in 45 days" check weekly; the whole calendar re-confirmed with the CA every April · Workbook tab: `Compliance Calendar` in [vsyst-coo-workbook.xlsx](vsyst-coo-workbook.xlsx)._

## Purpose

An Indian Pvt Ltd's year is homework with deadlines: GST returns, TDS deposits, advance tax, ROC filings, the AGM, labour dues, data-protection obligations, renewals. Misses are priced — AOC-4/MGT-7A run **₹100/day uncapped**, and three unfiled years disqualify directors for five ([ClearTax Advisors — MCA compliance 2026](https://cleartaxadvisors.in/mca-compliance-for-private-limited-company-2026/); [Razorpay Rize — ROC compliance calendar](https://razorpay.com/rize/blogs/roc-compliance-calendar/)). "The CA handles it" is an intention; **a row with an item, a form, a typical due date, an owner and a status is a mechanism** ([[05-legal-and-governance-foundation|lesson 05]]). One warning governs the whole template: **every date below is "typical" for a 31 March year-end and is VERIFY LIVE with the CA/CS** — thresholds, forms and dates move every Budget and often mid-year.

## When to use

- **Week 1 of the seat:** governance-only v0 from lesson 05 §8. **Months 2–3:** the full merge (tax + labour + data + renewals) in [[13-compliance-calendar-risk-and-insurance|lesson 13]].
- **Weekly:** a five-minute "what is due in the next 45 days" check in the ops meeting ([[T03-weekly-ops-meeting-agenda|T03]]).
- **Every April: the whole calendar is re-confirmed with the CA/CS, in writing.** This rule outranks every date on the sheet.
- **The day a trigger fires** (first employee → payroll rows; 10 employees → ESI/POSH IC/S&E; 20 → PF/bonus; GST registration → return rows): add the rows immediately, per [[09-people-operations|lesson 09]] §5.

## How to fill (rules)

1. **One owner per row** — even where the CA/CS files, an internal name chases and stores the acknowledgement/SRN.
2. **Status discipline**: Not started / In progress / Done (+ done date). A red row inside 45 days of due is automatically a top-3 issue in the ops meeting.
3. **Typical dates only, each carrying its VERIFY note** — what to confirm, with whom. The calendar tells you what to ask, never the answer.
4. **Event-driven items get rows the day the event happens**: share allotment → certificates in 60 days; ESOP resolution → MGT-14 in 30 days; director change → forms, bank mandate, registers (lesson 05 §8).
5. Dated items from the binder and the vendor register (DSC expiries, insurance, domains, store memberships) are **copied in, not duplicated** — this calendar is the one place dates live.

**The weekly/monthly ops-compliance mini-checklist**

- [ ] Weekly (ops meeting, 5 min): anything due in 45 days? Any row red? Owner named for each
- [ ] Monthly: TDS deposited by the 7th confirmed against the challan; GST filings confirmed with the CA; close done; new triggers fired (headcount, turnover, registrations)?
- [ ] Monthly: acknowledgements/SRNs for anything filed stored in the binder ([[T02-company-binder-checklist|T02]])

## VSYST example (illustrative)

October, walked: the AGM happened 30 Sep, so three clocks are already running — ADT-1 by ~15 Oct (CS), AOC-4 by ~30 Oct (CS, from the CA's audited accounts), MGT-7A by ~29 Nov (CS). Alongside: ITR-6 and the TDS Q2 return by 31 Oct (CA), MSME-1 by 31 Oct only if micro/small dues sat unpaid past 45 days (COO supplies the vendor list from [[T14-vendor-and-tool-register|T14]]), and the standing monthly block. The weekly check on 6 Oct shows five rows inside 45 days, two still "Not started" — both become named to-dos in that morning's ops meeting. Nothing in this paragraph required remembering anything.
