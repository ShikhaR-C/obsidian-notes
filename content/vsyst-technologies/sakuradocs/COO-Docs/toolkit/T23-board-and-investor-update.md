# T23 — Board and Investor Update

_Toolkit · fills §9 of [[19-planning-okrs-and-the-quarterly-rhythm|19 — Planning, OKRs and the Quarterly Rhythm]]; sits in the monthly ring of [[08-the-operating-cadence|08 — The Operating Cadence]] · Owner: the COO drafts, the CEO sends · Cadence: monthly, by a fixed date once the close pack lands · No workbook tab — it reads the `KPI Scorecard`, `Runway & 13-Week Cash` and `Budget vs Actual` tabs of [vsyst-coo-workbook.xlsx](vsyst-coo-workbook.xlsx)._

## Purpose

One page a month that says what happened, in numbers, to someone outside the room. VSYST is bootstrapped: there is no board demanding a deck and no investor asking for numbers by the 10th. **Write it anyway** — it costs about forty minutes once the scorecard, the budget-vs-actual and the plan doc exist, and it buys four things: the discipline of explaining a number in prose, outside eyes on what you have stared past, an archive of what you believed and when, and fundraise readiness that cannot be manufactured later ([[19-planning-okrs-and-the-quarterly-rhythm|lesson 19]] §9).

The same document wears three hats over time. Today it is the **mentor and CA update**. When a board exists it becomes the highlights page of the board pack. And the day you raise — the Startup India Seed Fund route, an angel, a strategic — twelve honest updates written _before_ anyone asked are the traction narrative ([[finance/08-phase-8-fundraising-and-cap-table|Finance Phase 8]]; the road there is [[finance/10-phase-10-pre-revenue-to-profitable|Finance Phase 10]]).

## When to use

- **Monthly, by a fixed date** — the 10th works, because the close pack has landed by then ([[08-the-operating-cadence|lesson 08]] §6). A monthly artefact without a date drifts to quarterly and then to never.
- **Before any board meeting**, expanded into a pack and circulated **one to two weeks ahead**, never in the room ([BoardCloud — how to create a board pack, 2026](https://boardcloud.us/news/posts/how-to-create-a-board-pack-complete-2026-guide/); [Boardable — board packs](https://boardable.com/resources/board-packs-for-board-meetings/)).
- **At the start of the quarterly planning half-day** — three updates read end to end is the most efficient quarter review there is, and the misses column usually names next quarter's rocks before anyone proposes one.
- **When a fundraise starts** — you assemble, you do not write.

## How to fill (rules)

1. **One page, one screen.** An update nobody finishes is a file, not a communication. The YC-shaped monthly investor update is about two pages at most; the board-deck literature is equally blunt that the opening view is Wins / Misses / Asks ([CFO Advisors — investor-ready board deck, 2026](https://cfoadvisors.com/blog/investor-ready-board-deck-template-2026)).
2. **Numbers before narrative.** The KPI block comes before any prose, straight from the scorecard — the same law as the weekly review ([[T28-weekly-business-review|T28]]).
3. **Misses before wins**, or at minimum in the same size type. An update that reports only wins trains its readers to stop reading and trains you to stop noticing.
4. **Never a number the scorecard doesn't carry.** If it matters enough to report monthly, it is a row ([[T05-kpi-scorecard|T05]]).
5. **Cash and runway every single month**, even when nothing changed — especially then. Runway against the red line, and the month's burn against plan ([[T20-budget-vs-actual-and-cash-forecast|T20]]).
6. **Every update carries an ask**, even a small one: an introduction to a transporter fleet, a view on a contract clause, a name for a support hire. The ask is what converts a broadcast into a relationship.
7. **Keep the rows identical month to month.** Changing what you report is how trends disappear; new rows join at quarterly planning, with the reason stated.
8. **Same recipients, same day, archived in the vault** at `updates/YYYY-MM.md`. The archive is the point as much as the send.

## Template

```
# <Company> — monthly update, <Month YYYY>        sent <date> by <CEO>
To: <CA, mentor, advisor, investors>          Prepared by: COO

## TL;DR (three lines, no more)
<the month in one sentence> · <the one number that moved> · <the one ask>

## KPIs vs plan
| Metric | This month | Last month | Plan | Comment (one line) |

## Cash and runway
Cash: ₹__   Burn: ₹__ (plan ₹__)   Runway: __ months (red line __)
13-week trough: ₹__ in week __. Collections/DSO: __

## Wins        (3 max — what actually happened, with names and numbers)
## Misses      (3 max — what didn't, why, and what changes because of it)
## Asks        (specific, addressed to someone, with a date)

## Hiring      open roles, triggers fired, offers out, anyone who left
## Product     what shipped, what's next, what got cut and why
## Risks       what changed on the risk register, top 2 only
## Next month  the three things that must be true by the next update
```

## When a real board exists — growing the update into a pack

The one-pager does not get replaced; it becomes the **highlights page** at the front of a pack. The standard pack is agenda, prior minutes, a one-to-two-page highlights document, a candid CEO report, the financials, and any proposals requiring a decision, circulated one to two weeks ahead so the meeting can discuss rather than present; operating decks run roughly eighteen to twenty-six slides across ten sections, opening on Wins / Misses / Asks (CFO Advisors, 2026; BoardCloud, 2026; Boardable). Two VSYST-specific attachments join it as soon as they exist: the compliance status line from [[T16-compliance-calendar|T16]] (anything filed late is a board matter) and the resolutions the board is being asked to pass — including the [[T22-delegation-of-authority-matrix|T22]] ratification and any headcount above the plan.

## VSYST example (illustrative)

Numbers invented; the shape is the deliverable.

> **TL;DR.** Fourteen active dealer tenants, up two. Activation inside 14 days slipped to 50% and that is this month's problem. Ask: two introductions to transporters running 10+ vehicles in Durg.
>
> **KPIs vs plan.** Active tenants 14 (plan 16) · activated in 14 days 2 of 4 (plan 3 of 4) · invoices through DZZLO 212 (up 9%) · rate-confirmation rate 61% (plan 75%) · FRT median 26 min (plan ≤ 30) · tickets/tenant/week 1.4 (flat).
>
> **Cash and runway.** Burn ₹— against plan ₹—; runway — months against a — month red line; nothing crossed. **VERIFY LIVE** against the close pack before sending.
>
> **Wins.** Support moved onto one number and one queue — every dealer message is now a ticket. First dealer-onboarding SOP run end to end by someone other than the founder. Compliance calendar built with the CA; nothing missed.
>
> **Misses.** Remote-district onboarding activated one of three; counter onboarding activated three of three — the SOP now requires a follow-up visit inside seven days. The OTP vendor failed for forty minutes during the evening window with no alert of its own; postmortem done, alert being built.
>
> **Asks.** Two transporter introductions in Durg (by month end). A view on the WhatsApp Business API decision — utility messages are cheap, marketing messages are not, and we would rather defer.
>
> **Hiring.** Support/Ops Associate trigger has not fired (founder support hours 7/week against a 10-hour trigger). No offers out.
>
> **Product.** Shipped: statement PDF regeneration fix, invite-flow error messages. Next: OTP vendor failure alert. Cut: dealer logo on invoices — asked for by two dealers, logged, not scheduled.
>
> **Risks.** Single SMS vendor re-scored up after the outage; mitigation is a second route, owner and date set.
>
> **Next month.** Activation back to 3 of 4 · the alert live · the first quarterly planning half-day held.
