# T19 — Quarterly Plan and Review

_Toolkit · fills the quarterly ritual of [[08-the-operating-cadence|08 — The Operating Cadence]] §7 and the method of [[19-planning-okrs-and-the-quarterly-rhythm|19 — Planning, OKRs and the Quarterly Rhythm]] · Owner: COO prepares and facilitates; the CEO decides trade-offs · Cadence: half a day, last week of every quarter, booked months ahead · Workbook tab: `OKR Tracker` in [vsyst-coo-workbook.xlsx](vsyst-coo-workbook.xlsx) holds scores and next-quarter OKRs._

## Purpose

The quarter is where strategy gets reset: the weekly meeting solves this week, the monthly review reallocates money, and the quarterly session decides **what the next 90 days are for**. The shape is EOS's meeting pulse — a weekly beat paired with a quarterly session (and a longer annual one) that sets the next quarter's **Rocks: the 3–7 priorities that must get done in the next 90 days** ([EOS — Meeting Pulse](https://www.eosworldwide.com/meeting-pulse); [Growth Institute — the Rockefeller Habits checklist](https://blog.growthinstitute.com/scale-up-blueprint/10-rockefeller-habits-checklist)). At VSYST's size the whole thing is **half a day**, and its two halves are strict: **review the quarter honestly before planning the next one.** OKR scoring keeps the review honest — score each key result 0.0–1.0, where landing around 0.7 on a stretch target is success, not failure ([Measure What Matters — notes](https://grahammann.net/book-notes/measure-what-matters-by-john-doerr); [[T06-okr-planning-sheet|T06]] holds the mechanics).

## When to use

- **Last week of every quarter**, half a day, booked months ahead ([[08-the-operating-cadence|lesson 08]] §7) — the slot that never survives being "found later".
- **The same week carries the three audits** — access review, vendor and tool audit ([[T14-vendor-and-tool-register|T14]]), risk register review ([[T15-risk-register|T15]]) — so they never need their own willpower.
- A **mid-quarter reset** happens only when reality breaks the plan (a partner lands, a founder is out for a month); it is rare, and it is a logged decision ([[T09-decision-log-and-adr|T09]]), not a drift.

## How to fill (rules)

1. **Prep goes out 48 hours ahead, written**: the 13-week scorecard trends ([[T05-kpi-scorecard|T05]]), OKR scores drafted by their owners, the quarter's budget-vs-actual ([[T20-budget-vs-actual-and-cash-forecast|T20]]), the top support/pipeline themes. The room reads nothing for the first time.
2. **Review before plan — no exceptions.** Planning before reviewing is how last quarter's fictions become next quarter's assumptions.
3. **3–5 rocks, one owner each.** Six rocks is a wish list. Each rock states its measurable finish line; big ones get a [[T18-project-charter|T18]] mini-charter.
4. **Score OKRs 0.0–1.0 and say why in one line each.** Not tied to pay at this stage ([[19-planning-okrs-and-the-quarterly-rhythm|lesson 19]]).
5. **Every rock leaves the room with its first two weeks of to-dos** — a rock without a first step is a poster.
6. **Decisions needed are listed and decided in the room** (or explicitly parked with a date), then logged.
7. **The plan doc is filed in the vault and the `OKR Tracker` tab updated before anyone leaves.** The next Monday's ops meeting reads the new rocks as its Rocks segment.

## Template

**The half-day agenda** (four hours; COO facilitates, a scribe writes):

| Time | Segment              | What happens                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---- | -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0:00 | Open                 | Rules restated: review before plan, phones away, decisions logged                                                                                                                |
| 0:10 | Scorecard review     | The 13-week trends walked; each red/amber row gets one honest sentence                                                                                                           |
| 0:40 | OKR scoring          | Owners present scores (0.0–1.0) with a one-line why each; no relitigating mid-quarter history                                                                                    |
| 1:10 | Retro                | Wins / misses / learnings (format below)                                                                                                                                         |
| 1:40 | Break                | 15 minutes                                                                                                                                                                       |
| 1:55 | Next quarter's rocks | Everyone proposes; argue down to **3–5**; one owner each; finish lines written as KRs into [[T06-okr-planning-sheet\|T06]]                                                       |
| 2:55 | Money and people     | Budget changes into [[T20-budget-vs-actual-and-cash-forecast\|T20]]'s categories; headcount plan changes ([[T21-headcount-plan\|T21]]); re-base the 13-week forecast assumptions |
| 3:20 | Risks and decisions  | [[T15-risk-register\|T15]] top rows re-read; "decisions needed" list decided or parked with dates                                                                                |
| 3:40 | Write and cascade    | Plan doc finished in the room; first-two-weeks to-dos per rock; cascading messages agreed (team, dealers, partners)                                                              |
| 4:00 | Close                | Meeting rated; next quarter's session confirmed in the calendar                                                                                                                  |

**The plan doc** (one vault note per quarter, `quarters/QN-YYYY.md`):

```
# QN YYYY — plan and review                       written <date>
## Last quarter
Scorecard summary   3 lines: what trended up, down, flat — with numbers.
OKR scores          Each objective: KRs, score 0.0–1.0, one-line why.
Wins               3–5, with the mechanism that produced each.
Misses             3–5, without blame — cause, not culprit.
Learnings          What we now know that we didn't 90 days ago.
## Next quarter
Rocks / OKRs       3–5, each: owner · finish line (KR) · first two weeks' to-dos.
Budget changes     By T20 category, with the one-line reason.
Headcount changes  Roles opened/closed/deferred, triggers (T21).
Risks              Anything ≥ amber after today's re-score.
Decisions needed   Decided today: … · Parked until <date>: …
Cascade            Who is told what, by whom, by when.
```

**Retro format** — three columns, everyone contributes before anyone discusses: **Wins** (what worked — keep doing it, name the mechanism), **Misses** (what didn't — cause, not culprit), **Learnings** (what changes a template, an SOP or a number). Every learning lands as an edit to an artefact the same week, or it evaporates.

## VSYST example (illustrative)

Q3 review, sketched: activation rate trended up after the onboarding SOP shipped (win — mechanism named); "10 paying pilots" scored 0.4 — pricing remained undecided, so the KR was mis-set (learning: KRs must not depend on decisions the quarter doesn't own); support FRT stayed green all quarter (win). Next quarter's rocks: (1) subscription billing rails decision-ready — owner CEO, charter required; (2) 15 activated tenants in the first district — owner domain director; (3) compliance calendar fully confirmed with the CA and every red cleared — owner COO. Budget: SaaS/tools line up by the helpdesk hosting; headcount: Support/Ops Associate trigger re-affirmed, not yet fired. Two decisions parked with dates; the tab updated before lunch.
