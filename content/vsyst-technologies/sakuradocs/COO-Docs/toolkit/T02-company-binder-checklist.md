# T02 — Company Binder Checklist

_Toolkit · fills the exercises in [[05-legal-and-governance-foundation|05 — Legal and governance foundation]] · Owner: COO (with the CA/CS) · Cadence: build in Days 1–7, tick monthly, renew per item · Workbook tab: dated items also go into `Compliance Calendar` in [the workbook](vsyst-coo-workbook.xlsx); the binder itself is this note plus one Drive folder and one physical file._

## Purpose

The **company binder** is the single list of every document, registration and credential that proves VSYST exists, may trade, may hire, may collect money and owns its product. The bank, the payment gateway, DPIIT, the app stores, an insurer, IOCL and eventually an investor each ask for a subset of it; building it once turns every such request into a ten-minute job — and it is the first thing a new COO reads.

## When to use

- Days 1–7 of the seat; before opening the current account or a payment-gateway KYC.
- Every month: five minutes to tick "have?" and move renewal dates.
- Whenever a document is issued (add a row) or the CA/CS asks for one — if it took more than ten minutes to find, the binder is wrong.

## How to fill (rules)

1. One row per item. **Have?** = Yes / No / N/A / In progress. **Where stored** = a link (Drive folder path) plus, for physical items, the cupboard — minutes books, share certificates and DSC tokens are physical.
2. **Owner** is a person; **Renewal / next action** is a date. Every date is copied to the `Compliance Calendar` tab and [[T16-compliance-calendar|T16]].
3. Every item marked **VERIFY LIVE** is confirmed with the CS/CA — the list tells you what to ask, not the answer.
4. Scan every paper; originals live in one folder at the registered office. DSC tokens stay with the director whose DSC it is, never with the CA.
5. **Never write a credential here.** Write only the vault name in the password manager where it lives.
