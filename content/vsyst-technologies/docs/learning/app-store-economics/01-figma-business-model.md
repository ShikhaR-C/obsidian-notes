# 1. Figma's Business Model

## What Figma Is

**Figma** is a collaborative, browser-first design platform (founded 2012 by Dylan Field & Evan Wallace, launched 2016). The core editor runs in the **browser** — a C++ rendering engine compiled to WebAssembly — so a design file is just a URL. That one architectural bet drives the whole business model: zero-install distribution, real-time multiplayer, and **no app-store gatekeeper between Figma and its customers**.

Product suite (all bundled into seats): **Figma Design**, **Dev Mode**, **FigJam** (whiteboarding), **Slides**, and the 2025 AI/site products (**Make**, **Sites**, **Buzz**, **Draw**).

---

## The Revenue Model: Freemium + Per-Editor Seats

Figma is **B2B SaaS sold per seat, per month** — but only _editors_ pay. **Viewing is free, forever, for unlimited people.**

### Seat prices (2026, billed annually)

| Seat type       | Professional | Organization | Enterprise | What it unlocks                                |
| --------------- | ------------ | ------------ | ---------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Full seat**   | $16/mo       | $55/mo       | $90/mo     | Design, prototyping, Dev Mode, AI — everything |
| **Dev seat**    | $12/mo       | ~$25/mo      | ~$35/mo    | Inspect designs as code (Dev Mode), no editing |
| **Collab seat** | $3/mo        | ~$5/mo       | ~$5/mo     | View + comment + full FigJam & Slides          |
| **View seat**   | Free         | Free         | Free       | View, comment                                  |

- Monthly billing exists only on Professional and costs ~25% more ($20 Full / $15 Dev / $5 Collab).
- **Starter tier is free** (limited files/projects) — genuinely usable, the top of the funnel.
- Organization/Enterprise add SSO, design-system governance, admin controls — sold on annual contracts.

### Why free viewers are the growth engine (PLG)

1. A designer adopts free Starter → sends a Figma **link** to engineers/PMs.
2. Recipients view free — no install, no license, no friction. About **two-thirds of Figma's users are not designers**.
3. The product spreads bottom-up inside the company until IT standardizes on it → sales converts that into Organization/Enterprise contracts ("land and expand").
4. Expansion then compounds: more seats, more products on the same account.

The metric that proves it: **Net Dollar Retention 136%** (Dec 31, 2025) — existing customers spend 36% more each year even before any new customer is signed.

## Key Takeaways

- **Charge where the value accrues** (people who produce designs), give the network away free (people who consume them).
- **Web-first = distribution without a toll booth** — no App Store / Play Store between Figma and revenue (see [[02-how-figma-avoids-store-tax]]).
- Expansion revenue (NDR) matters more than new logos at scale.
