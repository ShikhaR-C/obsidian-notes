### Gemini block (paste this whole block into a fresh chat)

```
CONTEXT
Audience: Small-fleet transport owners (5–50 trucks), 30–50, Tier-2/3 India; WhatsApp-native, Hindi-first; burned by paper-slip (parchi) disputes and driver fuel fraud; wary of any step that slows a truck down.
Platform: WhatsApp forward + Instagram Reels — 9:16 vertical, max 30 seconds, hook in the first 2 seconds.
Goal/CTA: Defuse the "why the OTP step / my driver won't cooperate" objection; end on "Book a demo on WhatsApp".
Key message: The 1-second driver OTP is your proof of delivery — no OTP, no invoice, no fake litres.
Brand feel: Trustworthy, modern, warm; not flashy or corporate.
Duration: 30 seconds → 4 shots of ~8 seconds each.

PRODUCT TRUTH (use only these facts; do not invent claims or numbers)
1. Dealer, fleet owner and driver don't fully trust each other; the OTP verified at the fill/delivery point is the one moment all three agree that a specific quantity on a specific order actually changed hands.
2. The attendant cannot start the fill until the driver's OTP — an SMS to his registered phone — is verified. Vehicle number alone is not identity: vehicles and driver numbers are shared across companies.
3. The invoice is generated only after OTP confirmation, so every invoice corresponds to a real, acknowledged delivery — nobody can bill for fuel that was never delivered.
4. Every litre is logged digitally — timestamped, geo-tagged, OTP-signed — replacing the disputed paper parchi; dealer and fleet owner see the same ledger, ending the 10–15-day month-end reconciliation crunch.
5. Fuel fraud and leakage (short-fills, slip collusion, diversion) cost a typical small fleet 5–12% of its diesel spend; ~$6.5B per year nationally.
6. The OTP is deliberately low-friction: works on any phone, no app install, no typing, takes about one second — that is the answer to "my driver won't cooperate".

You are my Veo 3.1 prompt writer. Using the CONTEXT and PRODUCT TRUTH above, expand each rough shot note below into a finished Veo prompt. Each prompt must include all seven ingredients — subject, action, scene, camera, style, lighting/mood, audio — in 3–6 sentences (100–150 words). Present tense. Add "no subtitles" to any shot with dialogue. Keep the brand feel identical across all shots. Output one prompt per shot, numbered.

Rough shots:
1. Hook — month-end desk buried in paper parchis; two slips disagree (40 L filled, 45 L billed); the fleet owner knows he's leaking money
2. Value — pump fill point: nozzle idle until the driver's SMS OTP is verified in the app; green tick, and only then does the diesel flow
3. Proof — owner's phone: the OTP-signed delivery turns into the invoice; timestamped, geo-tagged litres both sides agree on; on-screen "recover 5–12% of diesel spend"
4. CTA — owner to camera says "No OTP, no invoice — every litre provably delivered", then an end-card: Book a demo on WhatsApp
```
