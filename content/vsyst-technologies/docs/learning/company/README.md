## What DZZLO OMS is

DZZLO OMS is a mobile-first, cloud, multi-tenant Order Management System purpose-built for Indian fuel retail — petrol pump dealers, lubricant distributors, and bulk-diesel doorstep operators. It replaces the incumbent stack of Tally, paper Daily Sales Reports (DSR), manual tank-dip logs, and scattered WhatsApp messages with a single vertical product that handles DIP tank readings, vehicle-level fuel tracking, GST/TCS/TDS automation, credit ledgers, density-variance reconciliation, and WhatsApp-native customer billing. See [00_OVERVIEW.md](./00_OVERVIEW.md) for the full product, user, feature, technology, and geography breakdown.

## The 10-commandment quick reference

Ten things every DZZLO founder must internalize, distilled across the full playbook:

1. **Problem-first, distribution-first.** Product is the mechanism, not the strategy.
2. **Sales-assisted, product-retained.** Pure product-led growth will not work for Indian fuel SME; equally, sales without a sticky product will not retain.
3. **Dealer = powered-on side. Customer = WhatsApp-first, zero-install.** The dealer carries the login; the customer gets the receipt.
4. **Single-player value.** The product must work for the dealer even if zero customers join the customer-side flow. Never depend on two-sided adoption for the core loop.
5. **Atomic network first.** Saturate one district — 60–80% penetration in a 60 km radius — before expanding.
6. **Monetize via fintech.** Subscription is the hook; payment take-rate and invoice-discounting revenue share are the business.
7. **First 100 customers = founder-led sales.** They are R&D, not revenue. The founder visits every one personally.
8. **Warm channels beat cold by 10x.** Referrals > OMC Territory Manager > association sponsorship > field sales > digital.
9. **Fear of loss + social proof > features.** Owners buy protection, not dashboards. Every top-of-funnel asset leads with a rupee number of loss avoided.
10. **Build for the second-generation owner.** The son influences; the father signs. Marketing choreographs both.

## Cadence recommendations

- **Weekly**
  - Five customer interviews (founder-led, voice notes transcribed)
  - Sean Ellis survey review on activated accounts
  - Cohort retention curve (weekly cohorts for the first 6 months)
  - Pipeline velocity scan — lead → demo, demo → paid

- **Monthly**
  - NPS survey to active accounts
  - Unit economics review — CAC by channel, LTV, payback months
  - Churn post-mortem — what went wrong, what triggered the cancellation
  - Pricing test — annual vs monthly, tier upgrade prompts

- **Quarterly**
  - Positioning refresh — re-run the messaging framework against live customers
  - Competitive teardown — Petrosoft, Vyapar, Tally-verticals, new entrants
  - Fundraise readiness check — metrics vs benchmark, deck vs narrative, cap table vs plan
