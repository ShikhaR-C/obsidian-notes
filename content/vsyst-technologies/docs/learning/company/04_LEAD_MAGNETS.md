# 04 — Lead Magnets

A founder's reference for designing, building, and operating lead magnets for DZZLO OMS, the B2B SaaS for Indian fuel distributors and petrol pump dealers.

---

## 1. What a lead magnet is and why it matters

A lead magnet is a transactional value exchange. The prospect hands over contact information (email, phone, GSTIN, company name) and in return receives a standalone piece of value — a tool, template, report, or diagnostic — that stands on its own merit. The magnet is not a sales brochure wearing a PDF costume; it must solve one discrete problem well enough that the prospect would be happy to pay for it.

The 2026 content environment has two structural shifts founders need to internalize:

- **Interactive beats static.** 91% of B2B buyers now prefer interactive over static content, and AI-driven quizzes/assessments average a 78% lift in conversion over the static baseline ([Amraandelma 2026](https://www.amraandelma.com/lead-magnet-conversion-statistics/)).
- **Libraries beat whitepapers.** 90% of high-growth SaaS companies now prioritize interactive resource libraries over one-off whitepapers ([Prospeo 2026](https://prospeo.io/s/lead-magnet-examples)).

For DZZLO's audience — petrol pump owners and fuel distributors, mostly operating 1-5 outlets in Tier-2 and Tier-3 India — the implication is sharper still. Dealers are operationally busy, don't read long-form English content, and respond to two things: a concrete number ("you are losing ₹60,000/month") and a concrete tool (a calculator that tells them that number). Every DZZLO lead magnet should pass the "Rajesh test": would a 45-year-old HPCL dealer in Ahmedabad, on a WhatsApp forward at 9 PM, open it, finish it, and feel he learned something useful?

The lead magnet is also the cheapest entry point into the CAC engine. A well-built calculator produced once can run for two years and generate thousands of leads at near-zero marginal cost — the unit economics are superior to paid ads at DZZLO's price point (₹2,500/month ARPA).

## 2. Funnel stages primer

Three stages, each calling for different magnet design ([Spinta Digital](https://spintadigital.com/blog/tofu-mofu-bofu-guide/)):

- **TOFU — Top of Funnel (Awareness).** Prospect knows a pain but hasn't named it. Magnets should educate, quantify pain, or give a quick win. Checklists, benchmark reports, diagnostic quizzes, free utilities. Low commitment, broad appeal.
- **MOFU — Middle of Funnel (Consideration).** Prospect is weighing solutions. Magnets should prove DZZLO can solve their specific flavor of pain. Calculators, templates, mini-courses, webinars.
- **BOFU — Bottom of Funnel (Decision).** Prospect is choosing a vendor. Magnets should de-risk the purchase. Case studies, free audits, ROI tools with pricing, trials.

A mature funnel has at least one magnet at each stage. A startup should start with one great MOFU magnet (highest intent-per-lead) and then backfill TOFU for volume.

### Guidance per type

**Calculators / ROI tools.** Best single magnet type for B2B SaaS targeting SMBs. Inputs are personal, outputs feel personal, and the number stays in the prospect's head. Do not hide behind a gate before the prospect sees partial output — let them feel the shape of the answer first, then ask for email to unlock the full PDF.

**Diagnostic quizzes.** Ideal for mid-funnel qualification. Avoid BuzzFeed-style quizzes; build 15-25 serious questions mapped to a scorecard and a remediation plan. Do not make the quiz so long that drop-off kills CVR.

**Checklists.** The workhorse. A one-page printable checklist a dealer can tape above the DU console is high-perceived-value for low effort. Do not pad; a 4-page checklist is worse than a 1-pager.

**Templates.** Best when the output is an artefact the dealer would otherwise spend a day making (e.g., a GST-ready invoice template). Do not ship a blank shell — pre-fill with realistic dummy data.

**Ebooks / whitepapers.** Thought-leadership vehicle, not conversion vehicle, in 2026. Useful for SEO and authority but expect 3-5% CVR. Do not rely on this as your primary magnet; it is a secondary asset.

**Webinars.** High MQL-to-SQL because attendees self-select for intent. Do not skip the replay distribution — 60% of value comes from the async watch. Keep live webinars under 45 minutes.

**Case studies.** Double-duty — both a magnet and a sales-team asset. Include the customer's name, photo, outlet location, and a verifiable number. Do not anonymize; named cases outperform anonymized 4x in B2B.

**Swipe files.** Curated collection of examples (e.g., "50 cold email templates", "30 dealer margin dashboards"). Works when the audience is practitioners. Do not pretend curation is creation — credit sources.

**Mini-courses / email courses.** 5-14 day drip sequence delivered by email. Ideal for nurturing and building trust in a long sales cycle. Do not treat this as a product demo in slices; deliver teach first, sell in the final email.

**Industry reports / benchmarks.** Hardest to build, but highest PR and SEO return. Run once a quarter or year; reuse in press, on LinkedIn, at conferences. Do not publish without unique first-party data.

**Audits / reviews.** A founder or sales rep manually reviews the prospect's setup and sends a 1-page gap report. Extremely high SQL rate. Do not offer at scale before you can deliver one in under 90 minutes.

**Free tools (Chrome extensions, mini-apps, Slack bots).** The Grammarly model — the tool is the funnel. High engineering effort but unbeatable compound retention. Do not start here unless you have engineering capacity to maintain it.

**Directories / marketplaces.** SEO play. A dealer directory or an OMC commission rate tracker ranks on long-tail queries and creates organic inbound. Do not build this without a clear data moat.

**Resource libraries.** Bundle everything above into a single branded library behind one email gate. High CVR once the library has depth. Do not launch empty — minimum 8-10 assets.

## 4. DZZLO-specific lead magnets — 10 concrete ideas

Each designed around a real dealer pain, with a specific distribution plan.

### 1. DIP Chart Calibration Calculator (free, ungated tool)

- **Type:** Calculator / free tool
- **Stage:** TOFU
- **Effort:** M
- **Target persona:** Any petrol pump owner or manager
- **Gated:** Ungated (freemium; email optional for "download as printable PDF")
- **Why it works:** Every underground tank in India needs a calibrated dip chart. Dealers currently pay ₹3,000–₹10,000 to a local fabricator for one. A browser tool that takes tank dimensions (diameter, length, tilt, end-cap geometry) and outputs a dealer-printable PDF eliminates a painful recurring chore — and the dealer remembers where he got it.
- **Distribution:** SEO-optimized for "dip chart calculator petrol pump", WhatsApp share with AIPDA state chapters, LinkedIn post with before/after screenshots, dealer forum seeding.

### 2. Daily Shortage / Evaporation Audit Template

- **Type:** Template (Google Sheet + PDF)
- **Stage:** MOFU
- **Effort:** S
- **Target persona:** Owner, manager, or accountant
- **Gated:** Email-gated
- **Why it works:** Indian dealers lose 0.4-0.75% per tank to evaporation and shortage, and most have no structured way to track it. The template ingests daily dip readings, sales, and receipts and auto-flags abnormal shortage days. Serves as a MOFU bridge — dealers who use the sheet realise they need software to sustain the habit.
- **Distribution:** Gated landing page, lead magnet offer at end of DIP calculator, email footer, dealer WhatsApp groups.

### 3. GST Invoice Template for Petrol/Diesel Dealers

- **Type:** Template
- **Stage:** TOFU
- **Effort:** S
- **Target persona:** Dealer accountant or owner
- **Gated:** Email-gated
- **Why it works:** Petrol/diesel stay under VAT; lubricants, shop items, and convenience products fall under GST. Splitting the invoice correctly is a genuine compliance headache ([ClearTax — GST on petrol](https://cleartax.in/s/gst-on-petrol)). A Tally-compatible + Excel template that handles the split reduces the dealer's CA bill and positions DZZLO as the compliance-literate OMS.
- **Distribution:** SEO ("GST invoice template petrol pump"), CA partner network, association newsletters.

### 4. Dealer Margin Tracker

- **Type:** Calculator + benchmark
- **Stage:** MOFU
- **Effort:** M
- **Target persona:** Dealer-owner
- **Gated:** Email-gated for full benchmark report
- **Why it works:** Dealer commission on petrol/diesel is publicly fixed by OMCs ([PPAC — Dealers/Distributors Commission](https://ppac.gov.in/prices/dealers-distributors-commission-on-petrol-diesel-pds-kerosene-domestic-lpg)). Most dealers don't know exactly what their per-KL margin should be after transport differential. The tool reconciles their monthly payout against the official rate and flags underpayment or differential mismatch.
- **Distribution:** PPAC rate-update hijack on LinkedIn whenever commissions change, SEO, AIPDA/FAIPT partnerships.

### 5. Pump-Digitization ROI Calculator

- **Type:** ROI calculator
- **Stage:** MOFU-BOFU
- **Effort:** M
- **Target persona:** Dealer-owner with growth intent
- **Gated:** Partial output ungated; full report email-gated
- **Why it works:** Inputs are monthly volume, staff count, current shortage %, credit-customer outstanding. Output is monthly ₹ saved, payback months, 3-year NPV. ROI calculators drove 51.3% of enterprise pipeline revenue in 2026 — they work because the prospect pre-commits to the case for buying before sales ever shows up.
- **Distribution:** Primary CTA on homepage, retargeting ads, sales-rep lead-with asset.

### 6. Nozzle Reconciliation Checklist

- **Type:** Printable PDF checklist
- **Stage:** TOFU
- **Effort:** S
- **Target persona:** Shift supervisor or dealer
- **Gated:** Email-gated
- **Why it works:** A one-page, 18-line shift-change reconciliation checklist that a dealer prints once and tapes above each DU. Low barrier, extremely high perceived value because it is tangibly used every day.
- **Distribution:** Part of resource library, bundled with DIP calculator download.

### 7. Free Petrol Pump Compliance Audit

- **Type:** Diagnostic quiz
- **Stage:** MOFU
- **Effort:** M
- **Target persona:** Dealer-owner
- **Gated:** Email + phone to see full gap report
- **Why it works:** 20 questions covering GST invoice compliance, MSDS availability, Form R filings, stock register hygiene, staff PF/ESI registration, weights & measures certification. Auto-generates a gap report scored out of 100. Diagnostic quizzes average 40% CVR — the highest of any magnet type — because the prospect is sunk-cost-committed by question 5.
- **Distribution:** Paid social to dealer lookalikes, organic LinkedIn, sales-team warm-up asset.

### 8. Bulk Diesel Buyer Contract Template

- **Type:** Legal template
- **Stage:** MOFU
- **Effort:** M (needs lawyer sign-off)
- **Target persona:** Dealer selling to fleets, industrial, construction
- **Gated:** Email-gated
- **Why it works:** Dealers selling to bulk buyers (transport fleets, construction sites, factories) often use handshake contracts and get burned on credit and volume disputes. A lawyer-reviewed template covering price, credit period, minimum offtake, dispute resolution, and delivery terms is genuinely useful and signals that DZZLO understands the B2B sales side, not just retail.
- **Distribution:** LinkedIn founder post, bulk-dealer WhatsApp groups, trade-fair handouts.

### 9. State-wise Dealer Benchmark Report

- **Type:** Industry benchmark report
- **Stage:** TOFU-MOFU
- **Effort:** L (recurring quarterly)
- **Target persona:** Dealer-owners, OMC territory managers, journalists
- **Gated:** Email + company + state
- **Why it works:** Quarterly DZZLO-branded report on average daily volume, shortage %, credit-customer %, and margin per state, segmented by throughput tier. Requires DZZLO to have 50+ active dealers first to have real first-party data — but once produced, it is PR-worthy, SEO-gold for "[state] petrol pump benchmarks", and gives sales a reason to call every dealer in the state.
- **Distribution:** Trade press embargo, LinkedIn thread, AIPDA partnership, founder speaking slots.

### 10. "Digitize Your Pump" 2-week email course

- **Type:** Mini-course (email drip)
- **Stage:** MOFU
- **Effort:** M
- **Target persona:** Dealer-owner starting to consider software
- **Gated:** Email opt-in
- **Why it works:** 10-email Hindi/English parallel drip over 14 days. Day 1 — DIP basics; Day 3 — GST for fuel retail; Day 5 — shortage control; Day 7 — staff management; Day 10 — credit-customer discipline; Day 14 — soft CTA with founder video. Nurtures low-intent TOFU leads into MOFU over two weeks without sales touch.
- **Distribution:** Post-opt-in from every other magnet; LinkedIn organic; free-tier retention.

## 5. How to build each lead magnet (process)

### Research phase

- Validate every magnet title with 10 target dealers on WhatsApp before you build. A two-line poll — _"Kaunsa aapke liye useful hoga: (a) DIP chart calculator (b) Shortage audit template?"_ — saves months of wrong-direction work.
- Check what the top 3 Google results for the anchor keyword already offer; if the field is empty, you have a SEO-gold opportunity; if it is crowded, your magnet needs a differentiator (usually India/INR/GST specificity).
- Talk to one CA and one OMC territory manager per vertical magnet — they know the compliance nuance that makes the difference between a useful magnet and a generic one.

### Production stack

- **Templates:** Airtable or Google Sheets for interactive templates; Notion for written templates (public share link, duplicate enabled).
- **Checklists:** Notion or Canva for design; PDF export with DZZLO footer and QR code back to the site.
- **Calculators:** Typeform or Outgrow for simple; custom React widget on the DZZLO domain for complex (better for SEO and retention).
- **Quizzes:** Paperform or Outgrow — both support conditional logic, scorecards, and branded PDFs.
- **Mini-courses:** Write in Notion, deliver via the ESP (Mailmodo or ActiveTrail for India-DPDP compliance); record Loom videos for lessons that need motion.
- **Reports:** Data in Google Sheets; design in Figma; export to PDF and an interactive microsite.

### Distribution

- **Landing page:** Above-the-fold headline with the specific ₹ pain solved; form with only email + phone (GSTIN optional); one testimonial; one mini-FAQ; below-fold embedded calculator/template preview.
- **WhatsApp broadcast:** Short message + link via WhatsApp Business API to opted-in dealers; pre-populated share copy for viral re-share.
- **Founder LinkedIn:** Story-format post introducing the magnet with a before/after screenshot; engagement-bait comments directing to DM.
- **Dealer association partnership:** AIPDA, FAIPT, state bodies — pitch the magnet as a member benefit in exchange for a newsletter feature.
- **OMC rep referrals:** Territory managers who are advocates can forward the magnet to dealers they visit; offer them a small per-qualified-lead kickback if appropriate.

### Nurture

- Always attach a post-opt-in email sequence. [Leadfeeder 2026](https://www.leadfeeder.com) insists nurture is 50% of the lead magnet's value — the magnet captures, the sequence converts.
- Minimum 5-email sequence: welcome + magnet delivery → use-case deep-dive → case study → product intro → CTA.

## 6. Conversion and post-magnet nurture

### The 5-minute rule

Send the first automated email within 5 minutes of opt-in. Attention decays fast and response rates drop 10x after one hour. The magnet should ideally appear in-line in the first email (not behind a second link).

### The 5-7 email sequence

1. **Welcome + delivery** (t+5 min): deliver the asset, one sentence on how to use it, no ask.
2. **Value deep-dive** (day 2): expand on one insight from the magnet — e.g., "why 0.4% shortage is normal and 0.75% is a red flag".
3. **Case study** (day 5): a named dealer, a specific ₹ outcome, a link to a 2-page case study.
4. **Product intro** (day 9): 3-bullet explanation of DZZLO, positioned as "what happens if you want this to run automatically every day".
5. **Soft CTA** (day 14): offer to run a 15-minute walkthrough or a free pump audit.
6. **Hard CTA** (day 21): scarcity framing ("free setup for first 30 Gujarat dealers"), specific time slots.
7. **Breakup** (day 28): "closing your file" — graceful exit that often recovers latent interest.

### WhatsApp follow-up

India SMB channel preference is WhatsApp over email. At t+48 hours, a personal WhatsApp message from the founder or sales rep — _"Namaste Rajesh ji, DZZLO se shortage audit template aapne liya tha — use karne mein help chahiye toh batao"_ — routinely 3-5x the email reply rate on its own.

### Metrics to track

- **Opt-in rate:** landing-page visitors → form submitters. Target 25-40% for a dialled magnet.
- **Open rate:** welcome email. Target 55-70%.
- **CTA click rate:** downstream emails. Target 8-15%.
- **SQL rate:** opt-ins → sales-qualified leads. Target 3-8%.
- **Paid conversion:** opt-ins → paying customers within 90 days. Target 1-3% for TOFU magnets, 5-15% for MOFU/BOFU.

Track each magnet separately in a simple dashboard and kill magnets with <1% paid conversion after 60 days of traffic.
