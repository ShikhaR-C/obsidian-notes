## Quick Overview

- **What:** The Digital Rupee (e-₹) is India's sovereign digital currency — a tokenized digital form of the Indian Rupee issued by the RBI as legal tender.
- **Launched:** Wholesale pilot (e₹-W) on November 1, 2022; Retail pilot (e₹-R) on December 1, 2022.
- **Legal Basis:** Finance Act 2022 amended Section 22 of the RBI Act, 1934 to include banknotes in "digital form."
- **Current Status:** Pilot phase with ~5-6 million retail wallets, ~13-15 participating banks, across 26+ cities.
- **Key Differentiator from UPI:** e-₹ is digital *cash* (direct RBI liability with settlement finality), not a payment rail over bank accounts.
