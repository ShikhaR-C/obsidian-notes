## Scale Comparison: e-₹ vs UPI

| Metric | UPI | e-₹ (CBDC) | e-₹ as % of UPI |
|--------|-----|------------|-----------------|
| Monthly transactions | 14-16 billion | ~1-1.5 million | ~0.01% |
| Daily transactions | ~470-530 million | ~20,000-50,000 | ~0.006% |
| Users | 350+ million | ~5-6 million | ~1.5% |
| Merchants | 100+ million | ~400,000-500,000 | ~0.5% |

> At current volumes, e-₹ is commercially irrelevant compared to UPI. Its value lies in future potential, not current usage.

## Adoption Trajectory

### Initial Phase (Dec 2022 – Mid 2023)
- Wallet activations driven by bank promotion campaigns.
- Some banks incentivized employees and customers to open wallets.
- Daily transactions: **10,000-25,000/day**.

### Growth Phase (Mid 2023 – 2024)
- UPI QR code interoperability introduced — major catalyst.
- Daily transactions peaked at approximately **1 million/day** after UPI integration.
- However, sustaining those volumes proved challenging.
- Wallet activations grew to **~5 million** by October 2023.

### Plateau Phase (2024 – 2025)
- Daily transactions settled back to **20,000-50,000/day** range.
- Many wallets activated but **dormant** (ghost wallets).
- RBI acknowledged adoption challenges publicly.
- Focus shifted to feature development (programmability, offline) rather than pure adoption push.

---

## RBI's Official Stance

> *"We are examining the use cases and seeing how to make the e-rupee more attractive... We want to get the design right."*
> — RBI Governor Shaktikanta Das, 2024

- RBI has repeatedly stated it is **in no hurry** and views this as a "**learning by doing**" exercise.
- Deputy Governor T. Rabi Sankar emphasized that the pilot's purpose was to **test technology, not achieve mass adoption**.
- The RBI did not publicly commit to specific adoption targets.
- Officials indicated expanding use cases and interoperability are the priority.

## Notable Users & Use Cases

### Institutional
- Wholesale CBDC used for **government securities settlement** among participating banks.
- Some government departments experimented with **salary disbursement** via e-₹ (small-scale).

### Retail
- Primary usage in **metro cities** among tech-savvy early adopters.
- Some merchant clusters onboarded in market areas of pilot cities.
- University campuses and government offices trialed e-₹ acceptance.

---

## Key Insight: Why Active Usage Lags Wallet Creation

| Wallets Created | ~5-6 million |
|----------------|-------------|
| Estimated Active Users | <1 million |
| Active Usage Rate | ~15-20% |

**Reasons for dormancy:**
1. Bank-driven activation campaigns (open wallet to meet targets, never use it).
2. No recurring use case that UPI doesn't already serve.
3. Need to deliberately load wallet (extra step vs UPI which uses bank balance directly).
4. Limited merchant-initiated demand.
