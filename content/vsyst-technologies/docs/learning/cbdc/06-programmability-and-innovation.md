# 6. Programmability & Innovation Potential

## Programmable Money — CBDC's Killer Feature

Programmability is widely considered the **most significant differentiator** of CBDC over existing digital payment systems like UPI. While basic payments are already well-served by UPI, programmable money unlocks entirely new capabilities.

---

## Current State (as of 2025)

- The retail CBDC pilot focuses on **basic payment functionality** (P2P, P2M).
- Full smart-contract-style programmability is **not yet live in production**.
- However, the RBI has **actively discussed and is piloting** programmable features.

---

## Programmable Payment Use Cases

### 1. Government Subsidy Disbursement (Purpose-Bound Money)
- **Fertilizer subsidy** — e-₹ tokens programmed to be redeemable only at authorized fertilizer shops.
- **LPG subsidy** — tokens that can only be spent on cooking gas.
- **MGNREGA wages** — programmed for specific categories of spending.
- **Education vouchers** — e-₹ that can only be used for school fees, books, and educational supplies.
- **Healthcare disbursements** — tokens restricted to hospital/pharmacy spending.

> This is transformative for India's welfare system — ensures targeted delivery of benefits with **zero leakage**.

### 2. Conditional Payments
- **Escrow automation** — funds released only upon verified delivery of goods/services.
- **Rent payments** — auto-transfer on a set date if conditions are met.
- **Insurance payouts** — automatic disbursement triggered by verified events (e.g., crop insurance upon weather data confirmation).
- **Milestone-based project payments** — funds released upon completion verification.

### 3. Time-Bound Tokens
- e-₹ tokens can be programmed to **expire after a certain date**.
- Encourages spending and reduces hoarding.
- Useful for **stimulus payments** — government can ensure stimulus is spent quickly to boost economy.

### 4. Automatic Tax Deduction
- Tax deduction at point of sale built into the transaction protocol.
- Real-time GST collection on every transaction.

## IoT & Machine-to-Machine (M2M) Payments

As India's IoT ecosystem grows (smart cities, connected vehicles, industrial IoT), e-₹ enables:

- **Autonomous vehicle toll payments** — vehicle CBDC wallet auto-pays tolls
- **Smart meter utility payments** — electricity/water meters make automatic micropayments
- **EV charging** — electric vehicles auto-pay charging stations
- **Parking meters** — automated parking fee collection
- **Vending machines** — seamless contactless payments
- **Industrial M2M settlements** — machines in a factory transacting autonomously for supplies/maintenance

## Regulated DeFi on Sovereign Currency

Unlike decentralized crypto, e-₹ could offer **regulated, sovereign-backed programmable finance**:

| Feature | Crypto DeFi | e-₹ "Regulated DeFi" |
|---------|-------------|----------------------|
| **Backing** | Algorithmic / volatile | Sovereign guarantee |
| **Regulation** | Minimal / none | Full RBI oversight |
| **KYC/AML** | Optional | Built into protocol |
| **Stability** | Volatile | Stable (it's the rupee) |
| **Legal standing** | Grey area in India | Legal tender |

### Potential Applications
- **Tokenized government bonds** settled in e-₹
- **Programmable lending protocols** with regulatory oversight
- **Automated compliance** — KYC/AML built into transaction protocols
- **Composable financial products** — combining lending, insurance, and payments in programmable flows
- **Loyalty programs and vouchers** natively integrated

## Cross-Border CBDC

### Current Status: Domestic Only
- The Digital Rupee cannot currently be used for international transactions.

### Active International Initiatives

#### Project mBridge (BIS Innovation Hub)
- India participates alongside central banks of **China (PBoC), Hong Kong (HKMA), Thailand (BoT), UAE (CBUAE), and Saudi Arabia (SAMA)**.
- Uses a custom blockchain (mBridge Ledger) for **direct CBDC-to-CBDC transfers** between participating central banks.
- Bypasses the expensive correspondent banking system.
- Goal: Cross-border payments that are **near-instant** (vs 3-5 days), **much cheaper**, and **fully transparent**.
- Multiple successful pilot transactions completed as of 2024-2025.

#### Bilateral Agreements
- India exploring bilateral CBDC arrangements with **UAE** for rupee-dirham settlements.
- Aligns with India's push for **rupee internationalization**, particularly with Russia, UAE, and other bilateral trade partners.

#### BIS Interlinking Models Under Study
- Single shared multi-CBDC platform (mBridge model)
- Bilateral links between domestic CBDC systems
- Hub-and-spoke models

### Impact on Remittances
- India is the world's **largest recipient of remittances** (~$125 billion/year).
- Current remittance costs average **4-5%** of transfer value.
- Cross-border CBDC could reduce costs to **near-zero** and settlement time to **seconds**.

### Timeline
- Cross-border CBDC is a **medium-term goal (2026-2028)**.
- Domestic pilot needs to mature first.
