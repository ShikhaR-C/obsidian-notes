# 3. How to Use & Join — Digital Rupee (e-₹)

## Who Can Use It?

### Eligibility
- Any **Indian citizen or resident** with a bank account at a participating bank.
- Must have a **smartphone** (Android or iOS) to install the wallet app.
- During the pilot phase, access is limited to **select cities and select customers** — banks onboard customers in batches.

### KYC Requirements
- **Full KYC** is required — the wallet is linked to your bank account.
- Your existing bank KYC (Aadhaar, PAN, etc.) is leveraged — no separate KYC process needed.
- RBI's concept note mentions **tiered wallets** for the future:
  - **Basic wallet** — lower KYC, lower transaction limits
  - **Full wallet** — complete KYC, higher limits

### Age Restrictions
- Generally **18 years or older** (same as bank account eligibility).
- Minors with minor bank accounts (operated by guardians) were not part of the initial pilot.

### Geographic Availability
- Pilot started in 4 cities, expanded to **26+ cities** including Mumbai, New Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Ahmedabad, Kochi, Lucknow, Patna, and others.
- **Not yet available nationwide** — expanding gradually.

## Step-by-Step: How to Join

### Registration Process

1. **Check eligibility** — Confirm your bank participates in the e₹-R pilot and your city is covered.

2. **Download the wallet app** — Search your bank's name + "Digital Rupee" on Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Some banks integrate it into their main mobile banking app.
   - SBI: Digital Rupee wallet via SBI's app ecosystem
   - ICICI Bank: Digital Rupee wallet via ICICI's platforms
   - Yes Bank: Dedicated Digital Rupee wallet app
   - HDFC Bank: Digital Rupee wallet in their digital channels

3. **Register with your mobile number** — Must be the number linked to your bank account.

4. **Verify identity** — OTP-based verification using your registered mobile number.

5. **Link bank account** — Select/confirm the bank account for loading and unloading e-₹ tokens.

6. **Set wallet PIN/password** — Create a secure PIN for authorizing transactions.

7. **Load funds** — Transfer money from your bank account into the CBDC wallet. Digital tokens in specified denominations are created in your wallet.

8. **Start transacting!**

### Documents Needed
- No additional documents beyond your existing bank KYC.
- Effectively: **Aadhaar + PAN card** (already on file) + **registered mobile number**.

### Bank Account Requirement
- **Yes, mandatory.** You cannot hold a standalone CBDC wallet without a linked bank account in the current pilot design.
- Future versions may allow basic wallets with reduced KYC for financial inclusion.

## How Transactions Work

### Person-to-Person (P2P) Transfer
1. Open the CBDC wallet app.
2. Enter the recipient's **mobile number or wallet ID**.
3. Specify the amount.
4. Confirm with your wallet PIN.
5. Digital tokens transfer **instantly** from your wallet to theirs.

### Person-to-Merchant (P2M) Payment
1. Merchant displays a **QR code** (Digital Rupee QR or UPI QR with interoperability).
2. Open your CBDC wallet app.
3. **Scan the QR code**.
4. Confirm the payment amount.
5. Tokens transfer instantly to the merchant's wallet.

### Loading Your Wallet
1. Open wallet app → Select "Load Wallet."
2. Enter amount.
3. Funds are debited from your bank account.
4. Equivalent digital tokens are credited to your wallet.

### Unloading (Converting Back to Bank Balance)
1. Open wallet app → Select "Unload" or "Redeem."
2. Enter amount.
3. Digital tokens are destroyed; equivalent amount is credited to your bank account.

## Can Businesses Use It?

### Yes — Merchant Onboarding

1. Business contacts a participating bank to express interest.
2. Bank onboards the merchant (similar to UPI/POS merchant onboarding).
3. Merchant receives a **Digital Rupee QR code** for their outlet.
4. Customers scan the QR code and pay with their CBDC wallets.
5. Merchant receives digital tokens in their merchant CBDC wallet.
6. Tokens can be converted to bank balance at any time.

### B2B Transactions
- Direct B2B payments via e₹-R are technically possible if both parties have CBDC wallets.
- Not the primary focus of the retail pilot — retail CBDC targets P2P and P2M.

### Wholesale CBDC for Institutional Use
- The **e₹-W** is specifically designed for banks and financial institutions.
- Used for **settlement of government securities** in secondary markets.
- Reduces settlement risk and eliminates need for collateral in interbank G-Sec trading.

### Business Use Cases (Current & Future)
- **Retail acceptance** — grocery stores, restaurants, everyday commerce
- **Programmable payments** — government subsidies that can only be spent on designated goods
- **Supply chain payments** — potential for B2B supply chain settlements
- **Cross-border trade** — RBI exploring CBDC for international trade settlement

---

## Key Points to Remember

- e-₹ wallet is **separate** from your bank account — money must be explicitly loaded/unloaded.
- e-₹ does **not earn interest** — it is digital cash.
- Transactions are **real-time and final** — no pending settlements.
- **UPI interoperability** means you can pay at UPI QR code merchants using your e-₹ wallet.
