# 2. History & Timeline of India's CBDC

## Complete Timeline

| Date | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| **January 2017** | RBI's inter-departmental group first recommends introduction of a CBDC for India. |
| **January 2021** | Union Budget 2021-22: FM Nirmala Sitharaman announces a "Digital Currency" pilot by RBI. |
| **February 1, 2022** | **Union Budget 2022-23**: FM Sitharaman formally announces that RBI will introduce the Digital Rupee in FY 2022-23. Quote: *"Introduction of Central Bank Digital Currency will give a big boost to the digital economy."* |
| **March 30, 2022** | **Finance Act 2022 passed**: Amends Section 22 of the RBI Act, 1934 to allow RBI to issue banknotes in "digital form" — providing the legal foundation for CBDC. |
| **October 7, 2022** | **RBI publishes its Concept Note on CBDC** (~51 pages). The foundational policy document covering motivation, design choices, technology options, and phased rollout plan. Key decisions: two-tier distribution model, token-based retail CBDC, account-based wholesale CBDC. |
| **November 1, 2022** | **e₹-W (Wholesale) pilot launched.** Use case: settlement of secondary market transactions in government securities. Nine banks participate: SBI, Bank of Baroda, Union Bank of India, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank, Yes Bank, IDFC First Bank, HSBC. |
| **December 1, 2022** | **e₹-R (Retail) pilot launched.** Initial launch in 4 cities: Mumbai, New Delhi, Bengaluru, Bhubaneswar. Phase 1 banks: SBI, ICICI Bank, Yes Bank, IDFC First Bank. |
| **Early 2023** | Retail pilot expanded to additional cities: Ahmedabad, Gangtok, Guwahati, Hyderabad, Indore, Kochi, Lucknow, Patna, Shimla. Phase 2 banks added: Bank of Baroda, Union Bank of India, HDFC Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank. |
| **Mid 2023** | Feature additions: P2P (person-to-person) transfers enabled, P2M (person-to-merchant) via QR codes. |
| **August–October 2023** | **UPI QR code interoperability announced** — a major milestone allowing e₹-R wallets to transact at any UPI merchant QR code. |
| **October 2023** | RBI Deputy Governor T. Rabi Sankar reports ~4 million retail users and ~400,000 merchants onboarded. |
| **Late 2023–2024** | Programmability features explored. Offline functionality (NFC-based) under testing. Additional banks onboarded (Canara Bank, PNB, Federal Bank, IndusInd Bank, Axis Bank — total ~13-15 banks). |
| **2024** | RBI acknowledges adoption challenges. Daily transactions reach ~1 million at peak but sustaining volumes proves difficult. RBI Governor Shaktikanta Das indicates expanding use cases and interoperability are priorities. City coverage reaches 26+. |
| **2025 (up to May)** | Continued focus on scaling. RBI explores additional use cases: government subsidy disbursements (DBT), cross-border payment corridors, and programmable money for specific purposes. NFC-based offline trials continue. |

## Key Policy Decisions & Rationale

### Why the Two-Phase Approach?
1. **Wholesale first (Nov 2022)** — Lower risk, controlled environment with institutional participants, allowed RBI to test DLT infrastructure with sophisticated users.
2. **Retail second (Dec 2022)** — Higher complexity due to millions of potential users, diverse use cases, and UX requirements.

### Why Token-Based for Retail?
- Mirrors the nature of physical cash — each token is a bearer instrument.
- Enables potential offline transactions (tokens stored on device).
- Supports privacy for small-value transactions (unlike account-based which requires identity verification for every transaction).

### Why Non-Interest Bearing?
- Prevents e-₹ from competing with bank deposits.
- Avoids disintermediation risk (people draining bank savings to hold e-₹).
- Keeps e-₹ positioned as a *transactional currency*, not a *savings instrument*.

## Government Champions & Key Figures

- **FM Nirmala Sitharaman** — Announced CBDC in Union Budget 2022-23
- **RBI Governor Shaktikanta Das** — Oversaw pilot launch and ongoing development
- **RBI Deputy Governor T. Rabi Sankar** — Led much of the CBDC design and initial implementation before his term ended
- **RBI's Fintech Department** — Manages day-to-day CBDC operations
