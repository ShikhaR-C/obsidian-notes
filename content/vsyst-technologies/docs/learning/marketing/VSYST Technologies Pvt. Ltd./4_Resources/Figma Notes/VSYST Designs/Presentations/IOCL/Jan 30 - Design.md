### Social Welfare

#### Requirements / Needs

Driver Health Monitoring  
CA salary, PF, ESI  
Track users, driver  
Knowledge of current tax TDS TCS policies  
Burn-out & Tension & work paralysis & frustation

#### Challenges

Driver day health checkup, not centralised. Data not used for treatment  
CA salary not of minimum wages. No PF, ESI coverage / benefits  
Not warned with driver or user past work experiences / defaults / track record / license  
Taxation liability not known. Unnecessary penalties imposed  
Manual work & human error possibility leads to health problems

#### Solution

On each driver day store checkup details in DZZLO  
& suggest driver for proper checkup using Aayushman Bharat initiative with help of OMC, govt. support in top class hospitals  

Immediate action on emergency cases of driver or people accident  

DZZLO can provide HRA system to automatically dispatch salaries to CA in their bank accounts.  
Their work and records can be tracked to provide PF, ESI, Insurance perks.  

Same Driver or CA when tries to switch their jobs, company could demand to know their past experience and employee them accordingly. Leading to more secure and trustworthy hires. Can improve driver & CA profile  

Inbuilt taxation logic in DZZLO helps user to understand their liability, how to deduct / collect tax, when to cut tax, when to pay tax to govt.  

Involment of both parites in one platform leads to accuracy, efficinecy and transparency between transactions. Removing repetitive work, reconiliations. Improving overall productivity and healthly mind set for work
