# Sales Strategy — Motions, Playbooks, Team

**Purpose:** design the sales system — qualification framework, outbound playbooks, pilot-to-paid conversion, land-and-expand mechanics, hiring sequence, comp plans — calibrated for DZZLO's ACV band (₹500–₹10,000/mo per account at v1).

---

## Executive summary

1. **Primary motion: Inside sales + WhatsApp + light field presence.** Pure self-serve doesn't work for Indian SMB; pure enterprise field-sales is over-investment at your ACV.
2. **Qualification framework: SPICED by default; MEDDIC-lite for >₹1L ARR deals.**
3. **Outbound rhythm: 21-day WhatsApp + call + LinkedIn cadence.** 8–12 touches; 80% of closes need 5+ follow-ups.
4. **Pilots: 30-day paid** (not 90-day free). Paid at 10–20% of ACV, credited on conversion. Targets 40–60% pilot-to-paid.
5. **Hiring sequence: founder → SDR → field rep → CSM → AE → partner manager.** No VP Sales before ~₹20 Cr ARR.
6. **Land-and-expand is the N-R-R engine.** Plan "group plans" and module cross-sell from Day 1.

## 2. Funnel stages and qualification

**Stages:** Visitor → Lead → MQL → SQL → Opportunity (Discovery → Demo → Pilot → Proposal → Negotiation) → Closed Won/Lost → Onboarded → Expanded.

### Framework selection ([Claap](99_References.md#claap-meddic-spiced); [Qwilr MEDDPICC](99_References.md#qwilr-meddpicc); [Demodesk](99_References.md#demodesk-qualification))

- **BANT** (Budget/Authority/Need/Timeline) — fast SMB disqualify, weak on committees
- **MEDDIC** — Metrics/Economic buyer/Decision criteria/Decision process/Pain/Champion — forecasting rigor
- **MEDDPICC** — MEDDIC + Paper process + Competition — 6+ month cycles
- **SPICED** — Situation/Pain/Impact/Critical event/Decision — shorter SMB velocity ⭐ *DZZLO default*
- **GPCT / GPCTBA/C&I** — consultative selling

**DZZLO:** SPICED default; MEDDIC-lite for deals >₹1L ARR (fleets with 25+ trucks, multi-outlet dealer groups).

### SPICED discovery script (Hindi-first, 15 min call)

1. **S — Situation:** "Abhi aap kitne trucks chala rahe hain? Kitne pumps par credit account hai? Reconciliation कौन करता है?"
2. **P — Pain:** "Last month reconciliation में कितना समय लगा? कोई dispute हुआ? कितना पैसा फंसा?"
3. **I — Impact:** "अगर ये problem next 12 months जारी रही, आप का धंधा कितना affect होगा?"
4. **C — Critical event:** "GST audit? OMC renewal? New truck loan? कब तक fix करना ज़रूरी है?"
5. **D — Decision:** "कौन decide करता है? कब तक चाहिए? बजट कितना है?"

Structured qualification lifts win rates by 37% ([Claap](99_References.md#claap-meddic-spiced)).

## 3. Outbound playbook

### 3.1 Cold call — Indian SMB

8–12 attempts over 2–3 weeks, interleaved with email and LinkedIn; 80% of closes need 5+ follow-ups ([SalesHive](99_References.md#saleshive-cold-calling)).

**Opener (fleet owner):**

> "नमस्ते साहब, मैं [Name] बोल रहा हूँ DZZLO से। आप transport चलाते हैं ना? सिर्फ़ एक मिनट — हमारा काम है fleet का diesel expense 8–12% कम करना। क्या मैं WhatsApp पर एक short video भेज सकता हूँ?"

Quantified benefit + WhatsApp offer — Indian SMB prospects rarely book calendar slots on first call.

### 3.2 21-day WhatsApp + call + LinkedIn cadence

Full template in `08_Marketing_Strategy.md §2.1`. Key touches: Day 0, 2, 4, 6, 9, 13, 17, 21.

**Performance benchmarks:** WhatsApp 98% open vs email 20–25%; B2B SMB SaaS closure rates 28–32% higher with WhatsApp-integrated cadence; 65–70% rep-time savings ([Nutshell](99_References.md#nutshell-whatsapp-b2b); [Zoko](99_References.md#zoko-whatsapp-india)).

### 3.3 LinkedIn outbound

Mid-fleet (20+ trucks) and corporate (OMC, NBFC, tyre brand) partners only. Connect request <100 chars, no pitch; value message 48 hours after acceptance.

### 3.4 Trade-association + dealer-network outreach

The unlock for SMB segment. Targets:

- **AIMTC** — 95 lakh truckers + 50 lakh bus/tourist; 3,500+ Taluka/District/State federations ([AIMTC](99_References.md#aimtc-site)). Negotiate member-benefit listing + bulk-discount code.
- **State lorry associations** — Andhra, Bombay Goods Transport, Delhi Goods Transport Organisation. Sponsor monthly meetings; get the president as reference customer.
- **FAIPT / AIPDA / CIPD** — three national dealer bodies representing ~54,000 dealers ([Petrol World CIPD](99_References.md#petrol-world-cipd)). State chapter presidents → early pilots.
- **Transport nagars** — SGTN Delhi, Kurla Mumbai, Mehsana, Raipur, Nagpur. Dense clusters — ideal for field pods.

### 3.5 Channel/distributor-led pilots

Run a "channel-first" pilot where an OMC zonal office, lubricant distributor (Castrol, HP Lubricants), or tyre dealer (MRF, Apollo) introduces DZZLO to 10–20 of their customers with a joint offer.

**Conversion benchmark:** warm channel intros run 3–5× cold outbound in Indian SMB.

## 5. Demo, pilot, and POC conversion

Enterprise free trials convert <10%; structured paid pilots hit **40–60%** ([Heavybit](99_References.md#heavybit-saas-poc); [Dock](99_References.md#dock-sales-poc); [Dipam Shah pilots](99_References.md#dipam-shah-pilots)).

**Pilot structure that works:**

- **Duration: 30 days**, not 90. Urgency matters.
- **Paid: 10–30% of ACV credited to full contract on conversion.** "Paid" signals commitment.
- **Success criteria defined *with customer in writing*:** "We'll measure fuel cost/km before and after. If drops ≥5%, you sign annual."
- **Executive sponsor** committed from buyer side; named champion inside.
- **30/60/90 rhythm:** Day 0 kickoff, Day 15 check-in, Day 28 QBR + signature.
- Reframe language: "co-pilot plan" not "trial."

**Security & compliance one-pager** (prepare early): data residency (India AWS/Azure), DPDP Act 2023 alignment, RBAC, API encryption, ISO 27001 path.

**Champion enablement:** give the internal champion a pre-built family/board deck — they sell internally, you arm them.

## 6. Land-and-expand

Best SaaS land-and-expand: **200–400% account growth over 3–5 years** ([Rework](99_References.md#rework-land-expand); [Journeyz](99_References.md#journeyz-land-expand)). 44% of SaaS revenue growth is expansion.

**DZZLO expansion playbook:**

1. **Land narrow:** one outlet / one depot. Low friction, fast decision, cheap to try.
2. **Prove with data:** 30-day outcome (litres accounted, fraud caught, disputes resolved).
3. **Map the account:** other outlets/depots? Who signs for each? Who's the group owner?
4. **Group rollout:** founder/senior AE pitches group owner with field data. Price per outlet drops 15–25% on group commits.
5. **Module cross-sell:** fleet card → payments → credit → insurance (see product roadmap in `12_Roadmap_Phases.md`).

**KPI: Account Penetration %** (outlets live / outlets possible in account) — leading indicator of NRR. Target >70% within 12 months of landing.

## 7. Sales team structure, ramp, comp

### 7.1 Hiring sequence

([Blume dhandha](99_References.md#blume-enterprise-saas); [Rajesh Nair India](99_References.md#rajesh-nair-india-b2b); [SaaStr comp](99_References.md#saastr-sales-comp))

| When | Role | Primary responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Month 0 | Founder-AE | Close the first 30 customers personally |
| Month 1 | Field Rep #1 (Raipur) | Walk transport nagars, onboard dealers |
| Month 3 | SDR #1 | Outbound list-building, WhatsApp cadence |
| Month 5 | Customer Success Manager #1 | Onboarding, retention, expansion |
| Month 6 | Field Rep #2 (Nagpur) | Phase 1 west extension |
| Month 8 | Inside AE #1 | Mid-fleet deals from inbound |
| Month 12 | Partnerships Manager | OMC + tyre + NBFC channel |
| Month 15 | Inside AE #2 + Field Rep #3 (Kolkata / Indore) | Phase 2 corridor coverage |
| Month 24 | VP Sales (first) | Scale team 5→25 |

**Blume guidance:** don't hire VP Sales before ~₹20 Cr ARR; strong individual AEs beat a leader below that.

**SDR:AE ratio:** start 1:1, move to 2:1 as reps mature.

### 7.2 Ramp curves

US benchmark 4 months to productivity; India 5–6 months (longer relationship cycles + customization requests 2× more frequent — [Rajesh Nair](99_References.md#rajesh-nair-india-b2b)). Quota: 50% Q1, 75% Q2, 100% Q3.

### 7.3 Commission structure

([Everstage comp](99_References.md#everstage-saas-comp); [David Sacks](99_References.md#david-sacks-sales-team))

| Role | Base | Variable | OTE | Commission rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SDR | ₹4–6L | ₹2–3L (meetings + SQLs) | ₹6–9L | N/A |
| Inside AE | ₹8–12L | ₹6–10L | ₹14–22L | 8–12% of Y1 ACV + 50% of expansion in Y1 |
| Field AE | ₹12–18L | ₹8–14L + territory override | ₹20–32L | Same + territory bonus |
| CSM | ₹6–10L | ₹2–4L (retention-linked) | ₹8–14L | 2% of NRR upsell |

- **Accelerators:** 1.5× above 100% quota; 2× above 120%. Cap only at 200% to avoid windfall.
- **Clawback:** full if customer churns in first 90 days; half if 90–180 days.
- **Overrides:** 1–2% to channel manager on partner-sourced; 0.5% to VP Sales on all team deals (at scale).
- **Pay cadence:** monthly, not quarterly. Indian AEs value cash-flow predictability.

**Keep OTE ratios global-standard** so you can hire ex-Freshworks / Razorpay / Zoho talent.

- **CRM:** HubSpot Free → Starter, or Zoho CRM Free. Don't over-buy.

## 9. Discount and negotiation playbook (Indian SMB)

Indian SMB buyers default to asking for discounts ([Rajesh Nair](99_References.md#rajesh-nair-india-b2b); [Flock](99_References.md#flock-selling-saas-india)).

- Never discount without a concession in return (longer term, bigger commitment, case-study rights, logo use).
- Typical ranges: 10–25% for annual prepay + multi-outlet; 30%+ only for lighthouse/referenceable customers.
- Prefer value swaps (extra training, more users, priority support) over price cuts.
- Build legitimate urgency: implementation-slot filling, GST/DPDP deadlines, year-end book-closing.
- Offer written "price ladder" — Plan A list / Plan B with 2-year commit / Plan C with case study + reference rights.
- Close on a specific date: "by Friday EOD" > "this month."
- Don't push back on customization asks; triage: config toggle → promise; one-off → price separately.
