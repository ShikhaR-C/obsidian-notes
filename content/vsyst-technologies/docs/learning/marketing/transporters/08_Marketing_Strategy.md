# Marketing Strategy — Channels, Campaigns, Content Engine

**Purpose:** translate positioning into a channel plan and content engine that fits a bootstrapped, corridor-beachhead company with ₹30–50k/month marketing spend, scaling through Seed and Series A.

---

## Executive summary

1. **Two channels, not seven.** Until Seed, run (a) WhatsApp-first outbound + (b) programmatic SEO with Hindi long-tail. All else is distraction.
2. **Field-sponsorship of trade associations** is your unpaid third channel — compounds trust at near-zero ₹.
3. **Content engine = Hindi YouTube shorts + comparison pages + ROI calculator.** Three formats; do them weekly forever.
4. **WhatsApp Business API from Day 1** — it is the channel where Bharat SMB converts. Email is for investors, not customers.
5. **Partnership layer (OMC, tyre, lubricant, NBFC) is post-Seed** — unlocks pan-India reach without CAC.
6. **Measurement: one weekly dashboard** the founder personally reads. Cheap cadence. No BI tool needed before Series A.

## 2. Channel playbooks

### 2.1 WhatsApp Business API — the primary channel

Adoption data: 84% of Indian SMBs say WhatsApp helps them communicate; 80% say it drives growth ([Indian Retailer WA](99_References.md#indian-retailer-whatsapp); [Zoko](99_References.md#zoko-whatsapp-india); [Nutshell WA B2B](99_References.md#nutshell-whatsapp-b2b)).

**Stack:**
- BSP (Business Solution Provider): pick one of Wati, AiSensy, Gupshup, Interakt. Cost: ₹2,500–₹6,000/mo + session charges.
- **Template messages** pre-approved by Meta for marketing + OTP + transactional
- Chatbot flows for: onboarding, fleet registration, slip-photo upload, ledger snapshot

**Outbound cadence (21-day sequence after first WhatsApp send):**

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 0 | Cold call → voicemail OK → WhatsApp text intro + 45s Hindi video |
| 2 | Follow-up with 1-page ROI image (trucks × ₹/truck × months) |
| 4 | LinkedIn connect to owner + accountant |
| 6 | Call back, reference local customer ("Raipur के X साहब DZZLO use करते हैं") |
| 9 | WhatsApp case study voice note (30s, founder Hindi) |
| 13 | Formal proposal email + brochure |
| 17 | WhatsApp "breakup": "Sir, क्या मैं file close कर दूँ?" |
| 21 | Move to 90-day nurture list |

**Benchmark (Indian B2B SMB SaaS):** 28–32% higher close rate with WhatsApp vs email-only; 65–70% rep-time savings ([Nutshell](99_References.md#nutshell-whatsapp-b2b)).

### 2.2 Google Ads — Hindi long-tail first

**Not:** "fleet management software" (₹200+/click, unwinnable vs global).
**Yes:** long-tail Hindi + problem-phrasing.

High-intent low-CPC keywords to bid on:
- "पेट्रोल पंप पर्ची ऐप"
- "ट्रांसपोर्टर हिसाब ऐप"
- "diesel credit book app"
- "[competitor] alternative India"
- "petrol pump software [city]"
- "fuel tracking small fleet"

**Budget allocation Phase 0:**
- Search Ads: ₹8,000–12,000/mo, target ~300 clicks at ₹30–40 CPC
- Performance Max: skip until Phase 2 — wastes small budgets

**Landing pages** (see `07_Positioning_and_Messaging.md`): Hindi H1, WhatsApp click-to-chat as primary CTA.

### 2.3 Programmatic SEO

The long-term moat. [TripleDart](99_References.md#tripledart-programmatic-seo) and [Averi](99_References.md#averi-programmatic-seo) playbooks.

**Page templates to ship (50 pages Phase 0, 500 by Phase 2):**

| Template | Variable | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `[Competitor] alternative` | Petrosoft, Vyapar, Tally, Blackbuck, FleetX, LocoNav | "Vyapar alternative for transporters" |
| `[City] petrol pump software` | 50 cities | "Raipur petrol pump software" |
| `[Fleet size] fleet management` | 5/10/25/50/100/200 | "Software for 10-truck fleets in India" |
| `[OMC] [module] integration` | IOCL, BPCL, HPCL × Xtrapower, SmartFleet, DriveTrack | "IOCL XTRAPOWER integration for dealers" |
| `[Pain] for [persona]` | Reconciliation, Fraud, GST × Dealer, Transporter | "Fuel reconciliation for transporters" |
| ROI calculators | Fleet size, diesel/month | "Fuel fraud calculator" |

**Stack:** WebFlow or Framer + Google Sheets + Airtable (₹0–3,000/mo). Content in Hinglish + Hindi; canonical tag for duplicate variants.

### 2.4 YouTube — Hindi-first, phone-shot, weekly

Channel thesis: every transporter and pump owner watches Hindi YouTube shorts. Produce:

- **2 shorts/month Phase 0** (budget ₹2–5k/video)
- **8/month Phase 1**
- Formats: customer testimonials, "X's पंप day-in-the-life with DZZLO", "one thing you're losing if you don't use DZZLO"
- Length: 45–90s, vertical (9:16), Hindi voiceover, subtitles in Hindi + English

**Distribution:**
- YouTube Shorts (primary)
- Cross-post to Instagram Reels, Facebook, WhatsApp Status, LinkedIn (for metro reach)
- Embed in landing pages

### 2.5 Field — transport nagar walkabouts

Unpaid CAC engine. Weekly cadence:

- **Mondays:** field rep at one transport nagar, 8 am – 2 pm (dispatch window). Walk the rows, hand out single-page Hindi flyer + WhatsApp QR code. 30–50 conversations/day.
- **Wednesdays:** same rep visits pump association meetings.
- **Saturdays:** night-stop at a highway dhaba cluster (Nagpur or Raipur bypass) to meet long-haul drivers — "drivers become DZZLO evangelists" dynamic.

**Budget:** diesel + meals + printed collateral = ₹8,000–15,000/mo Phase 0. Scales with reps.

### 2.6 Association sponsorships

High-ROI trust build. Target:

- **Dealer side:** CGPDA (anchor), FAIPT, AIPDA, CIPD, state-level PDAs on corridor ([CIPD](99_References.md#petrol-world-cipd))
- **Transporter side:** AIMTC (3,500+ federations, 95 lakh truckers) ([AIMTC](99_References.md#aimtc-site)), state lorry associations, Bombay Goods Transport, Delhi Goods Transport Organisation

**Activities per association (₹3,000–8,000/mo):**
- Sponsor printed directory / member yearbook
- Host a "Digital Parchi" workshop at AGM
- Badge on DZZLO landing page ("Endorsed by CGPDA")
- Co-branded member-benefit listing ("DZZLO free for association members")

### 2.7 LinkedIn — mid-fleet + partner layer (Phase 1+)

For 50+ truck fleet managers, OMC partnership managers, NBFC truck-loan heads. Not for owner-drivers.

- Founder personal profile: post weekly; industry insights; customer wins; fund-raise narratives
- Company page: case studies, hiring posts
- Outbound: ≤100 connect requests/week; no-pitch message pattern

### 2.8 Partnerships (Phase 2+)

Channel partner economics typically 15–30% first-year ACV for referrals, 20–40% for reseller/VAR ([Rework channel](99_References.md#rework-channel); [Bessemer channel partnerships](99_References.md#bessemer-channel-partnerships)). Key targets:

- **OMCs:** IOCL, BPCL, HPCL, Reliance-BP, Shell, Nayara — co-marketing via zonal offices
- **Fleet-card issuers:** SBI BPCL, HPCL DriveTrack Plus, ICICI HPCL ([SBI BPCL](99_References.md#sbicard-bpcl))
- **Tyre brands:** MRF, Apollo, CEAT, JK Tyre — embed DZZLO in their trucker apps
- **Lubricant brands:** Castrol, Shell Rimula, HP Lubricants
- **Insurance:** ICICI Lombard, HDFC ERGO, Bajaj Allianz CV lines
- **NBFC truck loans:** Shriram, Cholamandalam, Mahindra, SK Finance

## 3. Content engine

### 3.1 Pillar content (Phase 0 — 12 total pieces)

- "India के ट्रांसपोर्टर के 5 सबसे बड़े headache" (SEO pillar page, 3000 words Hindi)
- "पेट्रोल पंप reconciliation का complete guide 2026"
- "Fleet card vs digital parchi — कौन बेहतर?"
- "GST input credit for transporters — step-by-step"
- 8 city-wise "Best fuel software in [City]"
- 1 calculator: Fuel Fraud & Shrinkage Calculator

### 3.2 Weekly content (Phase 0–1)

- 2 YouTube shorts/week
- 1 LinkedIn founder post (Phase 1+)
- 1 customer case study / story (can reuse for testimonial reel)
- 1 WhatsApp broadcast newsletter to leads + customers (tips + product updates)

### 3.3 Distribution discipline

For every hour spent creating content, spend an hour distributing. Checklist for each piece:

- [ ] Posted on native platform (YouTube, LinkedIn)
- [ ] Cross-posted to 3+ platforms with platform-native format
- [ ] Sent as WhatsApp broadcast to lead list
- [ ] Shared in 5+ relevant community groups (AIMTC, state association WhatsApp groups)
- [ ] Added to landing page or email sequence

## 4. Campaigns — the three that matter

### 4.1 "Free for your pump" campaign (Phase 0)

**Objective:** transporter → pump recruitment.
**Mechanic:** transporter on platform invites his home pump; pump gets DZZLO OMS free for 3 months + ₹1,000 first-month UPI credit.
**Channels:** WhatsApp, in-app referral, field-rep follow-up at the referred pump.
**Target:** 2 referrals per transporter; 30% pump-onboard rate.

### 4.2 "Save ₹50,000" calculator campaign (Phase 0–1)

**Objective:** lead generation via BOFU intent tool.
**Mechanic:** public calculator; user inputs fleet size, avg diesel/mo; outputs "DZZLO would save you ₹X/year" + WhatsApp CTA.
**Channels:** SEO pillar page, Google Ads, YouTube description.
**Target:** 200 completions/mo → 40 WhatsApp replies → 8 meetings → 2 paid.

### 4.3 "अपना पर्ची बुक, अपनी भाषा" (Phase 1)

**Objective:** regional-language expansion trigger at each new corridor state.
**Mechanic:** 60-second Hindi reel dropped weekly featuring local customers in the new state; tagged with city/state hashtags.
**Channels:** YouTube, Instagram, WhatsApp Status.
**Target:** 50k views per reel; 200 inbound WhatsApp replies.

## 7. What not to do (yet)

- **No CTV / TV ads** — wait for brand scale.
- **No programmatic display at scale** — untargeted spray.
- **No event booths before Phase 2** — trade events burn ₹5–15L for <5 leads at early stage.
- **No influencer marketing** — no credible Hindi-speaking trucker-influencer cohort at scale yet (watch this; will change 2027–28).
- **No cold email at volume** — SMB transporters ignore email.
- **No podcast sponsorships** — India B2B podcast listening is still metro-elite; your ICP isn't there.
