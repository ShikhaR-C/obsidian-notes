# Funding & Budget Plan — Bootstrap → Seed → Series A → Series B

**Purpose:** plot how VSYST Technologies goes from ₹10–20k/mo marketing spend (current bootstrap) through Seed, Series A, and Series B — with explicit triggers for each raise, target investors, and marketing budget at each stage.

---

## Executive summary

1. **Stay bootstrapped for 4–6 more months.** Don't raise until you have 25+ paid dealers and 85%+ retention proof. Raising before PMF burns equity at a low valuation and doesn't unlock growth.
2. **Chase the ₹20L Startup India Seed Fund grant** you already applied for. Grants are non-dilutive fuel.
3. **Seed: ₹2–4 Cr at Month 6–8.** Use: Phase 1 hires + 2 field pods + product (Digital-Lite mode). Target investors: Blume, 100X.VC, angels from fuel/logistics.
4. **Series A: ₹15–30 Cr at Month 14–18.** Use: Phase 2 corridor coverage + DZZLO Card launch. Target: Peak XV, Elevation, Lightspeed India, Matrix.
5. **Series B: ₹60–100 Cr at Month 24–30.** Use: Pan-India (NH-44 + adjacent corridors) + fintech scale. Target: Tiger, SoftBank, WestBridge, Chiratae.
6. **Marketing spend scales with revenue, not wish.** ₹30–50k/mo bootstrap → ₹1–2L/mo Seed → ₹5–8L/mo Series A → ₹15–25L/mo Series B.

## 3. Raise triggers — the investor-facing checklist

### 3.1 Triggers to raise Seed

**Do not raise until all five are true:**

- ✅ **50+ paying dealers** (or 25+ dealers + 500+ paying transporters in combined ARR equivalent)
- ✅ **>85% logo retention** over trailing 6 months
- ✅ **CAC under ₹5,000/paying account** (paid channel only; field CAC separate)
- ✅ **At least 1 OMC MoU or verbal partnership** (IOCL most likely)
- ✅ **2+ concrete customer case studies** with real numbers ("Dealer X saved ₹45,000/month"; "Fleet Y reduced reconciliation 6 hrs → 45 min")

### 3.2 Triggers to raise Series A

- ✅ **₹3 Cr ARR** (or ₹25L MRR run-rate)
- ✅ **110%+ NRR** on seed cohort
- ✅ **CAC payback <14 months**
- ✅ **Fintech attach >15%** of customers (DZZLO Card or Pay live)
- ✅ **3+ state coverage** (CG, MP, MH, OD or similar)
- ✅ **Repeatable acquisition channel** with CPL stable across 3+ months

### 3.3 Triggers to raise Series B

- ✅ **₹15 Cr+ ARR**
- ✅ **Rule of 40 >20**
- ✅ **Fintech revenue >30% of total** (proving the Toast / Shopify playbook works in your vertical)
- ✅ **3+ corridors / 10+ states**
- ✅ **NRR >120%**
- ✅ **Clear path to ₹50 Cr ARR in next 12 months**

## 9. When NOT to raise

Don't raise if:
- PMF not proven (churn >20%/yr)
- CAC payback >18 months
- No repeatable acquisition channel
- Founder energy low / co-founder friction unresolved
- Competitive landscape shifts you haven't analysed
- Market reality diverges materially from pitch deck

A bad raise is worse than no raise. An over-priced seed cripples Series A.
