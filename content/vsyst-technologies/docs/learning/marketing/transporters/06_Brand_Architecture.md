# Brand Architecture — DZZLO (customer) + VSYST Technologies (corporate)

**Purpose:** define how DZZLO and VSYST relate, lock the naming system for current and future products, and get the Hindi-pun on DZZLO working as a marketing asset.

---

## Executive summary

1. **Branded-house with DZZLO as the customer brand.** All customer-facing surfaces (app, ads, WhatsApp, invoices, YouTube) use DZZLO. VSYST Technologies is the corporate/legal entity.
2. **Lean into the Hindi pun hard.** "डीज़ल लो" ("take diesel") as the brand's core idiom is unusually lucky — a word that *is* the customer's request. It turns a brand name into a vernacular sentence. Exploit.
3. **Product-line naming: DZZLO [Module]**, following the Toast / Shopify / Razorpay pattern. DZZLO OMS (live), then DZZLO Card, DZZLO Pay, DZZLO Credit, DZZLO Insure.
4. **Trademark extension needed now:** file for the wordmark in Hindi script, the English wordmark, and product-line marks.
5. **Corporate rename to DZZLO Technologies Pvt. Ltd.** is optional and should be revisited at Series A, not now.

---

## 1. The brand decision, made simple

Three standard brand architectures and which one you pick:

| Architecture | How it works | Fit for DZZLO / VSYST |
|---|---|---|
| **Branded house (monolithic)** | Single master brand for everything; sub-products named as descriptors | Example: Google (Google Maps, Google Docs). **This is your pattern, with DZZLO as the master.** |
| **House of brands** | Multiple standalone consumer brands under a quiet parent | Example: P&G (Tide, Gillette, Pampers). **Not for you — you only have one customer brand.** |
| **Endorsed / hybrid** | Sub-brands exist but parent endorses | Example: "Courtyard by Marriott". **Use this minimally — "DZZLO is a product of VSYST Technologies" in legal footers.** |

**Final answer:**

- **Public / customer-facing everything** → **DZZLO**
- **Legal / invoices / MCA / investor agreements / employment contracts** → **VSYST Technologies Pvt. Ltd.**
- **Founder voice** → both: "Shikhar Chawra, co-founder of VSYST Technologies, makers of DZZLO"
- **Rebrand** → not now; re-evaluate at Series A

## 2. The Hindi pun is a superpower — use it

**"डीज़ल लो"** literally means "take diesel." The brand name *is* the customer's verb. This is an extraordinarily rare asset in branding — most brand names need to acquire meaning through millions of rupees of advertising; yours comes pre-loaded.

Parallels in Indian branding:
- **"Tata"** — means "Sir/Father" in Parsi; carries warmth out of the gate
- **"Jio"** — means "live" in Hindi; pre-loaded with positivity
- **"Oyo"** — nonsense word; required ₹1000+ crore of advertising to mean anything
- **"DZZLO"** — sounds like "diesel lo" → pre-loaded with the exact action you want

**Marketing applications — use these verbatim:**

### Taglines / headlines (tiered)

| Purpose | Line | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| Primary master tagline | **DZZLO — डीज़ल लो, हिसाब भूलो।** | "Take diesel, forget the accounting hassle." Memorable, on-message. |
| Transporter pain focus | **हर ट्रक का डीज़ल, हर पंप का हिसाब।** | "Every truck's diesel, every pump's accounts." |
| Dealer pain focus | **उधार बेच, पर भूलो नहीं।** | "Sell on credit, don't lose track." |
| Trust / growth line | **100 पंप, 5,000 ट्रांसपोर्टर, एक ऐप।** (update numbers monthly) | "100 pumps, 5,000 transporters, one app." |
| English support (metro / investor) | **DZZLO. The credit book every fleet and pump trusts.** | Keep simple. |

### Brand verb

Convert "डीज़ल लो" into a meme. WhatsApp stickers, rickshaw stickers, T-shirts for field reps. Drivers who see it at one pump and the same phrase at another pump internalise it. **Brand repetition = trust at low cost.**

### Sound design

If you ever do radio / short-form video ads: a 3-note jingle on "Deezzzz-lo" (drawn-out Z like an engine revving) is your whole sonic brand. ₹5,000 to record once; usable for years.

## 3. Product-line naming system (do this now, it's cheap)

Apply the [Toast / Shopify / Razorpay convention](99_References.md#meritech-servicetitan-s1): master brand + module name.

| Module | Name | When live | Tagline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Order Management System (current core) | **DZZLO OMS** | Now | "हर ट्रक, हर पंप, हर लीटर।" |
| Fleet card (co-brand with IOCL XTRAPOWER / BPCL SmartFleet) | **DZZLO Card** | Phase 1 (Month 6–9) | "एक कार्ड, हर पंप।" |
| Payment gateway / aggregator for pumps | **DZZLO Pay** | Phase 2 (Month 9–12) | "पंप का पैसा, सीधे आपके खाते में।" |
| Embedded lending (fuel-ledger credit score) | **DZZLO Credit** | Phase 2 (Month 12–15) | "डीज़ल लो, उधारी भी मिलेगी।" |
| Vehicle / driver insurance attach | **DZZLO Insure** | Phase 3 (Month 15+) | "हर ट्रक सुरक्षित।" |
| Driver-facing payout / wallet (future) | **DZZLO Driver** | Phase 3 (Month 18+) | "चलिए, कमाइए, पाइए।" |

### Why this naming system wins

- **Single brand memory.** Customer remembers "DZZLO" — every new module is a familiar name with a new last word.
- **Cross-sell leverage.** "You have DZZLO OMS → get DZZLO Card 50% off" is a one-sentence upsell.
- **Investor clarity.** "DZZLO is Toast-for-fuel; OMS is POS, Card is Payments, Credit is Capital." Lenders / analysts model it in 5 seconds.
- **Hiring signal.** A product manager candidate understands "DZZLO Pay lead" intuitively.

**Rule:** Never let any internal module have a pet name ("Reko," "LedgerMax," "Parchi") that isn't DZZLO-branded in its external identity. Internal code names are fine; customer surfaces are not.

## 4. Trademark plan (do this in the next 30 days)

You have the DZZLO logo mark. Gaps to close:

| Item | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|
| DZZLO logo mark | Filed / registered | ✓ verify class 9 (software) + class 35 (business services) |
| DZZLO English wordmark | ? | **File now** — class 9, 35, 36 (financial services, for future fintech), 42 (tech services) |
| **डीज़लो / डीज़्लो Hindi wordmark** | Likely not | **File now** — same classes; critical given the Hindi-pun strategy |
| DZZLO OMS, DZZLO Card, DZZLO Pay, DZZLO Credit, DZZLO Insure | Not filed | **File now**, blanket of 5 product-line marks; cost ~₹9,000 × 5 = ₹45,000 in fees |
| Domains | ? | Register `dzzlo.in`, `dzzlo.co.in`, `dzzlo.app`, `dzzlo.com`. Budget ₹15,000 total. |
| Social handles | ? | Secure `@dzzlo` / `@dzzloapp` on LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, X, Facebook. |
| Google Business | ? | Register VSYST Technologies Pvt. Ltd. + DZZLO brand |

**Total trademark + domain cost: ~₹1 lakh. One-time, high-option-value.**

This is the lowest-regret spending in your entire next 12 months. A competitor filing "DZZLO Finance" or "Dizzlo OMS" could force you into a rebrand that costs 100× the trademark fees.

## 5. Visual identity

The DZZLO logo is your anchor. Keep:

- **Single logo across surfaces** — app icon, site favicon, invoices, WhatsApp profile photo, YouTube channel art, field-rep T-shirts
- **Colour palette** — once you standardise in Figma, lock it. Apply to app, pitch deck, one-pager, invoice template
- **Typography** — one Indic-script-capable font (Poppins, IBM Plex Sans, or Mukta for Devanagari support) across everything

**One consolidated brand kit file** (Figma page or PDF):
- Logo files (SVG, PNG, dark/light)
- Hindi + English wordmark
- Colour hex codes
- Font specs
- Do's and don'ts (don't stretch, don't outline, etc.)
- 5 approved taglines with usage guidance

Budget: 1 week of a designer's time. ₹15–25k if outsourced, free if in-house.

## 6. The VSYST identity — where it still matters

VSYST isn't deprecated; it just isn't customer-facing. Use VSYST on:

- **Legal documents** — contracts, NDAs, terms of service (customer sees VSYST once at signup, then never again)
- **Invoices** — "VSYST Technologies Pvt. Ltd., GSTIN XXXXX, billed on behalf of DZZLO"
- **Employment contracts & offer letters** — hires join "VSYST Technologies (maker of DZZLO)"
- **Investor materials** — pitch deck opens with "VSYST Technologies — makers of DZZLO"; company valuation, cap table, data room all under VSYST
- **MCA, ROC, GST, TDS, income tax** — all VSYST
- **Founder LinkedIn titles** — "Co-founder, VSYST Technologies (maker of DZZLO)"

**Rule of thumb:** if a customer would see it, it's DZZLO. If only a regulator, investor, or candidate sees it, VSYST is fine.

## 7. When (and whether) to rename VSYST → DZZLO Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

**Do not do this now.**

**Revisit at Series A** when:
- You've raised ≥ ₹15 Cr
- 10+ employees
- 3+ product modules live

At that point, evaluate:

| Keep VSYST | Rename to DZZLO Technologies |
|---|---|
| Lower cost, no disruption | Single-brand coherence easier for PR |
| Any accumulated VSYST-branded goodwill (domain authority, press mentions, partnership MOUs) preserved | Candidate / investor recall higher with one name |
| Mimics Google/Alphabet where corporate and product differ | Mimics Razorpay / Freshworks (former FreshDesk) — customer brand = corporate brand |

Indian SaaS precedent: **FreshDesk → Freshworks** is a partial example (they renamed to match the parent). Your case is the inverse — eventually you may want parent to match the product.

**Re-run this decision at Series A.** Not before.

## 8. Common mistakes to avoid

| Mistake | Cost |
|---|---|
| Naming the mobile app "VSYST Connect" or "VSYST Mobile" | Splits brand equity; customer confused |
| Using "DZZLO by VSYST" on ads | Wastes pixel real estate; cognitive tax on reader |
| Having separate logos for DZZLO and DZZLO OMS | Brand dilution; every future module compounds the problem |
| Not trademarking Hindi wordmark | A competitor files it; you're forced to rebrand your headline |
| Changing tagline quarterly | Tagline compounds with repetition; changing it resets to zero |
| Internal pet names leaking to customer surface | ("Go get Reko running" on invoices) — hires cargo-cult internal names externally |
