## 2. What was checked and held

- **The cache key** is the body minus its cursor, so page 2 merges into page 1's entry and a new tab, range or chip set is a new entry; **the summary stays page 1's** (P‑11, A‑8) — both read in `daily_summary.js:50–76`.
- **The v3 tag sharing** with v1 (`order_msts_POST / LIST`) is what makes finding 1 reachable; the sharing itself is by decision D9.
- **The P‑6 prune on a refresh** is right for a page-1 fulfilment; finding 4 is only the load-more case past the cap.
- **`serializableCheck` is off** in the store, so a `Date` in query args is not a defect (the agent's own dropped candidate).
- **Suite:** 100 / 2,281 green at head; the three lint errors are the base's.

## 3. Where to fix

All three paging defects and the prune cap live in `daily_summary.js` and `useScreenModel.js`, both since reworked by #53 (`cursorFor`) and #54 (`useRefetchOnRefocus`, `useScrollToTopOnNewList`), so the fix belongs on the **top of the stack**, not here. Findings 1 and 2 share one design move — subscribe on page-1 args, fetch further pages unsubscribed — which also removes the `cursorFor` dance #53's review calls an altitude problem. The driver line is a one-liner anywhere.
